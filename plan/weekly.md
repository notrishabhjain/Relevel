# Applied AI PM: Weekly Working Plan (7 Oct 2026 – 6 Apr 2027)

This plan has one row for each week. Each row gives you one topic, a place to learn it, a sense of how hard the week is, and one thing to ship. If that one thing gets shipped, the week was a success, however the rest of it went.

This tab is also your tracker. Tick each week below as you go, and your progress is saved in this browser (and to your account, if you sign in). The reasons behind the plan, and the project briefs, are on the [Curriculum tab](#curriculum). The steps, code and links for building each deliverable are on the [How to build tab](#build). All three tabs read from the same data, so a week's topic, links and deliverable are the same everywhere.

**Start gently.** The weekly rows below assume 8–10 hours a week, and that is a steep first step. So the plan begins with the [gentle start](../#/start) instead: four weeks of ten minutes a weekday, reading and small tries only, in the same order as the daily 12:00 reminder. When the gentle start is done, pick up the weekly rows from Week 1, four weeks later than the dates show. A late start that keeps going beats an on-time start that stops.

**North star:** by 6 April 2027, you have four published AI artifacts with real evaluation numbers, and a portfolio you can take into an interview.

## The weekly rhythm (standard plan, 8–10 hours)

A normal week has four slots. Here is what goes in each one.

| Slot | Time | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Learn | Wednesday night, 1.5 h | Work through that week's learning item |
| Read | Thursday, 1 h | Read one newsletter or doc from the row, and put your notes in the repo README |
| Build | Saturday, 3–4 h | Do the main build or analysis block |
| Ship and review | Sunday, 3 h | Finish and publish the deliverable (2 h), write one ship-log entry or post (45 min), then do the 15-minute Sunday review |

You don't need to tie the weeks to fixed calendar slots. Do them whenever you have the hours, and keep to the order above.

### How to read the weekly tables

- **Complexity:** Low means you can do it even when you're tired. Medium needs a focused block. High needs your best two hours, so give it your longest slot.
- **Ship this week:** this is the proof that the week happened, such as a link, a commit or a published page. "Read about X" is never the deliverable.
- **Where to learn:** these are the only resources for that week, and each one is a link. If you feel like opening anything else, put it in the parking lot.
- **How to build it:** these are links to the recipes on the [How to build tab](#build). Each recipe gives you ordered steps, code to paste, and a "done when" test.
- **Holiday weeks (W12 and W13):** these are about 6 hours each, by design.
- **Light plan:** keep only the "Ship this week" item and one learning item for each week.

## Anti-drift system

Everyone drifts at some point. So the plan decides in advance what you will do when it happens. The rule is simple: when you fall behind, cut scope. Never cut dates, and never go down to zero.

### The 10 rules

### Parking lot

New ideas, courses and side projects come here first. You look at them again only at the monthly gate.

### Sunday review (15 minutes, same time every week)

Ask yourself three questions:

- Did I ship this week's row? Answer yes or no, and add the link.
- Did I spend time on anything that isn't in the plan? If yes, is it in the parking lot now?
- What is the one task for next week, and is it scheduled?

### Tripwires: if this happens, do this

A tripwire is a warning sign you decide on in advance. Here is what to do for each one.

| If this happens | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| You missed this week's ship item | Do the floor week next week, then redo the missed item in your build slot. Don't squeeze two weeks of content into one |
| You missed two ship items in a row | Switch to the light plan for 2 weeks, and tell your accountability person |
| You are more than 2 weeks behind at a monthly gate | Run the catch-up protocol below. Don't move any deadline |
| You feel the urge to start a new course, app or tool | Write it in the parking lot, close the tab, and open this week's row |
| A work crunch is coming | Declare a floor week on Sunday, before the crunch starts, instead of silently skipping |
| You haven't opened this plan for 7 days | Open it, read the north star, and do one 45-minute session today |

### Catch-up protocol: what to cut, in order

If you are behind, cut from the top of this list and work down. Stop as soon as you are back on track.

1. Optional reading and newsletters
2. The paid cohort and certificates
3. Project E and any other optional build
4. Eval set size (100 cases down to 50)
5. The Project C build (keep it as a written case study)

Never cut Projects A, B and D, the Sunday review, or the weekly ship log. Those are the plan.

## Monthly gates: pass or adjust

At the end of each month, spend 30 minutes on the gate. Check each pass criterion and attach a link as proof. Then tick the gate. After that, look through the parking lot and decide what, if anything, to take out of it.

<!-- gates -->

**If three gates in a row fail:** stop, and decide on purpose what to do. You can either switch to the light plan for the next month, or move the whole plan back by 4 weeks and write down why. Quietly abandoning the plan is the only option that's ruled out.
