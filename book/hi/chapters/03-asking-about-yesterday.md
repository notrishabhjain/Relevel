Anaya ki users se seekhne ki pehli koshish se do page ke notes mile, aur jaankari lagbhag koi nahi. Usne support lead Farah Sheikh se aadha ghanta maanga aur unse do sawaal poochhe.

"Kya yeh bura nahi hai ki customers ke pehchaan ke numbers support chat mein pahunch jaate hain?" Farah ne kaha ki haan, bahut bura hai. "Aur kya aisa tool, jo unhe apne aap chhupa de, kaam ka hoga?" Farah ne kaha ki woh bahut madadgaar hoga.

Anaya ne notes mein "Definitely" likha, aur das minute tak khush rahi. Phir usne page dobara padha. Dono jawaab bilkul wahi the jo usne maange the.

Is chapter mein hum dekhenge ki woh baatcheet kyun fail hui, aur baad ki paanch baatcheeton mein kya sahi hua. Phir dekhenge ki Anaya ne nateejon ko ek aise description mein kaise badla jise koi aur check kar sake.

## Case: do meethe jawaab

Farah jhooth nahi bol rahi thi. Log tameez se pesh aate hain. Aap kisi tameezdaar insaan ko apne pasand ke jawaab ka ishaara de dein, toh woh aksar wahi jawaab de dega.

Jo sawaal apna jawaab khud saath le aata hai, use *leading question* kehte hain. Anaya saboot ikattha karne nikli thi, aur ikattha kar layi sirf sahmati. Sahmati saboot se bahut sasti hoti hai, aur uski keemat bhi utni hi kam hoti hai.

Agar woh yeh poochhti ki chat mein itne saare numbers dikhna kitna achha hai, toh Farah shayad usmein bhi koi achhi baat dhoondh leti. Madad karne ki koshish mein log aisa hi karte hain.

## Logon se unke beete hue kal ke baare mein kaise poochhein

Anaya ko ek chhoti kitaab yaad aayi, jo kisi ne use saalon pehle di thi. Us kitaab ka topic tha ki apni maa se yeh poochhna bekaar hai ki unhe aapka business idea pasand hai ya nahi. Maa haan kahengi, kyunki woh aapse pyaar karti hain. Aur aisi haan se kuch pata nahi chalta.

Kitaab ne teen rules diye the. Anaya ne unhe ek index card par utaar liya.

::: key User se baatcheet ke teen rules
Unki zindagi ke baare mein baat kijiye, apne idea ke baare mein nahi. Beete hue kal ki khaas baatein poochhiye, yeh nahi ki woh aage kya kar sakte hain. Kam boliye aur zyada suniye.
:::

Achhe aur kharab sawaal mein farq aksar tense ka hota hai. Future ke sawaal ("kya aap ise use karenge?", "aap kitna dene ko taiyaar hain?") se aapko sirf shishtaachaar bhari kahani milti hai. Beete kal ke sawaal ("mujhe us aakhri baar ke baare mein bataiye jab...") se pata chalta hai ki sach mein kya hua. Aur kahani mein aksar woh baatein nikalti hain jo bolne wale ko khud nahi pata thi ki woh bolne wala hai.

Dekhiye Anaya ne apne sawaal kaise badle.

Table: Anaya ke sawaal pehle aur baad mein
| Pehle | Baad mein |
| --- | --- |
| Kya numbers ka leak hona pareshaan nahi karta? | Mujhe us aakhri baar ke baare mein bataiye jab aapne chat mein pehchaan ka number dekha. Aapne kya kiya? |
| Kya aap aise tool ke liye paisa denge jo ise theek kare? | Aap abhi isse nipatne ke liye kya karte hain, aur uska aapko kitna cost padta hai? |
| Kya aapko lagta hai AI madad kar sakta hai? | Kya aapne kuch try kiya hai? Kya hua? |

Naye sawaalon mein se kisi mein bhi Anaya ka idea nahi hai. Is tarah ki *user interview* gawah ko sunne jaisi hoti hai, vote lene jaisi nahi. Future ke baare mein kisi ki raay kamzor saboot hai. Beete kal ki khaas kahani mazboot saboot hai. Jo ek insaan aisi teen kahaniyan sunaye, usne un sau logon se zyada bata diya jo kehte hain ki woh "definitely" kuch use karenge.

## Paanch baatcheetein

Agle hafte Anaya ne 30-30 minute ki paanch baatcheetein ki. Notes haath se liye, aur har baatcheet ko number diya, taaki har line wapas apni jagah tak jaa sake. Table mein dekhiye unhone kya bataya.

Table: Paanch baatcheeton ne kya dikhaya
| Number | Kaun | Unhone kya kaha | Isse kya dikha |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Farah Sheikh, support lead | Agent jab number dekhta hai toh koi rule nahi hai. Kuch export mein use mita dete hain, kuch Farah ko batate hain, zyadatar kuch nahi karte | Koi procedure nahi hai, isliye number ka hatna ittefaq ki baat hai |
| 2 | Suresh Gaikwad, loan officer | Kabhi-kabhi customers se number type karwata hai taaki unhe jaldi dhoondh sake | Kuch staff khud numbers maangte hain, aur ise risk nahi, speed ki baat maante hain |
| 3 | Pooja Nair, nurse aur borrower | Bot ne kaha "verify karna hai, isliye maine sab de diya" | Chatbot ke greeting ne hi number ko nyauta diya |
| 4 | Sanjay Patil, stationery shop ke maalik | "adhar" type karte hain, number ke beech mein space ya dash ke saath, hamesha Roman letters mein | Asli numbers aksar card par chhape format se milte nahi |
| 5 | Rutuja Joshi, customer | Apni 68 saal ki maa ka number unke liye type kiya; maa ko "pata nahi ki yeh kahin jaata hai" | Jo log risk mein hain, woh hamesha type karne wale log nahi hote |

Inmein se do baatein khamoshi se nikli. Jab Pooja ne kaha ki bot ne verify karne ko kaha, toh Anaya ne beech mein follow-up nahi poochha. Usne intezaar kiya. Teeno rules mein se yeh sabse mushkil tha. Phir Pooja ne wahi shabd dohraya.

Anaya ne chatbot ka opening message dhoondha. Usmein likha tha: "Verification ke liye kripya apni details share karein." Kisi ne socha hi nahi tha ki yeh sentence kya nyauta deta hai. Woh bas tameez se likh diya gaya tha.

## Chat ke peeche ka kaam

Weekend par Anaya ne apne flat ke farsh par notes bichha liye. Ab usne har insaan ke baare mein ek alag sawaal poochha: woh karna kya chahta tha?

Ek idea hai jo yahan madad karta hai. Log product nahi chahte. Woh apni situation badalna chahte hain. Woh progress jo ek insaan apni zindagi mein karna chahta hai, aur jiske liye woh kisi product ko "kaam par rakhta" hai, use *job to be done* kehte hain. Ise likhne ka ek tay saancha hai: jab main is situation mein hota hoon, mujhe yeh karna hai, taaki main yeh pa sakoon.

Pooja ke liye yeh tha: jab main loan ke decision ka intezaar kar rahi hoti hoon aur dimaag usi mein atka rehta hai, mujhe jaanna hai ki woh kahan tak pahunchi hai, bina kisi ko phone kiye, taaki main chinta chhod kar apna mahina plan kar sakoon.

Dhyaan dijiye, is sentence mein pehchaan ka number hai hi nahi. Number toh bas ittefaq tha, us tareeke ka jisse usne apna kaam nipatane ki koshish ki.

Farah ke liye yeh tha: jab main hafte ki chats analysis ke liye bhejti hoon, mujhe pakka karna hai ki kuch sensitive building se bahar na jaaye, taaki baad mein samjhaane wali main na banoon.

### Achha tool bhi kyun use nahi hota

Isi idea se yeh bhi samajh aata hai ki kaam ke products kabhi-kabhi ignore kyun ho jaate hain. Koi insaan kuch naya tabhi apnaata hai jab chaar forces uske paksh mein ek saath aati hain.

Pehla force *push* hai: abhi ki situation kitni buri lagti hai. Doosra *pull*: naya kya vaada karta hai. Inke saamne do aur forces khade hote hain. Ek *anxiety*, yaani naye ko lekar chinta. Aur doosri *habit*, yaani purane ki aadat. Table mein dekhiye Farah ki team ke liye chaaron.

Table: Chaar forces, Farah ki team par
| Force | Sahaj mein |
| --- | --- |
| Push | Leak ka darr, aur haath se numbers mitaane ki thakan |
| Pull | Aisa tool jo mitaane ka kaam apne aap kare |
| Anxiety | "Agar woh kuch chhupa de jo agent ko chahiye? Agar woh phone number blank kar de jo mujhe wapas call karne ke liye chahiye?" |
| Habit | Team ke paas pehle se nipatne ka ek tareeka hai, chahe woh kharab ho |

Jis product mein doosron ke likhe shabd sambhalne hote hain, usmein anxiety aksar chaaron mein sabse badi hoti hai. Log dheemi speed ki chinta kam karte hain, galat jawaab ki chinta bahut zyada.

## Kyun, aur phir kyun

Ab Anaya ne ek purana tareeka try kiya, jisse symptom se uski wajahon tak pahunchte hain. Aap poochhte hain kyun, aur phir jo jawaab mile, us par dobara kyun. Ise *five whys* kehte hain.

*Chats mein pehchaan ke numbers kyun hain?* Kyunki customers unhe type karte hain. *Woh kyun type karte hain?* Kyunki chatbot ka pehla message verification ke liye details maangta hai. *Woh aisa kyun kehta hai?* Kyunki aath mahine pehle kisi ne greeting likha tha, aur kisi ne use dobara review nahi kiya.

Chain saaf thi, aur kuch der Anaya ko achha laga. Phir usne use interviews se milaya. Sanjay ko type karne ke liye kisi ne kaha hi nahi tha. Suresh khud numbers maangta tha. Aur Rutuja ki maa ko chat ke baare mein kuch pata hi nahi tha.

Yahi is tareeke ka jaal hai. Asli problems ki shayad hi kabhi ek wajah hoti hai. Log pehli saaf chain par ruk jaate hain, aur woh chain aksar kisi badi cheez ki sirf ek shaakha hoti hai.

Toh Anaya ne ek tree banaya. Sabse upar likha "chat mein numbers". Uske neeche chaar shaakhayein banayi.

- Greeting numbers ko nyauta deta hai.
- Customers ne saalon call centres se seekha hai ki madadgaar hone ka matlab hai pehchaan dena.
- Kuch staff jaanboojh kar numbers maangte hain.
- Chat window kuch bhi le leta hai, photos bhi, bina kuch kahe.

Tree ne uska plan badal diya. Naya greeting problem ko kam karta, lekin baaki teen shaakhaon ko chhod deta. Customer aur chat ke beech khada koi tool woh pakad sakta tha jo baaki upaay chhod dete. Phir bhi greeting badalne mein koi cost nahi tha. Isliye Anaya ne use apni list mein joda, aur saath mein note likha "Pehle yeh karo".

## Jo jaana, use likhna

Hafte ke ant tak Anaya ke paas aise notes the jin par use bharosa tha. Ab usne woh kiya jo pehle chhod diya tha: apne users ko ek aise form mein likha jise koi check kar sake.

Support team ka uska description chhe line ka tha. Har line ke ant mein un baatcheeton ke numbers the jinse woh juda tha, jaise "Agents ke paas kya karna hai uska koi rule nahi (1, 5)".

Aise description ko *persona* kehte hain. Yeh ek tarah ke user ka chhota sa account hai, aur woh tabhi kaam ka hai jab uski har line kisi baatcheet tak jaaye. Baatcheeton se pehle likha persona research ke kapde pehna hua andaaza hai. AI assistant ka likha persona toh aur kharab hai, kyunki woh ek banaye hue naam aur pasandeeda rang ke saath aata hai. Anaya ka rule tha ki jis line ka reference nahi, use mita do.

Phir usne *problem statement* likha. Woh batata hai ki problem kiski hai, kya hai, aur aaj unhe uski kya keemat padti hai. Ise do sentences mein samaane mein usse teen koshishein lagi.

::: example Anaya ka problem statement
Sahaj ke customers aksar support chat mein pehchaan ke numbers type kar dete hain, kuch had tak isliye ki chatbot ka apna greeting "details" maangta hai (baatcheet 2, 3). Support agents ke paas inse nipatne ka koi rule nahi, isliye zyadatar numbers chat history, exports aur logs se kabhi hatte nahi (baatcheet 1, 5).
:::

Ant mein usne woh sab likha jo uske idea ke chalne ke liye sach hona chahiye.

1. Support staff automatic chhupane par itna bharosa karenge ki use on rakhenge.
2. Jo leak hota hai, usmein zyadatar numbers honge jinki ek pehchaani shakl hai, naam aur pate nahi.
3. Company saaf kiya hua text us bahar ki company ko bhejne degi jo chatbot ke replies likhti hai.
4. Customers ke messages zyadatar chhote honge aur kai languages ke mix mein likhe honge.

Usne har ek ko do sawaalon par rank kiya. Agar yeh assumption galat nikla, toh nuksaan kitna hoga? Aur use khud kitna shak hai?

Doosra assumption uncertain tha, lekin 37 flags ko chhaantne mein ek dopahar lagti, aur baat tay ho jaati. Chautha uncertain tha, par jaanleva nahi. Teesra dono tha. Agar compliance head Lakshmi Iyer mana kar deti, toh list ke baaki points ka koi matlab nahi rehta.

Jo assumption serious bhi ho aur uncertain bhi, use *riskiest assumption* kehte hain. Rule seedha hai: pehle usi ko test karo.

Use ek email se test kiya ja sakta tha. Email mein Lakshmi se 30 minute maange gaye, saboot par baat karne ke liye, aur yeh jaanne ke liye ki Anaya ko us par kaam karne ki permission hai ya nahi. Jawaab chaar minute mein aa gaya: Thursday, 3 baje, aur saboot lana, josh nahi.

## Saaraansh

Future ke sawaal ka tameezdaar jawaab milta hai. Beete kal ke sawaal ka asli account milta hai. Is tarah ki kuch baatcheetein ek lambi survey se zyada keemti hain.

- Leading question apna jawaab khud sujhata hai. Ilaaj yeh hai ki beeti hui khaas ghatnaon ke baare mein poochhein, aur saamne wale se kam boleen.
- Job to be done woh progress hai jo insaan karna chahta hai. Woh switch karega ya nahi, yeh push, pull, anxiety aur habit par tikta hai, aur anxiety aksar sabse mazboot hoti hai.
- Baar-baar "kyun" poochhna wajahon tak tabhi pahunchata hai jab nateeja tree ki tarah banaya jaaye, kyunki asli problems ki aksar kai wajahein hoti hain.
- Persona utna hi achha hai jitni baatcheeton se woh juda hai. Problem statement batata hai kaun, kya, aur kis keemat par, references ke saath.
- Riskiest assumption woh hai jo sabse zyada nuksaan wala aur sabse kam pakka ho, aur use sabse pehle test karna chahiye.

Agle chapter mein Anaya compliance head se milti hai, aur dekhti hai ki uske plan mein aur kis-kis ka daanv hai.
