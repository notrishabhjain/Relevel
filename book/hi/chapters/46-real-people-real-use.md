April ke aakhri Thursday ko, raat ke do bajkar das minute par, Imran ke phone ne ek chhote jaanwar ke kuchle jaane jaisi awaaz nikaali.

Use usne khud isi maqsad se set kiya tha, aur woh andhere mein ek pal use sarahta pada raha ki woh kitna achha kaam kar raha hai. Phir woh baith gaya. Usne kaha ki demonstration page par errors ek line paar kar gaye. Dhai baje tak usne karan dhoondh liya tha, ek certificate jo khatam ho gaya tha, aur paune teen baje tak use theek kar diya tha, aur teen baje usne log mein chaar lines likhin, aur ek aise insaan ke santosh ke saath sone gaya jiska smoke alarm wahi kar gaya jis liye kharida gaya tha.

Usne Anaya ko naashte par bataya. "Isi ko taiyaar kehte hain," usne kaha. "Yeh galat hua tha, aur mujhe kisi aur se pehle pata chal gaya."

## Taiyaar, aur kisi ka

Anaya ko poora pichhla hafta lag gaya tha yeh baat samajhne mein. Ek demonstration tab kaam karta hai jab aap dekh rahe hote hain. Ek product ko tab kaam karna hota hai jab koi nahi dekh raha, aur yeh hairaani ki baat hai ki jo kuch farak banata hai woh kitna kuch us hisse ke bahar rehta hai jo aapne banaya.

Imran ne use ek chhoti list di, aur usne use ek table mein badla jo baaki saal Imran ki desk ke upar chipki rahi.

| Kshetra | Taiyaar jab |
| --- | --- |
| Monitoring | Errors ya der badhne par ek alert kisi insaan tak pahunchta hai |
| Analytics | Tracking plan ke events live product se aate hain |
| Privacy | Ek notice batata hai ki kya ikattha hota hai, kyun, aur ise mitane ka tareeka |
| Feedback | Ek user ek click mein samasya report kar sakta hai |
| Rollback | Pichhle version par jaana aazmaya gaya hai, sirf plan nahi kiya gaya |
| Kharcha | Model provider ke saath ek kharche ki seema set hai |

Har row ek behes ka vishay thi, aur har ek aakhirkaar ek dopahar ka kaam tha. Jis par kisi ne behes nahi ki woh aakhri thi, kyunki July mein provider ka bill ek figure ke saath aaya tha jisne Mr. Bhatia ko apni chai neeche rakhne par majboor kiya.

Har system ko ek *malik* bhi chahiye, ek naam wala insaan jo jawaab deta hai jab woh bure ghante par toote. Guard ke liye, Imran ne har mahine ka pehla hafta liya aur Tanvi ne doosra. Unke numbers us page par the jo batata tha ki fault kaise report karein. Anaya ke paas product tha, jiska matlab tha ki agar maalikon ko aksar jagaya jaata, toh yeh pata lagana uski problem thi ki kyun.

## Un logon ko dhoondhna jo sach bolenge

Kit ko aazmane wale pehle log ajnabi nahi the. Woh Anaya ki behen ki dost, do purane saathi, aur uski building ka ek ladka tha jo computers mein achha tha. Sabhi uske baare mein mohak the.

"Yeh pyaara hai," uski dost ne kaha. "Bahut chaalak."

Usne kai dinon mein dekha ki unme se kisi ne jo kaha uski wajah se product mein kuch nahi badla. Dost bahut vinamra hote hain ki upyogi ho sakein. Woh aapko batayenge ki woh sochte hain ki aap kya chahte hain, aur woh use dobara istemaal nahi karenge.

Isliye usne woh kiya jo usne doosre mahine mein karna seekha tha: woh un logon ke paas wapas gayi jinse usne vasant mein baat ki thi, aur unke jaanne walon ke paas, aur unhe dhoondha jinhe abhi problem thi aur jo usi tarah ka software istemaal karte the. Aakhir mein use chhe teams mili. Kochi ki ek travel company jiski chat passport numbers se bhari thi. Indore ki ek clinic-booking service. Bengaluru ka ek chhota tutoring business. Teen aur. Usne unhe *design partners* kaha, aur shabd ne farak dala, kyunki ek **design partner** ek shuruaati user hai jo ek adhoore product ko istemaal karne aur aapko imaandari se batane par sahmat hota hai ki use kya mila. Woh abhi customers nahi hain, aur woh ek test audience bhi nahi hain.

## Usne unhe pehle kya bataya

Unme se kisi ke bhi kit ko chhune se pehle, usne har ek ko ek page bheja, jise Lakshmi ne do baar padha tha aur do jagah theek kiya tha.

Usme kaha tha ki product shuruaati hai aur galat ho sakta hai, aur ki woh kabhi-kabhi ek aisi detail chhod dega jise chhupana chahiye. Usme theek-theek bataya tha ki guard ko kya milega, woh kahan process hoga, kya record hoga, jo ginti aur kism the aur kabhi message ka text nahi, aur ek partner sab kuch kaise mita sakta hai. Usme kaha tha ki kuch bhi kisi aur maqsad ke liye istemaal nahi hoga, aur ki kisi partner ke messages doosre partner ki madad nahi karenge. Usne unse kaha ki ek chhote namoone se shuru karein jo unhone khud chuna aur taiyaar kiya ho, ek live feed se nahi. Aur usme kaha tha ki woh kabhi bhi ruk sakte hain, bina spashtikaran ke aur bina asuvidha ke.

Yeh *informed consent* hai. Samjhauta ek box par click nahi tha. Woh ek insaan tha jo samajhta tha ki woh kis par haan keh raha hai aur use bataya ja raha tha ki woh ja sakta hai.

Badle mein uski peshkash shuruaati access aur pehle saal ke liye kam keemat thi. Usne kuch bada socha, aur Lakshmi ne use roka. "Agar tum unhe bahut zyada paisa dogi, toh unhe lagega ki unhe taareef ka karz hai," usne kaha. "Tumhein aise log chahiye jo prabhaavit na hone ke liye azaad hon."

## Haath baandhe baithna

May ke pehle hafte mein usne chhe logon ko kit istemaal karte dekha, aur usne yeh waise hi kiya jaise usne January mein paanch developers ko dekha tha, haath peeche baandhe.

Ek *observation session* bilkul wahi hai: aap kisi ko ek asli kaam dete hain, chup rehte hain, aur har baar wahi kuch cheezein likhte hain. Kaam mein kitna samay laga. Woh kahan ruke. Unhone zor se kya kaha. Unhone kya aazmaya jiski use ummeed nahi thi. Kya unhone khatam kiya.

Use yeh lagbhag shaareerik roop se dardnak laga. Indore ka ek aadmi chalees second tak us setting ko ghoorta raha jo tay karti thi ki kin kismon ki details chhupayi jaayengi, saaf taur par anishchit ki woh kya karti hai, aur woh bolna chahti thi itna ki usne apne gaal ke andar kaat liya.

Hafte ke ant tak uske paas woh tha jiska use pehle se shak tha aur saabit nahi kar sakti thi. Log jo kehte hain aur jo karte hain do alag source hain, aur woh asahmat hote hain. Kochi mein Joseph ne ek call par use bataya ki guard "bahut aakraamak" hai: woh bahut zyada chhupata hai. Analytics ne dikhaya ki uski team ne teen hafton mein restore button do baar dabaya tha. Ek aur partner ne kaha ki use yeh pasand hai, aur barah dinon mein kit ko ek call ki thi.

"Jab woh alag hon toh vyavahaar par vishwas karo," Karan ne kaha, jo woh dheeli, nirash karne wala kaam karne ke liye uske saath baitha tha. "Phir poochho kyun. Vyavahaar batata hai ki kya sach hai. Karan tumhein poochhna padta hai."

Jab usne Joseph se poochha ki use kyun laga ki woh bahut zyada chhupata hai, toh woh hans pada. "Mujhe lagta hai maine use ek baar postcode chhupate dekha," usne kaha. "Aur maan liya ki woh josh mein hai."

## Samay se zyada samasyayein

Do hafte ke ant tak uske paas takleefon ka ek page tha. Kuch machine ki failures thin. Kuch ek button thi jise koi dhoondh nahi paya. Usne unhe alag lists mein rakhne ki pravritti ka virodh kiya, kyunki ek galat faisla aur ek uljhi hui screen user ko bilkul ek jaisa bharosa khone ki keemat dete hain.

Usne har ek ko teen cheezon par score kiya: kitne logon ko mili, jab mili toh kitni buri thi, aur use theek karne mein kitna kharcha aayega.

| Samasya | Kitni baar | Kitni buri | Theek karne ka kharcha | Kram |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Koi nahi bata sakta ki ek detail kyun chhupayi gayi | 6 mein se 4 teams | Zyada: woh bharosa khote hain | Chhota | 1 |
| 5,000 se zyada messages ki chats ka import fail hota hai | 1 team | Unke liye zyada | Madhyam | 2 |
| Detail ke kism ke naam bahut lambe | 6 mein se 3 | Kam | Chhota | 3 |
| Doosre chat system ke liye support chahiye | 2 prospects | Unhe poori tarah rok deta hai | Bada | Baad mein |

Ise dekhna taazgi bhara tha. Ek cheez jo ek customer ko poori tarah rokti hai woh zaroori nahi ki us cheez ke upar ho jo chaar ko chupchaap pareshaan karti hai. Frequency aur severity alag dishaaon mein kheenchte hain, aur kharcha tay karta hai ki aap kitni jaldi kisi ka jawaab de sakte hain.

## Ek badlaav, aur uske baare mein jo usne ummeed ki

Sabse upar ki row ko kehna aasaan tha aur theek se karna mushkil. Chhe mein se chaar teams ne, kisi na kisi roop mein, poochha tha, *yeh kyun chhupaya gaya?* Guard ko pata tha, aur usne kabhi kaha nahi tha.

Usne yojana ek card par likhi, us roop mein jisme Imran ne use har yojana likhna sikhaya tha.

*Humne dekha ki chhe mein se chaar teams ne poochha ki ek detail kyun chhupayi gayi, aur reviewers ne chhupaye gaye details mein se sau mein sirf 58 ko sahi maana. Hum ek "yeh kyun chhupaya gaya" link jodenge jo woh teen phrase dikhata hai jinhone faisla kiya. Hum ummeed karte hain ki sahi maana gaya hissa do hafte mein 65 se upar jaye, prati message koi atirikt samay nahi. Agar woh nahi hilta, toh ho sakta hai problem vyakhya mein nahi, faisle mein ho, aur hum kuch aur badalne se pehle pachaas vivaadit cases haath se padhenge.*

Aakhri vaakya ko pehle likhna, kisi bhi nateeje se pehle, woh hissa tha jis par use sabse zyada garv tha. Iska matlab tha ki agar number nahi hila, toh use pehle se pata hoga ki use kya karna hai.

Woh 67 par pahunch gaya.

## Logon ko batana ki kya badla

Badlaav ek Thursday ko ship hua, aur usne usi tarah uske baare mein likha jaise un products se seekha tha jo use sabse zyada pasand the. *Changelog* har release mein kya badla uski chhoti public list hai, user ke liye likhi gayi, banane wale ke liye nahi.

*Naya: "Yeh kyun chhupaya gaya?" Kisi bhi masked detail ke bagal mein chhote sawaal ke nishaan par click karo aur woh teen phrase dekho jinke kaaran faisla hua. Agar woh galat tha, toh Restore dabao, aur humein batao.*

Usne bataya ki kya naya tha, ise kaise istemaal karein, aur agar yeh galat behave kare toh kya karein. Usne yeh nahi likha "masking subsystem ki explainability behtar ki." Tanvi ne, jo proofread kar rahi thi, sirf ek shabd kaat kar ek chhota rakha.

Usne us hafte ek aur kaam kiya jiska shipping se koi lena-dena nahi tha. Usne ek **support log** shuru kiya. Kisi bhi partner se madad ka har anurodh ek category aur ek account ke saath ek sheet mein gaya. Isme kuch kharcha nahi tha, aur usne ek mahine baad samjha ki yeh sabse sasta research tha jo usne kabhi kiya, kyunki users use bina maange bata rahe the ki unhe kya chahiye.

Mondays ko woh aur Karan ise analytics ke saath padhte the. Ek takleef jo support log mein, observation sessions mein aur numbers mein ek saath dikhti thi woh lagbhag pakka asli thi. Sirf ek mein dikhne wali ko woh dekhti thi.

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Ek demonstration tab kaam karta hai jab koi dekh raha ho; ek product ko tab kaam karna padta hai jab koi nahi dekh raha. Uske liye use alerts chahiye jo ek insaan tak pahunchein, live system se aate events, ek privacy notice, aur kisi fault ki report karne ka ek click wala tareeka. Use ek rollback chahiye jise aazmaya gaya ho, ek kharche ki seema, aur ek naam wala malik. Shuruaati users aise log hone chahiye jinhe abhi problem hai aur jo aapko niraash karne ke liye azaad mehsoos karte hain, jo dost shaayad hi karte hain. Unhe saaf, likhit mein bataya jaata hai ki kya ikattha hota hai, kahan jaata hai aur kaise jaana hai, aur unhe kuch nyayochit diya jaata hai, kuch bada nahi. Observation sessions mein aap ek asli kaam dete hain aur chup rehte hain, har baar wahi kuch cheezein note karte hue, aur jab log jo kehte hain aur jo karte hain asahmat hon toh aap vyavahaar par vishwas karte hain aur phir kyun poochhte hain. Samasyaon ko kitni baar, kitni buri aur kitni mehngi se score kiya jaata hai, machine ki failures aur uljhi hui screens ek hi list mein. Ek badlaav ko uske saboot, uske apekshit asar aur jab woh kaam na kare uske plan ke saath ship kiya jaata hai, aur saaf shabdon mein ghoshit kiya jaata hai. Aur madad ka har anurodh rakha jaata hai, kyunki ek takleef jo support requests, sessions aur numbers mein ek saath dikhti hai woh asli hai.
