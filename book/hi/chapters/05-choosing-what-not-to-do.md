Har Sunday subah Anaya Dr. Meenakshi Rao ko phone karti thi. Meenakshi ne Anaya ko linguistics padhayi thi, yaani bhasha ka vigyaan. Ab woh Bengaluru mein retire ho chuki thi. March ke aakhri hafte mein Anaya ne apne kaam ki list unhe padhkar sunayi.

Chatbot ka greeting dobara likho. Pehchaan ke numbers dhoondhne ka tool banao. Use naam aur pate tak badhao. Hindi mein chalao. Hinglish mein chalao. Photos padho. Voice notes padho. Doosri companies ko becho. Browser mein chalne wala chhota version banao.

Meenakshi ne poochha, in nau mein se aap kis kaam ko na kahengi? Anaya ne kaha ki woh sab karegi, aakhir mein. "Toh tumhare paas strategy nahi hai," Meenakshi ne kaha. "Tumhare paas calendar wali ek ummeed hai."

Yeh chapter dekhta hai ki strategy kin cheezon se bani hoti hai, aur team kahan se shuru karna chunti hai. Yeh bhi dikhata hai ki time ke liye takraate kaam ko rank kaise karein. Tarkeeb yeh hai: formula madad kare, par faisla woh na le.

## Case: nau achhe ideas

Shaadi ki guest list sochiye jismein har cousin ka naam hai. Kisi ko bahar nahi rakha, toh list yeh nahi bata sakti ki aage kaun baithega. Anaya ki list ka har item theek tha, aur mushkil wahi thi. Agar list mein kisi cheez ko kabhi mana nahi kiya jaata, toh woh Monday subah ke faisle mein madad nahi kar sakti. Har kaam ka bachaav kiya ja sakta hai.

Meenakshi kadvi nahi thi. Woh practical thi. Strategy tab shuru hoti hai jab koi cheez list se bahar hoti hai.

## Vision, strategy aur plan

Meenakshi ne Anaya se kaha ki woh bataye, teen cheezon mein farq kya hai. Log aksar inhe ek hi maan lete hain. Train ke safar jaisa sochiye. Vision woh shehar hai jahan aap pahunchna chahte hain. Strategy woh raasta hai jo aap chunte hain, aur kyun. Plan is hafte ka timetable hai. Table mein dekhiye guard ke liye teeno kaise dikhte hain.

Table: Vision, strategy aur plan
| | Kaun sa sawaal | Time horizon | Guard ka version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vision | Hum kahan ja rahe hain? | 3 se 5 saal | India mein customers se baat karne wali har company ki har team ek nazar mein dekh sake ki koi personal cheez utni door nahi ja rahi jitni jaani chahiye |
| Strategy | Hum kaise jeetenge? | 1 se 2 saal | Sahaj ki apni chat se shuru karo, aur kahin nahi. Log asal mein jis mix mein type karte hain, usmein desh ke sabse achhe bano |
| Plan | Ab hum kya karenge? | Is quarter | Number dhoondhne wala hissa chat mein, support team ko, June ke ant tak ship karo |

Vision bada ho sakta hai. Plan likhna aasaan hai. Strategy mushkil hai. Woh itni saaf honi chahiye ki galat ho sake. Aur usmein cheezein chhodni padti hain.

## Strategy ke hisse

Strategy ke teen hisse hote hain. Har ek ki apni wajah hai.

Pehla hissa hai *diagnosis*. Yeh dawai se pehle doctor ki jaanch jaisa hai. Yeh asli problem ka imaandaar hisaab hai. Ise aise likha jaata hai ki koi asahmat ho sake. "Humein ek privacy problem hai" diagnosis nahi hai.

Anaya ka version yeh tha. Personal details wahan pahunch jaati hain jahan unhe nahi pahunchna chahiye. Customers unhe aazadi se type karte hain. Chatbot unhe maangta hai. Unhe hataane ka kisi ke paas koi tareeka nahi hai. Aur aaj ke tools ek hi writing system ke liye bane hain, isliye yahan log jaise likhte hain, usmein fail ho jaate hain. Ise sales pitch jaisa na lagne mein chaar drafts lage.

Doosra hissa hai *guiding policy*. Ghar ke niyam sochiye. Yeh problem ke liye aapka poora tareeka hai, aur ismein woh bhi aata hai jo aap karne se mana karenge. Anaya ki policy yeh thi. Pehle details ko unki shakl se pakdo, kyunki woh sasta aur pakka hai. Phir naam aur pate sambhalo, kyunki doosre tools wahin fail hote hain. Sab kuch company ke andar karo, logon ke likhne ke asli tareeke mein. Aur har us cheez ko hal karne ki koshish mat karo jise privacy kaha ja sakta hai.

Teesra hissa hai saath-saath chalne wale kaamon ka ek set. Matlab kuch steps jo policy ko poora karte hain aur ek doosre ki madad karte hain. Anaya ne chaar likhe. Usne dekha ki do dusron par depend hi nahi karte, aur unhe kaat diya.

::: watch Goal strategy nahi hai
"India ka sabse bada privacy platform banna" ek goal hai. Usmein koi diagnosis nahi hai. Woh Monday ko kisi ko kuch nahi batata. Ek jaldi ka test hai. Strategy se company ka naam hata dijiye. Phir poochhiye ki kya koi rival use apne kaagaz mein chipka sakta hai, aur kisi ko pata na chale.

"Customer privacy ko bade scale par bachane ke liye best-in-class technology use karo" jaisa sentence is test mein paas ho jaata hai. Isliye woh abhi strategy nahi hai. Anaya ke doosre paragraph mein aisa ek version ghus aaya tha. Usne use mita diya.
:::

## Kiske liye, aur kahan se shuru

*Value proposition* dukaan ke bahar ka board hai. Woh saaf-saaf batata hai ki product kise serve karta hai, unke liye kaun sa kaam karta hai, aur unke paas jo hai usse behtar kyun hai. Pichhle chapter ka Anaya ka positioning statement iska pehla draft tha. Ab use asli bojh uthana tha. Poori strategy us par tiki thi.

Kahan se shuru karna hai, yeh alag faisla hai. Meenakshi ka rule tha: itne chhote se shuru karo ki kisi ek cheez mein sabse achhe ban sako. Team baad mein faila sakti hai. Lekin har cheez mein sirf theek-thaak hone se ubarna mushkil hota hai.

Product ka woh chhota pehla target, jise woh saaf taur par jeet sakta hai, uska *wedge* kehlaata hai. Lakdi mein ghusti patli nok sochiye. Woh itna chhota hona chahiye ki usmein behtareen ban sakein. Aur woh kisi badi cheez se juda hona chahiye, taaki use jeetne se ek darwaza khule.

Guard ke liye wedge "India ki companies ke liye privacy" nahi tha. Wedge tha: Sahaj ki apni support chat mein personal details dhoondhna aur chhupana, un teen tareekon mein jinmein log likhte hain. Yeh vision se bahut chhota tha. Aur ise jeeta ja sakta tha. Company ke andar ek taiyaar customer tha aur asli messages ka bhandaar tha. Aur Farah Sheikh thi, jise ek hafte mein pata chal jaata ki woh kaam karta hai ya nahi.

### Ek number

Wedge ke saath ek *North Star* joda gaya. Naav chalane wale ek taare ke sahare chalte hain. Team ek sentence aur ek number ke sahare chalti hai. Sentence batata hai ki customer ko kya value milti hai. Number use naapta hai.

Yahan sentence yeh tha: messages chat se is tarah nikalte hain ki unmein kuch personal nahi bachta. Number tha aise messages ka share. Number badhta, toh kaam safal ho raha tha. Agar Anaya kabhi khud ko kisi doosre number par behas karte paati, toh woh ruk kar poochhti ki aisa kyun.

## Product ka naam

Farah ne project ke baare mein suna, aur Anaya ke desk par ruki. Usne kaha, sab ise "privacy wali cheez" keh rahe hain. Agar ise ek sahi naam nahi mila, toh woh wahi naam pa legi jo meeting mein sabse pehle bola jaayega. Jaise bachche ko wahi nickname mil jaata hai jo sabse pehle koi bol de.

Naam mein yeh dikhna chahiye ki product kya hai aur kiske liye hai. Aur customer ko phone par bolna bhi aasaan hona chahiye.

Unhone baarah naam socha. Farah apne customers ki zubaan mein sochti hai. Usne Bharat Privacy Guard sujhaya. Usne andaaza lagaya ki ek hafte mein sab ise "the guard" kehne lagenge. Yehi naam chuna gaya, kuch is wajah se ki woh bilkul kisi signboard ke naam jitna bhaari-bharkam tha.

## Kaam ko rank karna

Strategy ab tay thi. Anaya ko nau items ka ek order chahiye tha. Usne ek aam scoring tareeka use kiya, *RICE*. Yeh exam ke marks jaisa hai. Har option ko ek score milta hai, jo chaar sawaalon se banta hai.

- Reach: ek tay time mein kitne log ya messages isse chhuyenge?
- Impact: har ek ke liye yeh kitna badlega?
- Confidence: percent mein, pehle do jawaab kitne pakke hain?
- Effort: kaam mein kitne person-weeks lagenge? Ek person-week matlab ek insaan ka ek hafte ka kaam.

Reach, impact aur confidence ko aapas mein guna kijiye. Phir effort se bhaag dijiye. Table mein uske chaar options hain.

Table: Chaar options ke RICE scores
| Option | Reach | Impact | Confidence | Effort (person-weeks) | Score |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Greeting dobara likhna | 400 | 1 | 80% | 0.1 | 3,200 |
| Fixed-shape numbers dhoondhna | 400 | 2 | 80% | 2 | 320 |
| Cards ki photos pakadna | 30 | 3 | 50% | 1 | 45 |
| Hinglish mein naam aur pate dhoondhna | 150 | 2 | 50% | 6 | 25 |

Aakhri row dekhiye. Usmein wahi kaam tha jo project ke liye sabse zaroori tha. Uska score sabse kam aaya.

Imran Qureshi yeh dekhne aaya tha ki Anaya bhaunhein kyun chadha rahi hai. Usne maana ki is hisaab se woh kaam sabse peeche aata hai. "Toh aap use override kar dijiye," usne kaha. "Zor se bolkar. Jo allowed nahi hai, woh yeh dikhana hai ki number ne aisa kaha."

::: key Score kis kaam ka hai
Teacher kisi achhe karan se student ko zyada marks de sakte hain, aur karan likh dete hain. RICE score bhi aise hi kaam karta hai. Uske inputs zyadatar andaaze hote hain. Formula ko strategy ke baare mein kuch nahi pata, na yeh ki kaun sa kaam kis par depend karta hai. Score behas ko vyavasthit karne mein madad karta hai. Woh behas ki jagah nahi leta. Jab team kisi score ko override kare, toh use wajah likh deni chahiye. Anaya ne aakhri row ke paas likha: "Yeh wedge hai. Baaki sab kahin aur maujood hai."
:::

Doosre logon ke saath scope par sahmati banane ke liye ek doosra tareeka behtar hai. Scope matlab kitna kaam andar hai aur kitna bahar. *MoSCoW* safar ke liye bag pack karne jaisa hai. Yeh har item ko Must, Should, Could ya Won't (nahi lenge) mein daalta hai.

Anaya ke Must the greeting aur fixed-shape numbers dhoondhne wala hissa. Should the naam aur pate. Could the photos. Saal ke Won't the: India ki doosri languages, voice notes, browser version, aur Sahaj ke bahar kisi ko bechna. Imran ne aakhri column do baar padha. Usne kaha ki yeh pehli Won't list hai jo sharmaati nahi.

Ek aur idea ek finance wale dost se aaya. *Cost of delay* woh hai jo aap har us hafte khote hain jab tak koi option intezaar karta hai. Yeh shelf par rakhe doodh ke phatne jaisa hai. Zyadatar items ke liye jawaab tha "thoda". Greeting ke liye jawaab tha: customers se unki details maangne ka ek aur hafta. Bas isi se greeting sabse aage aa gayi, bina aur hisaab ke.

## Saaraansh

Vision batata hai ki company kahan ja rahi hai. Strategy batati hai ki woh kaise jeetegi. Plan batata hai ki is quarter kya hoga. Cheezein chhodne par sirf strategy majboor karti hai.

- Strategy ke teen hisse hain: diagnosis (asli problem kya hai), guiding policy (tareeka, aur woh jo aap mana karte hain) aur ek doosre ki madad karne wale kaam. Company ka naam hata kar dekhna batata hai ki woh khaas hai ya nahi.
- Wedge woh chhota pehla target hai jise saaf taur par jeeta ja sake. North Star customer value ka ek sentence aur use naapne wala ek number hai.
- RICE options ko reach, impact aur confidence ko effort se bhaag dekar score karta hai. Score faisle ko vyavasthit karta hai. Wajah likh kar aap use override kar sakte hain.
- MoSCoW items ko Must, Should, Could aur Won't mein baant kar scope tay karta hai. Won't list sabse zyada batati hai.
- Cost of delay, yaani intezaar ke har hafte ka nuksaan, aksar wahan order tay kar deta hai jahan scoring nahi kar paati.

Agle chapter mein Anaya is order ko ek aise plan mein badalti hai jis par doosre log bharosa kar sakein.
