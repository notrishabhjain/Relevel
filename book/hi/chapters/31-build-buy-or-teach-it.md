Vendor ka naam VaultLeaf tha, aur usne do log bheje, ek salesman aur ek engineer, jo Imran ke hisaab se achha sanket tha aur Lakshmi ke hisaab se aur bura.

August ke teesre hafte ka ek Thursday tha, aur pitch chamakdar thi. VaultLeaf woh software bechti thi jo text mein personal data dhoondhta aur chhupata tha, aur woh banks ke liye aisa karti thi. Uske paas graphs wala console tha. Uski slide ki deewar par ek certificate tha. Usne kaha ki woh Sahaj mein teen hafte mein chalu ho sakti hai aur uski accuracy ninyaanbe percent hai.

"Kis ke khilaaf?" Anaya ne poochha.

Engineer muskuraya, jaise keh raha ho ki yahi sawaal tha jiske poochhe jaane ki woh ummeed kar raha tha. "Hamare apne benchmark ke khilaaf."

"Kya hum ise apne ke khilaaf chala sakte hain?"

## Sabke liye wahi test

Jab koi aisa kuch kharidne ka prastaav rakhe jo aap bana sakte the, toh pehli cheez karni hai yahi, aur ise zyadatar kharidar chhod dete hain. Behes aamtaur par daam aur demo par ghoomti hai. Dono sawaal ko tay nahi karte. Jo tay karta hai woh hai vendor ke product ko waise naapna jaise aap apna naapte: aapki answer key par, aapke kism ke text ke saath.

VaultLeaf sahmat hui, is tarah jisse lagta tha ki woh yeh aksar nahi karti, do sau chaar rows chalane ke liye. Ek hafte baad numbers aaye. English pehchaan ke numbers par woh shaandaar tha, guard se thoda behtar. Hinglish naamon par, woh cheez jiske liye guard design hua tha, usne sau mein bausath dhoondhe. Devanagari lipi mein Hindi par usne aur kharab kiya. Shabdon mein likhe number ko sambhalne ka uske paas koi tareeka nahi tha.

Salesman ko hairaani nahi hui. "Humare zyadatar customers English-first hain," usne kaha.

"Humare nahi hain," Anaya ne kaha.

Usi email mein ek doosra jawaab tha, aur wahi tha jo Lakshmi ne teen baar padha. Standard product text ko VaultLeaf ke cloud par process ke liye bhejta tha, India ke bahar ek kshetra mein. Ek private deployment, Sahaj ke apne systems ke andar chalta hua, lagbhag teen guna daam par uplabdh tha.

## Aap asal mein kya kharid rahe hain

Imran shaant tha. Use vendor pasand tha, usne kaha, jab tak sab samajh lein ki paisa kis liye hai.

"Aap shaayad hi model kharidte hain," usne kaha. "Zyadatar wahi models istemaal karte hain. Aap jo kharidte hain woh connectors hain, permissions, kisne kya kiya uska record, ek support contract, aur ek insaan jo raat ke do baje uthta hai jab kuch toot jaata hai. Woh aakhri aksar license se zyada keemti hota hai, aur aksar jod se bahar chhoda jaata hai."

Usne board par chaar sawaal likhe, jo, uske anubhav mein, tay karte the. *Kya yeh woh cheez hai jiske liye humara product hai, ya plumbing? Ise kitni baar badalna padega? Raat do baje kaun jaaga hai? Agar vendor khareed liya jaye, ya band ho jaye toh kya hoga?*

Unhone bina behas ke unme se guzare. Hinglish mein personal details dhoondhna woh cheez thi jis par Sahaj ka poora mamla tika tha, isliye unhe ise apna rakhna chahiye. Ise lagatar badalna padega, kyunki answer key har hafte badhti thi aur failures asli customers se aati thin. Raat ke do baje ke baare mein, unke paas koi plan bilkul nahi tha, jo jaanne layak tha. Aur aakhri sawaal par VaultLeaf mein koi nahi bata paaya tha ki chhodne ke liye kya karna padega.

Unhone do saal ke andaaze banaye, mote numbers ke saath, ek kaagaz par. Private deployment kharidna lagbhag atthaavan lakh rupaye aaya. Guard banana aur chalana lagbhag ikahattar. Farak itna chhota tha ki dono taraf behes ho sakti thi. Jisne ise tay kiya woh jod nahi tha.

"Plumbing kharido aur woh hissa banao jo product ko alag banata hai," Imran ne kaha. "Yeh aamtaur par sahi jawaab hai, aur yahi hamare paas hai."

Plan, jaise woh khatam hua, finder, answer key aur scoreboard banana tha, aur sirf commodity cheezein kiraye par lena: India ke ek kshetra mein models ki hosting, aur logs ke liye ek readymade console. Anaya ne, kuch bhi dastakhat karne se pehle, woh likha jo usne likhna seekha tha. *Main kharidne par switch karungi agar: VaultLeaf hamari key par Hinglish naamon par nabbe tak pahunche, ya agar rakhrakhav ek insaan ke samay ke paanchve hisse se zyada par pade. Main aur banane par switch karungi agar: agle tier par daam badhe. 1 December ko review.* Ek faisla jiske saath tareekh ho, usne paya, uske bina wale se kahin zyada aasaani se palta ja sakta hai.

## Product ke neeche kya hai

Meeting se wapas chalte hue usne Imran se woh sawaal poochha jo March se uske dimaag mein tha.

"Kya hum un logon se muqabla kar rahe hain jo bade models banate hain?"

Woh Sahaj ki mithai ki dukaan ke saamne footpath par ruk gaya, aur dukaan ke maalik se udhaar liye pen se ek invoice ke peeche usne use banaya.

AI products layers mein bante hain, usne kaha, har ek neeche wali par tiki. Sabse neeche, computers aur chips aur cloud. Uske upar, models: kuch un companies ke jo unka access bechti hain, aur kuch khule roop se jaari. Uske upar, unhe sambhalne ke tools: matlabon ke naqshe ke store, test karne aur track karne ke upkaran. Aur sabse upar, applications jo log asal mein istemaal karte hain. Poori shrinkhala *AI value chain* hai, aur har layer ka fayda kisi alag cheez se aata hai. Neeche ki layers ka scale aur paise se. Upar ka workflow, data aur bharose se.

"Hum mein se zyadatar upar rehte hain," usne kaha. "Aur upar, model tumhara fayda nahi hai. Tumhare competitors wahi ek ko usi darwaze se bula sakte hain. Jo copy karna mushkil hai woh yeh hai ki tum kisi ke kaam mein kitne gehre bune ho. Aur woh data jo kisi aur ke paas nahi."

"Corrections," Anaya ne kaha.

"Corrections. Jab bhi Farah ka koi agent *guard ne woh chhupa diya jo mujhe chahiye tha* dabata hai, hum kuch seekhte hain jo kisi competitor ko nahi pata. Par tabhi jab woh anokha ho, product ko behtar banaye, aur paana mushkil ho. Jo data ka fayda in teeno mein se kisi mein fail ho woh ek kahani hai."

Usne chalte-chalte zikr kiya tha ki kuch models sabke liye khule hain. Yeh *open-weight models* hain: unke weights prakashit hain, taaki aap unhe download karke apne computers par chala sakein, kisi aur ke ko bulane ki jagah. Woh aamtaur par sabse achhe band wale se thode peeche hote hain. Woh aapko is par zyada control dete hain ki data kahan jaata hai aur uska kharcha kya hai. Aur aap iska paisa hosting aur rakhrakhav mein dete hain. Ek company ke liye jisne waada kiya hai ki customer ka vaakya kabhi building se bahar nahi jaayega, yeh aasaan chunav tha. Yeh uski specification ke hashiye ke sawaal ka jawaab bhi tha. Saavdhaan judge ek open-weight model hoga, Sahaj ke apne servers par chalta hua.

Usne, aakhri baat ke roop mein, ek shabd joda jiska vendor ke office mein bahut wazan tha. *Lock-in* ek provider se bandh jaana hai kyunki chhodna bahut mehnga padega, dobara likhne mein, dobara naapne mein ya khoye hue features mein. Ilaaj, usne kaha, answer key mein tha. "Agar test set kisi vendor par nirbhar nahi hai, toh badalne mein din lagte hain."

## Sikhao

Monday ko founder ne Anaya ko corridor mein roka. Mr. Bhatia ne plane mein kuch padha tha, aur usne asar chhoda tha.

"Hum apna model train kyun nahi karte?" usne kaha. "Hinglish par. Sab kar rahe hain. Woh humara apna hota."

Yeh ek sawaal tha jo har team se poochha jaata hai, aamtaur par roadmap meeting mein, aur aamtaur par pehle ki kisi ne kaha ho ki kya galat hai. Haan kehna ek quarter baandh deta hai. Na kehna mahatvakanksha-heen lagta hai. Anaya ne kaha ki woh Friday tak jawaab laayegi, aur usne yeh maana.

Idea ko *fine-tuning* kehte hain: ek maujooda model ko le kar use aapke apne udaharanon par aur train karna. Yeh aise model ka seedha raasta lagta hai jo aapki duniya jaanta hai. Anaya ke paas woh ek cheez thi jo sawaal ko jawaab dene layak banati hai, yaani list.

June ki ikatees failures, unki ginti ke saath. Usne har ek ko chaar kismon mein se ek mein chhaanta, kyunki chaar hain, aur har ek ek alag ilaaj ki taraf ishaara karta hai. Kuch machine ke kuch *na jaanne* ke the: jawaab gayab, purana ya gadha hua tha. Kuch woh *jaanti thi par galat behave kar rahi thi*: content sahi tha par format ya niyam galat. Kuch woh *kaam karke nikaal nahi paa rahi thi*: kai charan, ya asli dwividha. Aur kuch *sahi par bahut mehnge* the.

Uski ikatees mein se, koi bhi na jaanne ka case nahi tha. Order numbers saboot ki problem the, lookup se hal hui. *Ji* ke baad wale naam, aath, vyavahaar the: machine ko convention dikhayi nahi gayi thi, aur ek worked example ne unme se zyadatar ko ek dopahar mein theek kar diya. Aadhe chhupe pate format the. Lines mein tooti numbers kaatne ke niyam ki thi, machine ki nahi.

"Fine-tuning vyavahaar sikhata hai, tathya nahi," usne Friday ko Mr. Bhatia se kaha. "Agar shikayat yeh hoti ki use humari policy nahi pata, toh woh galat tool hota. Humari shikayatein vyavahaar hain, aur humne abhi sasti cheezein aazmayi nahi hain. Pehli do ghanton mein palti ja sakti hain."

Ek kharcha tha jo usne use gina diya, kyunki prastaav use chhod dete hain. Kisi ko udaharan banane aur unhe taza rakhna hoga. Pehle ek test set chahiye, warna koi nahi bata sakta ki usne madad ki ya nahi, aur woh kabhi unhi udaharanon jaisa nahi hona chahiye jin se model ne seekha. Har baar jab base model retire ho, tuning dobara karni hogi. Aur sudhaar nirdeshon aur index ki jagah ek provider ke system ke andar rehta hai, jo apne aap mein ek lock-in hai.

"Toh jawaab na hai?" Mr. Bhatia ne kaha.

"Yeh *abhi nahi* hai. Is kram mein: nirdesh, phir saboot, phir ek chhota model, phir fine-tuning. Agar pehle teen ke baad bhi *ji* wale naam fail hote hain, toh hum sikhayenge. Aur humein pata chalega, kyunki humare paas ek key hai."

Chhota model woh vikalp tha jis par usne shaayad hi socha tha, aur Imran use sabse zyada maayne rakhne wala maanta tha. Ek sasta, tez, sankra model, andar chalta hua, aksar ucch volume aur sankre kaam wale feature ka arthashastra tay karta hai. Chhote messages mein naam dhoondhna bilkul aisa hi kaam tha.

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Jab koi aisa kuch kharidne ka prastaav rakhe jo aap bana sakte the, toh daam aur demo ise tay nahi karte; vendor ke product ko aapki apni answer key par naapna karta hai, aur chaar sawaal bhi: kya yeh aapke kaam ka kendra hai, ise kitni baar badalna padega, jab yeh toote toh kaun jaaga hai, aur ise chhodne ke liye kya karna hoga. Achha jawaab aksar plumbing kharidna aur woh hissa banana hai jo product ko alag banata hai, un wajahon ke saath jo faisla palatengi, tareekh ke saath likhi hui. Value chain ke upar model shaayad hi fayda hota hai; anokha data, workflow aur bharosa hote hain. Open-weight model apne machines par chalaya ja sakta hai. Jab model kam pade, toh badalne ki chaar cheezein hain, aur fine-tuning, jo vyavahaar sikhata hai tathya nahi, nirdeshon, saboot aur ek chhote model ke baad aata hai, aur use ek alag test set chahiye.
