Rhea Kapoor ne June mein ek Sunday shaam phone kiya. Usne shuruaat wahan se ki jahan se woh log karte hain jinhone maafi rehearse ki ho, yaani uske ant se. Usne kaha ki use pata hai ki yeh Anaya ki akeli free raat hai, aur woh use zyada der nahi rokegi.

Anaya Rhea se autumn mein ek meetup mein mili thi, jahan Rhea ne speaker se wahi ek sawaal poocha tha jo koi aur poochna nahi chahta tha. Rhea chhe saal ek bank mein operations analyst rahi thi. Phir ek saal usne woh sab ek-ek hissa karke seekha jo Anaya ne ek saal akele, raat mein kiya tha. Aur usne apna kuch banaya bhi tha: ek chhota tool jo customer-support tickets ka ek hafta padhta, unhe themes mein baant deta, aur woh report likhta jo uska purana manager ek din mein likhta tha. Das design partners ne use use kiya tha. Lekin usne yeh kaam kisi ko nahi dikhaya tha jo use job de sake.

Rhea ke saamne das job advertisements khule the, aur sab par "AI product manager" likha tha. Use samajh nahi aa raha tha ki woh unmein se kisi ke liye sahi kyun hai. Anaya ne kaha ki woh das bhej de, aur yeh bhi bataye ki uske resume ke sabse upar kya likha hai. Thodi der ruk kar Rhea ne kaha ki usmein likha hai ki woh AI ke prati passionate hai.

## Case: das advertisements aur ek resume

Aage ke sections kuch Sunday shaamon ka kaam hain. Yeh book ke ant mein isliye aate hain kyunki yeh pehle ke sab kuch ko use karte hain. Yeh woh skill bhi sikhate hain jo zyadatar log chhod dete hain: dikhana ki aapne kya kiya hai.

## Ek title, chaar jobs

Anaya ne Rhea ko bataya ki sabse pehle yeh samajhna hai ki title ek label hai jo kam se kam chaar alag jobs par chipka diya gaya hai. Table chaaron dikhati hai, aur yeh ki har kisam ki company apne hire kiye hue insaan se kya chahti hai.

Table: Ek title ke peeche chaar jobs
| Company ki kisam | Kya banati hai | Kya chahti hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ek maujooda product mein AI features jodti hai | Un users ke liye features jo uske paas pehle se hain | Judgement, ship karne ka record, aur un logon ke saath kaam karne ki kabiliyat jo models banate hain |
| Aisi cheez banati hai jo AI ke bina hoti hi nahi | Ek naya product | Speed, prototyping, aisi cheez ke saath aaram jo kabhi-kabhi fail hoti hai, aur naapne ki aadat |
| Doosri teams ke liye tools, interfaces aur models banati hai | Platforms | Technical depth aur is baat ki samajh ki developer ko kya takleef deta hai |
| Apne hi employees ke liye AI banati hai | Internal tools | Koi jo logon ko unke kaam karne ka tareeka badalne par laa sake, ise secure rakhe, aur bachaye hue ghante dikha sake |

Anaya ne kaha ki chaaron alag saboot maangte hain, isliye ek resume das advertisements ke kaam nahi aa sakta. Phir usne Rhea se ek boring kaam karne ko kaha. Das advertisements ek sheet ke upar likhiye. Left side mein har woh skill likhiye jiska koi bhi zikr karta hai. Jahan koi role kisi skill ko maangta hai, wahan box tick kijiye.

Jab sheet poori ho jaaye, toh sabse zyada ticks wali rows dikhati hain ki market kya chahta hai. Jin rows mein ek bhi nahi, woh shor hain. Yeh sheet ek *role matrix* hai. Har row ke liye Rhea ko ek aur nishaan lagana tha, aur imaandaari se: uske liye woh skill proven thi, partly proven thi, ya missing thi?

Rhea ko teen shaamein lagi. Usne sheet ek note ke saath wapas bheji: usne zyadatar woh kiya tha jo woh maangte the, aur use aise nahi kaha tha jise koi check kar sake. Anaya ne use kaha ki jo usne saabit kiya hai, usi ke roop mein bataye. "Maine das asli users ko ek AI product ship kiya aur uski useful-result rate 58 se 71 percent tak badhayi" ek sentence hai, usne kaha. "AI ke prati passionate" ek mood hai.

## Page par kya jaata hai

Unhone do Sundays mein resume dobara likha, aur tareeka ek sentence ka tha. Har line mein likha ho ki usne kya kiya, kya decide kiya aur kya hua. Table mein do lines before aur after dikhayi gayi hain.

Table: Resume ki lines, pehle aur baad
| Pehle | Baad |
| --- | --- |
| Support teams ke liye ek AI tool par kaam kiya | Support tickets ke liye ek theme-finding tool banaya; har theme ko quote kiye hue phrases par tikane ka decision liya, aur das partner teams mein useful mark hone wale themes ka share 58% se 71% kiya |
| Testing ki zimmedari | 20 pichhle hafton ka ek fixed test set likha aur use do aisi releases rokne mein use kiya jo accuracy kam kar deti |

Anaya ne dhyaan dilaya ki doosri line padhne wale ko batati hai ki Rhea ship karne se pehle quality ke baare mein sochti hai. Padhne wale ko usse yeh bolwane ki zaroorat nahi.

Phir aayi *case study*: kaam ke ek hisse ka ek ya do page ka bayaan. Rhea ki case study chhe sawaalon ka order mein jawaab degi.

1. Problem kya thi, aur kis saboot ne dikhaya ki woh asli thi?
2. Aapne kya decide kiya, aur kya reject kiya?
3. Aapne jaan-boojhkar kya chhoda?
4. Kya hua, numbers mein?
5. Kya galat hua, aur aapne kya badla?
6. Aap aage kya karenge?

Anaya ne kaha ki paanchva sawaal asli kaam karta hai. Safalta koi bhi bata sakta hai, aur log use hire karte hain jise pata ho ki kya toota. Usne chhe nahi, do case studies ki salah di. Unhe wahan hona chahiye jahan recruiter unhe dhoondhega: Rhea ka apna ek chhota page, jo uski profile ke upar se link ho. Profile ki headline mein woh job likhi ho jo Rhea chahti hai aur ek cheez jo usne saabit ki hai. Usmein "lifelong learner" ke baare mein kuch nahi hona chahiye.

## Woh chaar sawaal jo har loop poochhta hai

June ke beech mein unhone interviews shuru kiye. Anaya ne yeh bata kar shuru kiya ki zyadatar interview loops kaise bane hote hain. Chaar kisam ke sawaal hote hain, aur har ek ka ek shape hai jise follow kiya ja sakta hai.

Table: Interview ke chaar kisam ke sawaal
| Kisam | Ek example | Follow karne ka shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product sense | Busy parents ke liye ek product design kijiye | Goal clear kijiye, ek user chuniye, uski problems list kijiye, ek chuniye, solutions sujhaiye, unhe rank kijiye, batayiye ki aap ise kaise naapenge |
| Strategy | Kya hume Indian market mein jaana chahiye? | Goal, market, customer, competitors, hamara advantage, phir ek recommendation aur uske risks |
| Metrics | Daily users 10 percent gir gaye. Kyun? | Check kijiye ki data sahi hai, internal ya external cause, kaun sa segment, kaun sa step, hypotheses, unhe test kaise karein |
| Execution | Do teams ko wahi engineer chahiye. Aap kya karenge? | Har ek ki kya value hai, options, trade-offs, ek decision, logon ko kaise batayenge |

*Product sense* ek user chunne, uski asli problem dhoondhne aur ek samajhdaar solution sujhane ki kabiliyat hai. Yeh logon ko sabse zyada daraata tha, kyunki lagta tha ki isme ek aisi inspiration chahiye jo unke paas nahi hai. Aisa nahi hai. Isme kuch sujhane se pehle ek user chunne ki aadat chahiye, aur ek saal ke kaam ke baad Rhea ki aadat achhi thi.

Anaya ki salah thi ki shuru karne se pehle structure zor se bol dijiye: kuch aisa ki "main clarify karungi, ek user chunungi, problems dhoondhungi aur phir choose karungi." Isse interviewer saath chal sakta hai. Agar woh candidate ko kahin aur chahte hain, toh woh pehle minute mein bol sakte hain, chaalisve mein nahi.

Aur aakhir mein, choose kijiye. Interviewer dekhna chahte hain ki candidate ek decision par tikta hai aur wajah deta hai. Jo log paanch options ginate hain aur ek ki bhi recommendation nahi dete, woh yahan kisi aur cheez se zyada fail hote hain.

Usne us raat ek metrics sawaal ka rehearsal kiya. Daily users 10 percent gir gaye the. Rhea ne yeh poochkar shuru kiya ki kya number khud galat ho sakta hai: ginne ke tareeke mein badlaav, ya ek toota hua event. Anaya ne kaha ki yeh pehla sahi kadam hai, aur zyadatar candidates ise chhod dete hain.

## Bolkar ek system design karna

Naye kisam ki job ka sabse khaas sawaal, aur woh jisse Rhea darti thi, design interview tha. Interviewer kehta hai: hamare product ke liye ek support assistant design kijiye. Yeh *system-design interview* hai, aur ismein koi code nahi maanga jaata. Yeh is baat ka signal maangta hai ki candidate ek AI feature ke baare mein poori tarah soch sakta hai.

Anaya ne use chhe steps diye, aur Rhea ne unhe usi order mein likha jisme woh unhe bolegi.

Table: System-design jawaab ke chhe steps
| Step | Kya kehna hai |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Users aur task | Yeh kiske liye hai, task kya hai, aur success kaisi dikhegi |
| 2. Approach | Akela model, ya model jise jawaab dhoondhne ke liye documents mile, ya ek fixed workflow, ya ek agent, aur kyun |
| 3. Evaluation | Launch se pehle aur baad aapko kaise pata chalega ki yeh kaam kiya |
| 4. Failure | Jab yeh galat, dheema ya attack ho, tab kya hota hai |
| 5. Cost aur delay | Ek task ka mota cost, aur woh delay jo users sah lenge |
| 6. Rollout | Pehle kise, aap kya naapenge, aur ise wapas kab palatenge |

Numbers use kijiye, usne Rhea se kaha, chahe mote hi sahi. Jaise: har sawaal ke liye lagbhag 2,000 words ka context andar aur 300 bahar, toh ek rupaye se kam. Numbers dikhate hain ki candidate ko pata hai ki yeh cheez har baar chalne par paisa leti hai. Anaya ne ek baar ek bahut achhe candidate ko cost ka zikr kabhi na karne ki wajah se job khote dekha tha.

Rhea ne dheere se kaha ki saal bhar ka sab kuch isi ki taiyaari tha. Anaya ne kaha ki woh wahi saal tha.

## Apne baare mein kahaniyan sunana

Aakhri kisam ka sawaal sabse purana hai: mujhe ek aisa waqt bataiye jab aap kisi engineer se asahmat hue. Interviewer candidate ke beete hue kal ke baare mein isliye poochta hai kyunki aage kya hoga, iska woh sabse achha guide hai.

Anaya ne Rhea ko kaha ki asli kaam ki chhe se aath sachchi kahaniyan taiyaar karo, aur har ek ko wahi chaar hisson mein sunao. Log ise *STAR method* kehte hain: situation, task, action aur result. Zyadatar time is par jaata hai ki candidate ne kya kiya, is par nahi ki team ne kya kiya, kyunki interviewer candidate ka interview le raha hai.

Unhone ek kahani par ek ghanta kaam kiya. Woh ek aise decision ke baare mein thi jo galat gaya tha, kyunki aisi kahaniyan sabse achhi hoti hain.

::: example Rhea ki kahani chaar hisson mein
**Situation:** mere tool ke pehle version mein ek agent weekly report ke steps chunta tha. **Task:** use das design partners ke liye kaafi bharosemand banane ki zimmedari meri thi. **Action:** mujhe ummeed thi ki agent unusual hafton ko behtar sambhalega, isliye maine use rakha. Jab mere 20 test hafton mein se teen fail hue, toh maine wahi flow ek fixed workflow ki tarah banaya aur dono ko wahi hafton par chalaya. **Result:** workflow 20 mein ek baar fail hua aur doguna tez tha, isliye maine switch kar liya. Maine seekha ki commit karne se pehle dono options naapne chahiye, aur ab main har bade design choice ke liye yeh karti hoon.
:::

Anaya ne kaha ki yeh achhi kahani hai, kyunki galat decision asli tha, aur sudhaar maafi maangne se nahi, naapne se aaya.

## Akele, aur doosre logon ke saath

Do chhoti baatein bachi theen. Pehli *take-home* hai: employer ka diya hua kaam jo ek bataye hue time mein akele karna hota hai. Time ke andar rahiye. Apni assumptions upar rakhiye, aur ant mein likhiye ki zyada time hota toh aap kya karte. Reviewers lambe jawaab se ek saaf, imaandaar jawaab pasand karte hain. Jo insaan bata deta hai ki usne kya chhoda, us par us se zyada bharosa hota hai jo dikhaye ki kuch chhoota hi nahi.

Doosri networking hai, jo Rhea ko pasand nahi thi. Yeh tab sabse achhi chali jab uske paas dikhane ko kuch tha. Anaya ki salah thi ki logon se referral mat maango. Uske bajaye, ek khaas insaan ko case study bhejiye aur usse ek khaas sawaal poochiye, kyunki woh aisa sawaal hai jiska woh jawaab de sakta hai.

Pay taiyaari ka maamla tha. Pata kijiye ki us shehar mein us role ke liye aam taur par kitna milta hai. Pehla offer company ko dene dijiye. Phir ek baar, tameez se, ek wajah ke saath maangiye, jaise ek aur offer ya kaam ka size.

## Phir kya hua

Yeh batana achha lagta ki Rhea ko mahine ke andar job mil gayi. Nahi mili. Das mein se do companies ne use doosre round ke liye bulaya, jab resume aur case study tay hue tareeke se dobara likhe gaye. July ke teesre hafte tak woh ek kamre mein ek mahila ke saamne baithi thi jisne use company ke product ke liye ek support assistant design karne ko kaha. Rhea ne zor se, thodi kaampti awaaz mein kaha, "Main yahan se shuru karungi ki ise kaun use karta hai aur achha outcome kya hai."

Anaya wahan nahi thi. Use baad mein ek phone call par pata chala, jisme Rhea ne sawaal bataya aur kaha ki beech mein use ehsaas hua ki woh ab rehearse nahi kar rahi thi. Woh kuch aisa samjha rahi thi jo usne banaya tha.

## Saar

"AI product manager" kam se kam chaar alag jobs ka naam hai. Isliye kaam ka pehla kadam yeh hai ki asli job advertisements ko ek role matrix mein rakhein, har skill ko proven, partly proven ya missing mark karein, aur khud ko us par position karein jo aap saabit kar sakte hain.

- Resume ki har line batati hai ki kya kiya gaya, kya decide hua aur kya result aaya. Case study mein reject kiye gaye options, trade-offs, numbers, jo fail hua aur aage kya aata hai, yeh sab jodte hain.
- Interview loops chaar kisam ke sawaal poochte hain: product sense, strategy, metrics aur execution. Har ek ka jawaab zor se ek structure ka naam lekar aur phir ek choice par tik kar diya jaata hai.
- System-design interview approach, evaluation, failure, cost aur rollout maangta hai, mote numbers ke saath bolkar.
- Apne beete hue kal ki kahaniyan chaar hisson mein sunayi jaati hain, zyadatar time apne action par.
- Take-home apni assumptions aur jo woh chhodta hai, woh batata hai. Networking ek khaas sawaal aur kaam ke ek tukde se shuru hoti hai. Negotiation research se shuru hoti hai, aur ek baar maangti hai.
