Rhea Kapoor ne June mein ek Sunday shaam phone kiya. Woh un logon ki tarah shuru hui jinhone maafi rehearse ki ho. Usne maafi ke ant se shuruaat ki. Use pata tha ki yeh Anaya ki akeli khaali shaam hai, usne kaha. Woh use der nahi rokegi.

Anaya Rhea se pichhle autumn mein ek meetup mein mili thi. Wahan Rhea ne speaker se wahi ek sawaal poocha jo koi aur poochne ki himmat nahi karta tha. Rhea ne chhe saal sarkari vibhagon ke liye technology programmes deliver kiye the. Har mahine ek steering committee hoti thi. (Yeh woh bada group hai jo plan manzoor karta hai.) Har Friday ek status report jaati thi. Phir usne ek saal woh sab kiya jo Anaya ne apne saal mein kiya tha, akele aur raat ko.

Usne apna kuch banaya bhi tha. Ek chhota tool, jo customer-support tickets ka ek hafta padhta aur unhe themes mein baant deta. Phir woh report likhta jise support lead ka poora din lagta tha. Das design partners ne use use kiya tha. Par usne use kisi aise ko nahi dikhaya tha jo use job de sake.

Rhea ke saamne das job advertisements khule the. Sab par "AI product manager" likha tha. Use samajh nahi aa raha tha ki woh kisi ke liye bhi kyun theek hai. Anaya ne kaha ki saare das bhejo. Usne yeh bhi poocha ki resume ke upar kya likha hai. Thodi der chup rehkar Rhea ne kaha ki likha hai ki use AI ka junoon hai.

## Case: das advertisements aur ek resume

Neeche ke sections kuch Sunday shaamon ka kaam hain. Yeh book ke ant mein isliye aate hain kyunki yeh pehle ke sab kuch ko use karte hain. Yeh woh skill bhi sikhate hain jo zyadatar log chhod dete hain: dikhana ki aapne kya kiya hai.

## Ek title, chhe jobs

Anaya ne Rhea ko pehli baat batayi. Title ek label hai jo kam se kam chhe alag jobs par chipka hai. Yeh "dukaan" shabd jaisa hai. Dukaan joote bech sakti hai, repair ka bench chala sakti hai, ya chai pila sakti hai. Table chhe jobs dikhata hai, aur har employer apne hire se kya chahta hai.

Table: Ek title ke peeche chhe jobs
| Employer ki kisam | Kya banati hai | Kya chahti hai |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ek maujooda product mein AI features jodti hai | Un users ke liye features jo uske paas pehle se hain | Judgement, ship karne ka record, aur un logon ke saath kaam karne ki kabiliyat jo models banate hain |
| Aisi cheez banati hai jo AI ke bina hoti hi nahi | Ek naya product | Speed, prototyping, aisi cheez ke saath aaram jo kabhi-kabhi fail hoti hai, aur naapne ki aadat |
| Doosri teams ke liye tools, interfaces aur models banati hai | Platforms | Technical depth aur is baat ki samajh ki developer ko kya takleef deta hai |
| Apne hi employees ke liye AI banati hai | Internal tools | Koi jo logon ko unke kaam karne ka tareeka badalne par laa sake, ise secure rakhe, aur bachaye hue ghante dikha sake |
| Ek consulting firm jo clients ke liye AI systems banati hai | Doosre organisations ke projects, aksar bade | Delivery ka anushasan, saaf likhna, evaluation aur governance, aur client ko bata paane ki himmat ki system kya nahi kar sakta |
| Ek public-sector ya digital-public-infrastructure body | Nagrikon ke liye services, aksar kai bhashaon mein aur kabhi awaaz se | Field deployments, Indic bhashayein aur voice, ministry stakeholders, aur open-weight options |

Anaya ne kaha ki chhe jobs alag saboot maangte hain. Isliye ek resume saare das advertisements ke kaam nahi aa sakta. Jisne technology projects deliver kiye hain, uske liye teen darwaaze khule hain. Ek, woh company jo AI par bani hai. Do, ek consulting firm jo clients ke liye AI systems banati hai. Teen, ek public-sector body.

Rhea ke chhe saal teesre ke sabse kareeb the. Phir Anaya ne use ek boring kaam diya. Das advertisements ek sheet ke upar likhiye. Left side mein har skill likhiye jo koi bhi naam leta hai. Jahan role skill maangta hai, wahan tick kijiye.

Jab sheet bhar jaaye, toh sabse zyada ticks wali rows dikhati hain ki market kya chahta hai. Jin rows mein ek bhi tick nahi, woh shor hain. Yeh sheet ek *role matrix* hai. Yeh dukaanon ke saamne tick ki hui kharidari ki list jaisi hai. Phir Rhea ko har row mein ek aur nishaan lagana tha, imaandaari se. Uske liye skill proven thi, partly proven ya missing?

Rhea ko teen shaamein lagi. Usne sheet ek note ke saath wapas bheji. Usne zyadatar woh kiya tha jo woh maangte the. Bas use aise nahi kaha tha ki koi jaanch sake. Anaya ne kaha ki use bataye jo usne saabit kiya hai. "Maine das asli users ko ek AI product ship kiya. Maine uski useful-result rate 58 se 71 percent kar di." Yeh ek sentence hai, usne kaha. "AI ka junoon hai" ek mood hai.

## Page par kya jaata hai

Unhone do Sundays mein resume dobara likha. Tareeka ek sentence ka tha. Har line batati hai ki usne kya kiya, kya decide kiya aur kya hua. Resume ki line dukaan ke price tag jaisi hai. Woh batati hai ki cheez kya hai aur uska daam kya hai. Woh yeh nahi kehti ki dukaan ko "maal ka junoon" hai. Table do lines pehle aur baad dikhata hai.

Table: Resume ki lines, pehle aur baad
| Pehle | Baad |
| --- | --- |
| Support teams ke liye ek AI tool par kaam kiya | Support tickets ke liye ek theme-finding tool banaya; har theme ko quote kiye hue phrases par tikane ka decision liya, aur das partner teams mein useful mark hone wale themes ka share 58% se 71% kiya |
| Testing ki zimmedari | 20 pichhle hafton ka ek fixed test set likha aur use do aisi releases rokne mein use kiya jo accuracy kam kar deti |

Anaya ne dhyaan dilaya ki doosri line padhne wale ko batati hai ki Rhea ship karne se pehle quality ke baare mein sochti hai. Padhne wale ko yeh use kehna nahi padta.

Phir aayi *case study*. Yeh kaam ke ek hisse ki ek ya do page ki kahani hai, seekh ke saath. Rhea ki case study chhe sawaalon ka order mein jawaab degi.

1. Problem kya thi, aur kis saboot ne dikhaya ki woh asli thi?
2. Aapne kya decide kiya, aur kya reject kiya?
3. Aapne jaan-boojhkar kya chhoda?
4. Kya hua, numbers mein?
5. Kya galat hua, aur aapne kya badla?
6. Aap aage kya karenge?

Anaya ne kaha ki paanchva sawaal asli kaam karta hai. Jeet koi bhi bata sakta hai. Log use hire karte hain jise pata ho ki kya toota. Usne chhe nahi, do case studies ki salah di.

Unhe wahan hona chahiye jahan recruiter unhe dhoondhe. Yaani Rhea ka apna ek chhota page, jo uski profile ke upar se link ho. Profile ki headline mein woh job likhi ho jo Rhea chahti hai, aur ek cheez jo usne saabit ki. Usme "umar bhar seekhne wala" jaisa kuch na ho.

## Woh chaar sawaal jo har loop poochhta hai

June ke beech mein unhone interviews shuru kiye. Anaya ne pehle samjhaya ki zyadatar interview loops kaise bante hain. (Loop yaani rounds ka woh silsila jisse company aapko guzarti hai.) Chaar kisam ke sawaal hote hain. Har ek ka ek shape hota hai, recipe ki tarah.

Table: Interview ke chaar kisam ke sawaal
| Kisam | Ek example | Follow karne ka shape |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product sense | Ek state helpline ke liye, do bhashaon mein, ek voice assistant design kijiye | Goal clear kijiye, ek user chuniye, uski problems list kijiye, ek chuniye, solutions sujhaiye, unhe rank kijiye, batayiye ki aap ise kaise naapenge |
| Strategy | Kya hume Indian market mein jaana chahiye? | Goal, market, customer, competitors, hamara advantage, phir ek recommendation aur uske risks |
| Metrics | Daily users 10 percent gir gaye. Kyun? | Check kijiye ki data sahi hai, internal ya external cause, kaun sa segment, kaun sa step, hypotheses, unhe test kaise karein |
| Execution | Do teams ko wahi engineer chahiye. Aap kya karenge? | Har ek ki kya value hai, options, trade-offs, ek decision, logon ko kaise batayenge |

*Product sense* matlab pehle user chunna, phir uski asli problem dhoondhna aur samajhdaar hal sujhana. Yeh khana tay karne se pehle poochne jaisa hai ki mehmaan kaun hai. Isse log sabse zyada darte the, kyunki lagta tha ki ek chingari chahiye jo unke paas nahi. Aisa nahi hai. Bas ek aadat chahiye: kuch sujhane se pehle user chuniye. Ek saal ke kaam ke baad Rhea ki aadat achhi thi.

Usne ek aisi duniya ke prompt par practice ki jo use pata thi. Ek state helpline ke liye, do bhashaon mein, ek voice assistant. Uske pehle teen kadam chhote the. Usne goal saaf kiya. Goal tha kam callers ka intezaar aur kisi scheme ke niyamon par kam galat jawaab, koi chatur assistant nahi.

Usne ek user chuna. Woh ek basic phone wala caller tha. Woh sirf kshetriya bhasha bolta hai aur kaam ke din ke ant mein phone karta hai. Usne koi feature ka naam lene se pehle us caller ki problems likhi. Caller ko nahi pata ki kaun si scheme lagti hai. Caller ko nahi pata ki kaun se kaagaz laane hain. Line kharab ho toh caller ka phone kat jaata hai. Tab jaakar usne bataya ki teeno mein se woh pehle kaun si hal karegi.

Anaya ne use salah di ki shuru karne se pehle structure bolkar bataye. Jaise: "Main goal clear karungi, ek user chunungi, problems dhoondhungi, phir chunungi." Tab interviewer saath chal sakta hai. Agar woh candidate ko kahin aur le jaana chahein, toh pehle minute mein keh sakte hain, chalisve mein nahi.

Aur ant mein, chuniye. Interviewers dekhna chahte hain ki candidate decision par tikta hai aur wajah deta hai. Jo paanch options gina dete hain aur ek bhi recommend nahi karte, woh yahan sabse zyada fail hote hain.

Us raat usne ek metrics sawaal ki practice ki. Daily users das percent gir gaye the. Rhea ne pehle yeh poochkar shuru kiya ki kya number khud galat ho sakta hai. Shayad gintne ka tareeka badla ho. Shayad koi event toot gaya ho. Anaya ne kaha, yeh pehla sahi kadam hai, aur zyadatar candidates ise chhod dete hain.

## Bolkar ek system design karna

Naye tarah ki job ka sabse khaas sawaal design interview tha. Rhea ko isse dar lagta tha. Interviewer kehta hai: hamare product ke liye ek support assistant design kijiye. Yeh *system-design interview* hai. Ismein code nahi maanga jaata. Yeh ghar ka naksha bolkar banane jaisa hai, eent lagana nahi. Ismein is baat ka sanket chahiye ki candidate ek AI feature ko poore roop mein soch sakta hai.

Anaya ne use chhe steps diye. Rhea ne unhe usi order mein likha jisme woh bolegi.

Table: System-design jawaab ke chhe steps
| Step | Kya kehna hai |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Users aur task | Yeh kiske liye hai, task kya hai, aur success kaisi dikhegi |
| 2. Approach | Akela model, ya model jise jawaab dhoondhne ke liye documents mile, ya ek fixed workflow, ya ek agent, aur kyun |
| 3. Evaluation | Launch se pehle aur baad aapko kaise pata chalega ki yeh kaam kiya |
| 4. Failure | Jab yeh galat, dheema ya attack ho, tab kya hota hai |
| 5. Cost aur delay | Ek task ka mota cost, aur woh delay jo users sah lenge |
| 6. Rollout | Pehle kise, aap kya naapenge, aur ise wapas kab palatenge |

Numbers use kijiye, usne Rhea se kaha, mote hi sahi. Jaise: har sawaal ke liye lagbhag do hazaar shabd ka context andar aur teen sau baahar. Yeh ek rupaye se kam hai. Numbers dikhate hain ki candidate jaanta hai ki yeh cheez har baar chalne par paisa kharch karti hai. Anaya ne ek baar ek bahut achhe candidate ko cost ka zikr na karne se job khote dekha tha.

Rhea ne dheere se kaha ki poora saal isi ki taiyaari tha. Anaya ne kaha ki woh wahi saal tha.

## Apne baare mein kahaniyan sunana

Aakhri kisam ka sawaal sabse purana hai: mujhe ek aisi baar batayiye jab aap kisi engineer se sehmat nahi the. Interviewer beete kal ke baare mein isliye poochta hai ki woh aane wale kal ka sabse achha guide hai.

Anaya ne Rhea se kaha ki asli kaam ki chhe se aath sachchi kahaniyan taiyaar rakhe. Har ek ko wahi chaar hisson mein sunana tha. Yeh *STAR method* hai. Yeh chaar khaanon ka form hai: situation, task, action aur result. Zyadatar time is par jaata hai ki candidate ne kya kiya, team ne kya kiya is par nahi. Interviewer candidate ka interview le raha hai.

Unhone ek kahani par ek ghanta kaam kiya. Yeh ek galat gaye decision ke baare mein thi, kyunki wahi sabse achhi kahaniyan bante hain.

::: example Rhea ki kahani chaar hisson mein
**Situation:** mere tool ke pehle version mein ek agent weekly report ke steps chunta tha. **Task:** use das design partners ke liye kaafi bharosemand banane ki zimmedari meri thi. **Action:** mujhe ummeed thi ki agent unusual hafton ko behtar sambhalega, isliye maine use rakha. Jab mere 20 test hafton mein se teen fail hue, toh maine wahi flow ek fixed workflow ki tarah banaya aur dono ko wahi hafton par chalaya. **Result:** workflow 20 mein ek baar fail hua aur doguna tez tha, isliye maine switch kar liya. Maine seekha ki commit karne se pehle dono options naapne chahiye, aur ab main har bade design choice ke liye yeh karti hoon.
:::

Anaya ne kaha ki yeh achhi kahani hai. Galat decision asli tha, aur sudhaar naapne se aaya, maafi maangne se nahi.

## Uske title ka sawaal

Doosre round mein interviewer ne script rakh di aur resume utha liya. Usne kaha ki sarkari vibhagon ke liye chhe saal programme delivery programme management jaisi lagti hai. Woh kyun maane ki Rhea ek product ki owner ban sakti hai?

Anaya ko yeh umeed thi. Unhone ise ek Sunday ko rehearse kiya tha. Jawaab label se behes karna nahi tha. Jawaab tha teen decisions ke naam batana jinke Rhea owner thi. Pehla, usne kya banane se mana kiya. Uska tool ek chat box mein report ke sawaalon ke jawaab de sakta tha. Usne use chhod diya kyunki woh use naap nahi sakti thi.

Doosra, woh threshold jisne ek release roki. Uske fixed test set ne do versions rok liye the. Teesra, woh number jiske liye woh jawabdeh thi. Yeh useful mark hone wale themes ka share tha, jo 58 se 71 percent hua.

Phir Rhea ne apne itihaas ko taakat bana diya. Ek regulated sector mein chhe saal ka matlab audits, data-location rules aur un logon ke sign-offs the jo na keh sakte the. Zyadatar candidates ko yeh reading list se claim karna padta. Usne yeh nahi kaha ki woh product manager thi. Usne kaha ki woh ek outcome ki owner thi, aur woh number dikha sakti thi.

## Jab woh dekh rahe hon aur aap build karein

Kuch loops mein ek round aur hota hai, live build ka. Candidate ek coding assistant ke saath kuch chhota banata hai jabki interviewer dekhta hai. Yeh typing se zyada judgement parakhta hai. Yeh aise cooking round jaisa hai jisme woh saath-saath chakhte hain.

Anaya ki salah book mein pehle aaye verifier idea se thi. Assistant se kuch maangne se pehle bataiye ki aap uska kaam kaise jaanchenge. Jaanch zor se boliye, jaise "result mere das sample messages ke har number ko chhupaye." Chhote steps maangiye.

Jo wapas aaye use padhiye, aur boliye ki aap abhi kya nahi maanenge. Interviewers dekhte hain ki candidate us change ko pehchaan paata hai ya nahi jo chala, aur jo sirf sahi dikhta hai. Adhoora build jiski checks pass hon, poore build se behtar hai jise kisi ne check nahi kiya.

## Akele, aur doosre logon ke saath

Do chhoti baatein baaki thi. Pehli *take-home* hai. Yeh employer ka diya kaam hai, jo akele, tay time mein karna hota hai. Time ka dhyaan rakhiye. Apni assumptions upar likhiye. Ant mein likhiye ki zyada time hota toh aap kya karte. Reviewers lambe jawaab se saaf, imaandaar jawaab ko pasand karte hain. Jo bata deta hai ki usne kya chhoda, us par zyada bharosa hota hai, us se jo dikhata hai ki kuch chhoota hi nahi.

Doosri networking hai, jo Rhea ko pasand nahi thi. Yeh tab sabse achhi chali jab uske paas dikhane ko kuch tha. Anaya ki salah thi ki logon se referral mat maangiye. Ek khaas insaan ko case study bhejiye aur uske baare mein ek khaas sawaal poochiye. Yeh woh sawaal hai jiska woh jawaab de sakte hain.

Pay taiyaari ki baat hai. Pata kijiye ki us shehar mein us role ka aam pay kya hai. Pehla offer company ko dene dijiye. Phir ek baar, tameez se, wajah ke saath maangiye, jaise koi doosra offer ya kaam ka size.

## Phir kya hua

Yeh kehna achha hota ki Rhea ek mahine mein hire ho gayi. Nahi hui. Das mein se do companies ne use doosre round ke liye bulaya. Tab tak resume aur case study tay hisaab se dobara likhe ja chuke the.

July ke teesre hafte tak woh ek kamre mein ek mahila ke saamne baithi thi. Mahila ne use company ke product ke liye ek support assistant design karne ko kaha. Rhea ne zor se, thodi kaanpti awaaz mein kaha, "Main yahan se shuru karungi ki ise kaun use karta hai aur achha outcome kya hai."

Anaya wahan nahi thi. Usne baad mein phone par suna. Rhea ne sawaal bataya. Usne kaha ki beech mein use ehsaas hua ki woh ab rehearse nahi kar rahi. Woh kuch samjha rahi thi jo usne khud banaya tha.

::: key Ab aap kya keh sakte hain
Har N apne project se bhariye.
- Meri case study batati hai ki kya toota, N cheezein, aur woh number jo hila, N se N tak.
- Maine price is hisaab se rakhi ki paisa kaun deta hai: ek message ko serve karne mein N rupaye lagte hain, aur base use ke das guna par bhi margin N percent se upar rehta hai.
- Main ginta hoon ki log kya karte hain, bina yeh rakhe ki unhone kya likha: N measures, aur unmein se koi bhi message store nahi karta.
:::

## Saar

"AI product manager" kam se kam chhe alag jobs ka naam hai. Isliye pehla kaam ka kadam yeh hai ki asli job advertisements ko ek role matrix mein rakhein. Har skill ko proven, partly proven ya missing mark kijiye. Phir khud ko us par position kijiye jo aap saabit kar sakte hain.

- Resume ki har line batati hai ki kya kiya gaya, kya decide hua aur kya result aaya. Case study mein reject kiye gaye options, trade-offs, numbers, jo fail hua aur aage kya, yeh sab bhi aate hain.
- Interview loops chaar kisam ke sawaal poochte hain: product sense, strategy, metrics aur execution. Har ek ka jawaab pehle structure bolkar dijiye, phir ek choice par tikiye.
- System-design interview approach, evaluation, failure, cost aur rollout maangta hai, mote numbers ke saath bolkar.
- Agar aapka resume programme management jaisa padhta hai, toh un teen decisions ke saath jawaab dijiye jinke aap owner the, aur ek number. Label par behes mat kijiye. Live build round mein, assistant se kaam maangne se pehle jaanch bataiye.
- Apne beete kal ki kahaniyan chaar hisson mein sunaiye, zyadatar time apne action par.
- Take-home apni assumptions aur jo usne chhoda woh batata hai. Networking ek khaas sawaal aur kaam ke ek tukde se shuru hoti hai. Negotiation research se shuru hoti hai, aur ek baar maangti hai.
