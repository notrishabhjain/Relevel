Anaya ke pehle manager ne use aise plan banana sikhaye the jo railway timetable jaise dikhte the: ek taraf features, upar mahine, aur har feature ke liye ek bar jo ek tareekh par shuru aur doosri par khatam hota. Usne aise lagbhag ek darjan banaye the. Use yaad nahi tha ki unmein se koi sach hua ho.

Tareekhein aksar galat hoti thi, par yahi sabse badi kami nahi thi. Har bar kuch banane ka vaada tha, aur kisi bar ne yeh vaada nahi kiya tha ki woh banana madad karega. Jab tareekhein khisakti thi, plan toot-te vaadon ki list ban jaata. Jab koi feature maayne nahi rakhta tha, woh galat cheez ke toot-te vaadon ki list ban jaata. Founders ke guard ke tool ko principle mein maanne ke baad Anaya ne ek saaf page par alag dhaancha shuru kiya.

Yeh chapter us dhaanche ko, usse jude lakshyon ko, un logon ko jo use rok sakte the, aur ek aisi meeting ko samjhata hai jisne use shuru hone se pehle parkha.

## Case: bina tareekhon ka plan

Pichhle chapter ki strategy ek vaakya thi. Use engineers, compliance staff aur founders ke liye karne laayak banane ke liye kaam ka ek kram chahiye tha, yeh jaanne ka tareeka ki kaam safal hua ya nahi, aur yeh ki kaun use rok sakta hai. Neeche ke section in teen zaroorton ko kram se dekhte hain, aur ek chauthi ko bhi: ek meeting jisme saathiyon se kaha gaya ki maan lo project pehle hi fail ho chuka hai.

## Outcome roadmap

Anaya ne jo *roadmap* apnaya usme teen column hain aur calendar nahi hai. *Now* mein woh kaam hai jo team kar rahi hai, aur woh pakka vaada hai. *Next* mein woh hai jo woh uske baad karne ki ummeed rakhti hai, jo sambhav hai par badal sakta hai. *Later* mein woh hai jo woh tatol rahi hai, maujooda soch ke hisaab se kram mein, aur kisi se vaada nahi. Har cheez ek nateeje ke roop mein likhi jaati hai, logon ke kuch kar sakne mein badlaav, feature ke roop mein nahi, aur uske neeche kuch line ka byora hota hai.

Table: Pehli cheez ke liye Anaya ki roadmap entry
| Field | Entry |
| --- | --- |
| Nateeja | Support staff ko chat mein pehchaan ya tax ke numbers nahi dikhte, aur exports mein bhi nahi |
| Saboot | Ek hafte mein 400 mein se 37 conversations; paanch baatcheetein |
| Maalik | Anaya aur Imran |
| Kis par nirbhar | Chat system ka read access; Lakshmi ki manzoori |
| Kill criterion | Agar chaar hafton mein agents das mein se do se zyada chats mein ise band kar dein toh ruk jaao |

Aakhri line uske liye nayi thi. *Kill criterion* ek aisi shart hai jo pehle se tay ki jaati hai ki kaam kab rokna hai. Yeh ek manovaigyanik wajah se kaam karta hai. Teen hafte ki mehnat ke baad koi bhi yeh tay karne ke liye sahi insaan nahi hota ki kaam kharab chal raha hai, kyunki sab ke paas kehne ki wajah hoti hai ki sab theek hai. Shuru mein likhi hui shart, jab koi natije se judaa nahi hota, asafalta ko aise shabdon mein batati hai jinpar baad mein behas nahi karni padti.

Kill criteria tab aur zyaada zaroori ho jaate hain jab kaam aise software ka ho jo text padhta aur likhta hai. Uski sahi hone ki dar pehle se nahi jaani ja sakti, kyunki woh software ko aazma kar hi pata chalti hai. Pehle test se pehle di gayi tareekh ek andaaza hai jo vaade ki tarah pesh kiya gaya hai.

## Lakshya aur unke neeche ke number

Lakshmi ka sawaal ab bhi Anaya ke desk par ek sticky note mein tha: woh kaise jaanegi ki tool kaam karta hai? Companies is tarah ke sawaal ka jawaab *objectives and key results* se deti hain, jise aam taur par *OKR* kehte hain. Objective shabdon mein kehta hai ki kya chahiye. Uske neeche ke *key results* teen-chaar number hote hain jo dikhate hain ki woh haasil hua.

::: key Nateeje, output nahi
Key result ek nateeja naapta hai, logon ke liye kya badla, output nahi, jo ship kiya gaya. "Privacy tool release karo" ek output hai: yeh ho sakta hai aur kuch na badle. "Kam conversations personal detail ke saath chat se nikalti hain" ek nateeja hai.
:::

Anaya ne ek objective aur do key results likhe. Objective tha ki Sahaj ki support chat ko export karne ke liye surakshit banana. Pehla key result tha ki jin conversations mein chat se nikalte waqt koi unhidden personal detail nahi bachi, unka hissa 91 percent se 99 percent tak badhega. Doosra tha ki support agents report karenge ki pachaas mein se ek se kam chat mein tool ne woh chhupaya jo unhe chahiye tha.

Key results ke saath do aur tarah ke number chalte hain. *Input metric* woh hai jise team seedhe hila sakti hai aur jiske hilne se nateeje ke hilne ki ummeed hai. Uske liye woh tha fixed-shape numbers ka woh hissa jo tool ne pakda: nateeje ko order nahi diya ja sakta, par input ko behtar kiya ja sakta hai. *Guardrail metric* woh number hai jo doosron ke peeche bhaagte waqt kharab nahi hona chahiye. Woh aise jeetne se rokta hai jo nuksaan kare. Uska tha false-alarm rate, jo chhupaye gaye cheezon mein un cheezon ka hissa hai jo personal thi hi nahi. Jo tool har message ka har number blank kar de woh pehle key result par poora score karega aur kisi kaam ka nahi hoga, isliye usne seema sau mein teen rakhi.

Ek aur antar technical kaam hone par aasaani se kho jaata tha. Tool ka apna score apni jaanch par hoga, jo tool ko naapta hai. Key results naapte hain ki logon ke saath kya hota hai. Tool apni jaanch mein achha kar sakta hai aur phir bhi agents use ignore kar sakte hain kyunki woh unhe chidhaata hai, ya itni der se aaye ki farak na pade. Dono tarah ke number chahiye the, aur har ek par label lagana tha ki woh kaun sa hai.

## Jo "na" keh sakte hain

Imran ne poochha ki plan sach mein kaun padhega. Anaya ko nahi pata tha. Jo bhi project ki madad kar sakta hai ya use rok sakta hai, ya jisse woh prabhavit hoga, woh *stakeholder* hai, aur stakeholders barabar nahi hote. Usne ek chaukor banaya jisme do axes the, ek insaan ke paas project par kitni taakat hai aur doosra use kitni parwaah hai, aur usme naam rakhe.

Table: Taakat aur ruchi ke hisaab se stakeholders
| | Kam parwaah | Bahut parwaah |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Uspar taakat | Santusht rakho: founders, Lakshmi | Nazdeek se kaam karo: Imran, support head |
| Kam taakat | Soochit rakho: doosri product teams | Shaamil rakho: Farah ke agents, customers |

Har naam ke paas usne yeh bhi likha ki us insaan ko kis par naapa jaata hai, jisse pata chalta hai ki woh kaise pratikriya dega, aur woh kya tay kar sakta hai: chun sakta hai, salah de sakta hai ya veto kar sakta hai.

Lakshmi galat kone mein thi. Uske paas asli taakat thi aur, jahan tak Anaya dekh sakti thi, kam ruchi. Yeh sabse khatarnaak jodi hai, kyunki security, legal aur compliance ke staff ke paas aksar veto hota hai jabki woh rozmarra ke kaam mein kam hissa lete hain. Woh launch se ek din pehle tak chup rehte hain, aur phir "na" kehte hain, aur aam taur par woh sahi hote hain. Upaay saadharan tha. Anaya ne use doosre column mein hafte mein ek baar milkar aur poochhkar rakha ki use kya chahiye.

## Decision memo

Founders plan nahi padhenge. Woh memo ka pehla paragraph padhenge aur shayad doosra. *Decision memo* aise likha jaata hai ki vyast insaan sirf uski shuruaat padhkar kaam kar sake. Kram hai maang, wajah, kharcha, sabse bada risk, aur vikalp, kuch na karna bhi.

::: example Anaya ka pehla paragraph
Main Imran ka aadha samay aath hafte ke liye aur cloud mein lagbhag chaalis hazaar rupaye maang rahi hoon, ek chhota tool banane ke liye jo hamari support chat mein personal details ko aage jaane se pehle chhupa de. Ek hafte ki chats mein 400 mein se 37 conversations mein pehchaan ka number, tax number, bank account ya pata tha, aur kuch bhi use hamare logs, exports ya bahari company ke systems se nahi hatata. Sabse bada risk yeh hai ki tool Hinglish mein likhi cheezein chhod sakta hai. Hum ise live chat par chalane se pehle asli udaharanon par test karenge, aur agar agents das mein se do se zyada chats mein ise band karte hain toh ruk jaayenge.
:::

Paragraph chaar vaakyon mein maang, saboot, mukhya risk aur rukne ki shart batata hai.

## Fail ho chuke project ki kalpana

Founders ki meeting se pehle Anaya ne ek jamavda kiya jisse Imran darta tha aur Farah ko achha laga. Sabko maan lena tha ki chhe mahine baad ka waqt hai aur project fail ho chuka hai, aur alag-alag, bina baat kiye, likhna tha ki kyun. *Pre-mortem* isliye kaam karta hai ki log kalpanik asafalta mein un risks ke naam lete hain jo woh us plan ke khilaaf nahi uthate jiska samarthan karne ki unse ummeed hoti hai.

Farah ne likha ki agents ne tool band kar diya kyunki usne baatcheet ke beech mein ek customer ka callback number chhupa diya aur kisi ne nahi bataya ki use wapas kaise laayein. Lakshmi ne likha ki usne Hinglish mein likha ek pata chhod diya, ek screenshot export mein chala gaya, aur ek regulator ne poochha ki yeh kaise hua. Imran ne likha ki usne har reply mein ek second jod diya aur customers ne shikayat ki ki chatbot dheema lagta hai. Anaya ne likha ki test examples ki file kabhi taza nahi rakhi gayi, isliye koi nahi bata sakta tha ki chautha version teesre se behtar tha ya nahi.

Table: Pre-mortem se nikli risk list
| Risk | Sambhavna | Nuksaan | Maalik | Jawaab |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Agents ise band kar dete hain | Madhyam | Zyada | Farah | Agents ko dikhao ki woh kya chhupata hai aur use wapas kaise dekhein |
| Hinglish pate chhod deta hai | Zyada | Zyada | Anaya | Launch se pehle asli Hinglish par test karo; agar fail ho toh scope chhota karo |
| Replies dheeme karta hai | Madhyam | Madhyam | Imran | Pehle hafte se hi jodi gayi der naapo |
| Test file puraani ho jaati hai | Zyada | Madhyam | Anaya | Ek insaan ki zimmedaari; har badlaav par update |

List ke saath Anaya ne ek chhoti list rakhi un cheezon ki jo use doosron se chahiye thi. *Dependency* woh hai jo project ko team ke bahar se chahiye: kisi aur ka kaam, supplier ka sahyog, data tak pahunch ya koi manzoori. Plans dependencies mein hi khisakte hain, kyunki team ka un par koi niyantran nahi hota. Uski teen thi: chat system ka read access, kuch live jaane se pehle Lakshmi ki manzoori, aur bahari company ka vaada ki uska software text lene ka tareeka nahi badlega. Pehla use Friday tak mil sakta tha. Teesre ke baare mein woh kam pakki thi.

## Nateeja

Founders ne memo par gyarah minute lagaye. Unhone poochha ki kitna samay lagega, kitna kharcha aayega, aur kya Lakshmi maan gayi hain. Anaya ne teesre sawaal ka jawaab pehle diya. Faisla us log mein gaya jo usne pehli raat shuru kiya tha, uske neeche ek nayi line ke saath: agar Imran ka aadha samay teesre hafte ke baad nahi mil paaya toh woh apni raay badal degi.

Aisa plan jis par doosre bharosa kar sakein, woh nahi hota jo aatmavishwaas se sunayi de. Woh woh hota hai jo unhe pehle hi bata de ki woh kaise fail ho sakta hai.

## Saaraansh

Nateeje ke Now, Next aur Later column wala roadmap tareekhon par features ke vaade wale roadmap se kam tootta hai, aur shuru mein likhi hui kill criterion rukna aasaan banati hai.

- Objective lakshya ko shabdon mein batata hai, aur key results naapte hain ki logon ke liye kya badla. Input metric woh number hai jise team seedhe hila sakti hai, aur guardrail metric kharab nahi hona chahiye. Tool ka apna test score key result se alag cheez hai.
- Stakeholders mein taakat aur ruchi alag hoti hai. Jis insaan ke paas taakat ho aur ruchi na ho, woh project ko der se rokne ke sabse zyada sambhavna wala hai.
- Decision memo maang, saboot, mukhya risk aur rukne ki shart se shuru hota hai.
- Pre-mortem sabse pehle asafalta ki kalpana karwata hai, aur uska nateeja maalikon ke saath ek risk list hota hai. Dependencies, jinpar team ka niyantran nahi, alag likhi jaati hain.
