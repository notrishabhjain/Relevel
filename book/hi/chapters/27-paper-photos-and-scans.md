Saal ke pehle chhe mahine, Anaya ke dimaag mein customer ke message ki tasveer phone par kuch lines ke text ki thi.

Yeh ek aisi tasveer thi jise usne kabhi jaancha nahi tha. Woh transcripts se aayi thi, jo apni prakriti se text hote hain, aur answer key se, jo vaakyon ka ek column thi. Usne ab tak jo kuch banaya tha, har rule aur har test, ek shaant assumption par tika tha: ki kisi ne pehle hi customer ke shabdon ko characters mein badal diya hai.

July ke ek Friday ko Farah ne uske saamne kaagaz ka ek tukda rakha jo bilkul vaakya jaisa nahi dikhta tha.

Woh ek photograph tha. Ek customer ne rasoi ki mez par ek bank passbook ki tasveer kheenchi thi, thodi tedhi, ek kone par khidki ki roshni ke saath, aur use chatbot ko bheja tha *account number check karo* shabdon ke saath. Usme kuch bhi text nahi tha. Woh pixels ka ek grid tha jo, ek insaan ko, naam, account number aur branch wali table jaisa dikhta tha.

"Humein aise bahut milte hain," Farah ne kaha.

"Bahut kitne?"

"Mujhe nahi pata," Farah ne kaha. "Maine socha tumhein jaanna hoga."

## Random pachaas

Usne samajhdaari ka kaam kiya. Usne Farah se pichhle mahine ki chats se pachaas attachments yaadrichhik tareeke se nikalne ko kaha. Dilchasp wale nahi. Woh nahi jo ek utsahi saathi chunta. Pachaas jo ek number generator ne chune, asli traffic se, ek Saturday subah garmi se bachne ke liye aadhe band shutters ke saath ek-ek karke dekhe gaye.

Chhaantna bayaan karne mein saral hai aur chupchaap zaroori. Un documents par nirbhar kisi bhi project se pehle, teen numbers hone chahiye. Documents ka kitna hissa saaf digital text hai jise jaisa hai waisa padha ja sakta hai? Kitna scanned ya photographed hai, jise pehle text mein badalna padega? Aur logon ko jo jaankari chahiye uska kitna hissa tables, forms aur layouts ke andar band hai, jahan matlab is par nirbhar hai ki kaun sa dibba kiske bagal mein hai? Aisa karna *document audit* hai. Isme ek dopahar lagti hai. Lagbhag koi nahi karta.

Unhone pachaas ko dheron mein baanta.

| Woh kya tha | Ginti |
| --- | --- |
| Saaf digital files, jaise bank se download kiya statement | 12 |
| Cards, cheques aur passbooks ki photos | 31 |
| Doosre apps ke screenshots | 7 |

Pachaas mein se ikatees, chhe se thoda zyada mein se das, photographs the. Aur unme se lagbhag sab mein, bilkul pehli line mein, wahi kism ki detail thi jise dhoondhne ke liye guard bana tha.

"Yahi woh hissa hai jo maine galat kiya," Anaya ne kaha. "Maine April mein *photographs* ko *Could* ke neeche likha tha. Cards ki photos. Kyunki mujhe laga ki woh durlabh hain."

"Tumhare paas number nahi tha."

"Nahi." Usne ek pencil uthayi. "Mere paas ek ehsaas tha."

Usne line ko *Could* se *Should* mein sarka diya. Ek aise document mein jo bahut had tak vishwas bhari chhaapon ki failure ke baare mein tha, yeh ek chhoti nijee jeet thi, aur usne uske bagal mein wajah saaf shabdon mein likhi. *Ek random namoone ka baasath percent. 12 July ko jaancha gaya.*

## Kadam se pehle ka kadam

Ek tasveer ko koi rule nahi padh sakta. Kisi ko pehle use text mein badalna padta hai, aur us kisi ko *OCR* kehte hain, optical character recognition. Yeh software hai jo likhawat ki tasveer ko dekhta hai aur jo characters use dikhte hain woh lautata hai. Yeh bahut achha hai. Yeh poora nahi hai, aur jo galtiyan yeh karta hai woh ek khaas, dhokha dene wale kism ki hain.

Imran ne dher mein se ek card liya, ek dummy Aadhaar card jo building mein ek designer ne ek gadhe hue number ke saath banaya tha, aur use apne phone se thodi tirchhi dasha mein photograph kiya. Usne photograph ko reader se guzaara aur nateeja screen par rakha.

Card par number tha 4321 5678 9012. Jo text wapas aaya usne kaha **432l 5678 9O12**.

Anaya ne use teen baar padha. Ek chhota akshar *l* jahan ank ek hona chahiye tha. Ek bada *O* jahan zero hona chahiye tha.

"Reader ne unhe galat samjha," usne kaha.

"Zaroor. Kai typefaces mein ek aur *l* lagbhag ek jaise hote hain. Waise hi *O* aur zero. Software shapes se andaaza lagata hai. Aur tumhein kya lagta hai pattern checker ise kya karta hai?"

Usne use dekha jaise woh bola. Rule barah ank dhoondhta tha. Yeh das ank aur do akshar the. Woh use pehchaanta nahi, aur asli number seedha paas se nikal jaata, ek bilkul saaf record ke saath jo dikhata ki rule ko kuch galat nahi dikha.

"Yeh spelling ki galti hai, lekin ek number mein."

"PAN mein wahi problem doosri disha mein hai. PAN ke pehle paanch sthaanon mein akshar aur agle chaar mein ank hote hain. Agar reader akshar ki jagah ank rakh de, toh shape ab PAN nahi rehti."

Ilaaj sadharan tha. Kisi number ko dekhne se pehle rule ke, ek chhota kadam milti-julti shaklon ko wapas badal sakta tha, is samajh ke saath ki jahan sirf ank allowed hain wahan *O* ek zero hai, *l* ek ek hai, *S* ek paanch hai aur *B* ek aath hai. Yeh woh cheez thi jise niyamon mein daalne ka kisi ne socha nahi tha, kyunki phone par type karne wala koi bhi yeh galti nahi karta. Sirf ek machine jo photograph padhti hai karti hai. Usne answer key mein rows ka ek parivaar joda, yaad dilane ke liye naam ke saath: *scanned, lookalikes ke saath.* Yeh chautha version tha, aur images se bana pehla.

## Ek page padhne ke do tareeke

Imran ne vikalp banaye.

Aap page ko pehle text mein badal sakte hain, aur phir text par kaam kar sakte hain. Yeh sasta hai. Isse aap ab tak jo kuch banaya hai woh sab istemaal kar sakte hain: kaatna, niyam, search. Lekin yeh aksar sanrachna ko nasht kar deta hai. Teen column ki table dekhne wala reader aksar characters ki ek sapaat line banata hai. Har value bach jaati hai. Kaun si rakam kis row ki hai, yeh kho jaata hai.

Ya aap kisi saksham model ko page ki tasveer hi de sakte hain. Yeh prati page zyada mehnga hai, lekin yeh layout rakhta hai, aur ek table ya form ke baare mein sawaal ka jawaab de sakta hai. Woh kabhi-kabhi code galat padh leta hai, aur use us page ki taraf ishaara karne ka tareeka chahiye jo usne dekha.

Bahut se systems dono karte hain. Woh search ke liye text nikaalte hain, aur model ko tasveer dikhate hain jab sawaal ek table ya form ke baare mein ho.

"Pehle tareeke mein ek shaant khatra hai," Imran ne kaha. "Maan lo ek table sapaat ho gayi, aur sawaal ek column ke ek number ke baare mein hai. Search kaam karta hai. Model saaf jawaab deta hai. Woh ek number quote karta hai, galat column se. Aage koi nahi pakad sakta, kyunki aage kabhi page dekhta hi nahi."

Anaya ne passbook ki photograph phir dekhi. Naam, account number, branch code. Agar reader ne unhe galat kram mein rakha hota, toh woh nahi bata paati ki kaun sa number kaun sa hai. Guard dono chhupa deta, jo surakshit hota, ya dono mein se koi nahi, jo nahi hota.

## Aur phir awaaz hai

Dopahar ke ant mein Farah ne doosri baat kahi. Kuch customers ne voice notes bhejna shuru kiya tha. Call centre ek voice assistant ke baare mein pooch raha tha. Anaya ne dono ko *Won't* list mein rakha tha, aur usne, khud ke prati nishpaksh hone ke liye, poochha ki kyun.

"Kyunki awaaz mukhya seema badal deti hai," Imran ne kaha. "Text mein do second ka intezaar theek hai. Bolne mein, do second ki khamoshi baatcheet tod deti hai aur log ek doosre ke upar bolne lagte hain. Poora round trip is mein fit hona chahiye: awaaz andar, sochna, awaaz bahar. Aur garmiyon ki sochne wali machines, niyam se, bahut dheemi hain."

Awaaz, usne joda, mukhya roop se accuracy ka sawaal nahi hai. Yeh is baare mein hai ki insaan ne kab bolna khatam kiya, agar woh beech mein kaat de toh kya karna hai, system ke kaam karte waqt kya kehna hai. Yeh ek alag problem aur ek alag project tha. *Won't* wahin raha jahan tha.

## Kya badla

Us raat board par, purani laal line ke bagal mein aur nayi row ke neeche, Anaya ne likha ki audit ne kya kharcha kiya aur kya kharida.

Isme ek dopahar, pachaas files aur bahut saari chai lagi thi. Isne photographs ko *Could* se *Should* mein sarkaya, niyamon ko look-alikes ke baare mein sikhaya, aur use agli baar ke liye ek thumb-rule diya: kisi bhi aise project se pehle jo documents par nirbhar hai, saaf wale gino.

"Yeh project ko chhe hafte baad fail hone se bachane ka sabse sasta tareeka hai," usne kaha.

"Yeh sabse upekshit bhi hai," Imran ne kaha. "Mujhse yeh kabhi kisi ne nahi maanga."

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Asli documents saaf text se kahin zyada baar pages aur tasveerein hote hain. Tasveer ko characters mein badalne wale kadam ko OCR kehte hain, aur uski galtiyan ek khaas kism ki hoti hain, jaise zero ki jagah ek O ya ek ki jagah ek l, jo ek pehchaan ke number ko seedhe ek aise rule ke paas se nikaal sakti hain jo ank dhoondhta hai. Tables us kadam ki sabse shaant haani hain, kyunki values bach jaati hain aur unke beech ke sambandh nahi. Ek page ko pehle text mein badla ja sakta hai, jo sasta hai, ya ek saksham model ko tasveer ke roop mein dikhaya ja sakta hai, jo uska layout rakhta hai, aur bahut se systems dono karte hain. Awaaz mukhya seema ko accuracy se samay mein badal deti hai. Aur kisi bhi document project ke shuru hone se pehle, ek document audit, jo random chune gaye pachaas files aur teen ginti hai, aapko batati hai ki aap asal mein kis cheez se nipat rahe hain.
