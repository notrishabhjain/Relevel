Imran ne printout waise hi padha jaise woh sab kuch padhta tha: neeche se upar ki taraf, aur pencil ko sigaret ki tarah pakde hue.

Sahaj ka conference room kaanch ka ek dibba tha, jisme ek whiteboard tha jis par koi bharosa nahi karta tha, kyunki us par do saal purani kisi meeting ke ek diagram ka dhundhla bhoot ab bhi baaki tha. Anaya ne printout mez par rakha tha, aur uske saath saintees peele flags, har conversation ke liye ek. Imran ki chai, jo usne laane ka waada kiya tha aur laaya bhi, uski kohni ke paas thandi hoti rahi aur woh ek-ek flag dekhta raha, kuch bole bina.

"Yeh asli hai," usne aakhir mein kaha.

"Mujhe pata hai asli hai. Maine gina hai."

"Nahi, main keh raha hoon ki yeh asli hai." Usne ek flag par ungli maari. "Yeh ek insaan hai jisne cheque ki photo bheji. Kisi ne cheque maanga nahi tha. Bas bhej diya. Chatbot mein attachments sambhalne wala hissa maine likha hai, aur maine kabhi socha hi nahi ki log usme kya daalenge." Usne upar dekha. "Toh tum mujhse kya poochh rahi ho?"

Anaya ne jawaab aate hue raaste mein practise kiya tha. Raaste mein woh behtar lag raha tha. "Main poochh rahi hoon ki isko theek karne ke liye kuch banana achha idea hai ya nahi. Aur main chahti hoon ki tum mujhe bataao ki yeh achha idea kyun nahi hai."

Isse woh muskuraya, aaj subah pehli baar. "Achha hai. Kyunki is company ko maine chaar aisi cheezein banate dekha hai jinhe kisi ne use nahi kiya. Mujhe paanchvi ke baare mein pakka hona hai."

## Woh reminder jo kisi ko mila hi nahi

Usne use reminders ki kahani sunayi.

Dedh saal pehle Sahaj ne tay kiya tha ki customers bill bharna bhool jaate hain. Sach tha, aur is wajah se unhe late fees lagti thi, jo customers ko pasand nahi thi aur company bhi zyadatar lena nahi chahti thi. Ek chhoti team ne ek feature banaya tha jo bill ki due date se do din pehle reminder bhejta tha. Kaam dhyaan se kiya gaya tha. Wording ek darjan customers par test ki gayi thi. Messages theek waqt par pahunchte the. Imran ne khud check kiya tha ki woh aadhi raat mein na chalein.

Launch ke teen mahine baad, sau mein se do se bhi kam customers ne use on kiya tha.

"Hum sawaal hi sahi nahi pooch paaye," usne kaha. "Humne poochha, 'kya reminder achha hai?' aur woh tha. Humein poochna chahiye tha, 'kya kisi ko pata bhi hai ki yeh hai?' Switch settings mein tha, teen screens neeche. Settings mein koi kabhi jaata hi nahi tha."

Anaya ne yeh vaakya likh liya kyunki lagta tha ki aisi cheez use dobara kaam aayegi. *Jo feature kisi ko mile hi nahi, uska asar waisa hi hota hai jaise kisi ne use banaya hi nahi.*

Imran ne jo bataya tha woh teen hisson ka ek chakkar tha, aur har product is chakkar mein ghoomta hai, chahe use banane waalon ne dhyaan diya ho ya nahi. Pehla hissa hai *discovery*: yeh pata lagana ki problem asli hai ya nahi, kiske liye, aur kitni badi. Doosra hai *delivery*: cheez ko achhe se banana, jiska matlab hai plan, likhi hui specification, code aur checking. Teesra hai *distribution*: logon ko use dhoondhne, aazmane aur baar-baar aane ke liye taiyaar karna. Reminder discovery aur delivery ki jeet tha aur distribution ki haar, aur us haar ne baaki sab ko bekaar kar diya. Products aksar teeno mein se sabse kamzor hisse par dam todte hain. Yeh chakkar isliye zaroori hai ki teesre stage mein jo seekhte hain, kaun ruka aur kaun gaya, woh wapas pehle stage ko khilata hai.

## Fail hone ke chaar tareeke

"Theek hai," Imran ne kaha. "Dekhte hain yeh chaar mein se kisse marta hai."

Woh whiteboard ki taraf mudaa, ek pen dhoondha jo chalta tha, aur upar chaar shabd likhe. Anaya ne wahi chaar shabd ek kitaab mein dekhe the, lekin kisi ko unhe hathiyaar ki tarah istemaal karte nahi dekha tha.

*Value. Usability. Feasibility. Viability.*

"Ek chuno," usne kaha, "aur apne aap par sakht ho jao."

Usne value se shuru kiya, kyunki woh pehle aata tha aur baaki se use ghabrahat thi. Kya koi ise, jo woh abhi karte hain uske bajaye, chunega? Aaj koi kuch bhi nahi kar raha tha. Yahi dikkat thi. Farah ki team kabhi-kabhi chat mein koi number dekh leti thi aur export mein haath se use mita deti thi, jab unhe yaad aata, aur zyadatar unhe yaad nahi aata tha. "Kuch nahi karna" bhi ek competitor hai, aur hairaan karne wala mazboot. Log aadat badalne se pehle bahut kuch jhel lete hain. Lekin Anaya ke paas saintees flags the aur Imran ka apna reaction, aur use kaafi yakeen tha ki value tik jayegi.

Usability ajeeb thi. "Ise chalayega kaun?" Imran ne poochha. Customers nahi; woh ise kabhi dekhenge hi nahi. Farah ki team iska asar dekhegi, yahan-wahan blur kiya hua ek number, ek note ki kuch chhupaya gaya hai. Engineers ko ise jodna hoga. Imaandaar jawaab yeh tha ki iski apni koi screen lagbhag hogi hi nahi, yaani jo log ise chalayenge woh wahi honge jo iske aas-paas cheezein banayenge, aur unke liye usability ka matlab buttons se bilkul alag kuch tha. Usne likha: *Kya koi engineer jo mujhse kabhi mila nahi, Friday dopahar ise mujhe message kiye bina jod sakta hai?*

Feasibility woh thi jisse woh bachti aa rahi thi. "Kya hum ise sach-much bana sakte hain? Kitne time mein, kaun se hunar se, kaun se data se, kitne paise se?" Woh ruki. "Chaar-chaar ke groups mein barah ank, haan. Mujhe lagta hai woh main khud likh sakti hoon. English akshron mein type kiya hua Hindi naam? Mujhe koi andaaza nahi."

"Yeh bahut kaam ka vaakya hai," Imran ne kaha. "'Mujhe koi andaaza nahi.' Ise likh lo. Yeh sabse mehnga kism ka idea hota hai."

Viability sabse aakhir mein aayi, aur mez par pencil ki ek thapak ke saath. "Paisa kaun dega? Har baar chalne par kitna kharcha hoga? Aur Lakshmi kya kehti hai?"

Is kaarobaar mein in chaaron ko *product risks* kehte hain. Inmein se koi bhi ek akela product ko doba sakta hai, aur teams usi ko jaanchne mein achhi hoti hain jo unhe pasand hota hai. Bahut se fail hue features us test mein paas ho gaye the jis par team ka dhyaan tha, aur us mein fail ho gaye jise unhone chhod diya tha. Jo machine text padhti aur likhti hai, usme feasibility ke saath ek extra sawaal juda hota hai jo aam software mein nahi hota: kya woh itni baar sahi hai? Jo tool paanch mein se chaar baar sahi ho, woh kisi ke janamdin ke card ke liye title sujhane mein kamaal hai. Lekin building ke us ek kaam ke liye woh aafat hai jahan ek baar ki galti kisi customer ki bachat chheen sakti hai. Wahi accuracy ek kaam ke liye kaafi ho sakti hai aur doosre ke liye bekaar, aur tool mein kuch aisa nahi hota jo batata ho ki kaun sa kaam kaisa hai.

Ek doosri keemat bhi thi, jo Anaya ne aadhi dekhi thi aur ab saaf dikhi. Aam feature ki har click par lagbhag kuch kharcha nahi hota. Jo feature text padhta aur likhta hai, uski har baar chalne par asli paisa lagta hai. Isliye viability sirf is par nahi tikti ki log ise use karenge ya nahi, balki is par bhi ki kitni baar, aur unse kya charge kiya ja sakta hai.

## Kaun paisa deta hai, kaun chalata hai, kaun mana kar sakta hai

"Ek aur baat," Imran ne kaha, "kyunki yeh sab kuch badal deti hai aur koi batata nahi."

Usne do dibbe banaye. Pehle mein usne likha *customers Sahaj ko paisa dete hain*. Doosre mein, *companies ek supplier ko paisa deti hain*.

Usne bataya ki Sahaj individuals ko bechta tha: bijli ka bill bharti ek aurat, loan ke liye apply karta ek aadmi. Yeh *B2C* hai, business to consumer. Jo paisa deta hai wahi app chalata hai, aur woh minuton mein faisla karta hai, zyadatar is par ki use aasaan lag raha hai ya nahi. Agar Anaya ne privacy tool sirf Sahaj ke apne chatbot ke liye banaya, toh "customer" ek internal team hogi, aur bikri ek galiyare mein hui baatcheet hogi.

Lekin Anaya Sahaj se aage ke baare mein pehle hi soch rahi thi, aur Imran use uske chehre par dekh sakta tha. Agar tool chala, toh doosri companies bhi use chahengi: lenders, insurers, clinics, schools, har woh jiski chat window zaroorat se zyada ikattha karti hai. Unhe bechna *B2B* hoga, business to business, aur B2B alag khel hai. Jise demo pasand aata hai woh shaayad hi dastakhat karta hai. Budget kisi aur ke haath mein hota hai, aur woh aksar user nahi hota. Security ya legal mein koi bikri rok sakta hai aur user ko jawaab dene ka paabnd nahi hai. Ek bada customer poori company ki priorities badal sakta hai, kyunki agar unki security team ko dastakhat se pehle kuch chahiye, toh woh sabse upar chala jaata hai, chahe kisi user ne kabhi maanga ho ya nahi.

"Toh har customer ke liye," usne kaha, "teen sawaal poocho. Paisa kaun dega? Chalayega kaun? Mana kaun kar sakta hai?"

Anaya ne whiteboard dekha. Sahaj ke liye jawaab yeh the: company paisa degi, Farah ki team aur engineers ise chalayenge, aur Lakshmi mana kar sakti hai. Woh abhi tak Lakshmi Iyer se mili nahi thi. Use shak hua ki aakhri aadhe ghante se woh ek aise product ka design bana rahi thi jisko rokne ki taqat building mein ek hi insaan ke paas thi, aur usne ek baar bhi use poochha nahi tha ki woh kya sochti hai.

## Assistant ne kya likha

Apne desk ki taraf wapas jaate hue Anaya ne kuch kiya jo woh mahino se kar rahi thi aur ab jisse use bechaini hone lagi thi. Usne ek AI assistant khola, problem batayi, aur use likhne ko kaha ki India mein log chats mein pehchaan ke number kyun share karte hain.

Usne chaar bharose se bhare paragraph likhe. Bahut achhe paragraph the. Unmein se ek mein likha tha ki customers zaroorat se zyada isliye batate hain kyunki "digital literacy kam hai aur sarkari-sunayi dene wali services par bharosa zyada hai", aur doosre mein samjhaya gaya tha ki yeh pravritti "pehli baar loan maangne waalon mein khaas taur par zyada thi".

Usne use do baar padha aur socha: *isko yeh pata kahan se hai?*

Yeh Farah ke transcripts se to nahi ho sakta tha, jo usne kabhi dekhe hi nahi the. Yeh, use laga, woh baat thi jo koi soch-samajhkar dinner party mein bol deta. Woh theek waise hi sahi lag rahi thi jaise ausat raay sahi lagti hai. Zaroori nahi ki woh galat thi. Woh jaanchi nahi gayi thi. Ek assistant draft kar sakta hai, summary de sakta hai, sawaal sujha sakta hai aur kisi plan ki aalochana kar sakta hai, jo sab kaam ki cheezein hain, lekin woh aapke customers ke saamne baith nahi sakta, aur aapke market ke baare mein woh jo kehta hai woh uske padhe hue ka mishran hai, aapke logon ke baare mein koi tathya nahi.

Isliye usne apne liye doosra niyam banaya, pehle ke neeche. Uske notes ka har daava ya toh saboot tak jayega, ya assumption likha jayega. Woh paragraph par wapas gayi, pehli baar waale applicants wala vaakya dhoondha, aur uske bagal mein likha: *Asamarthit. Kya settle karega: kya woh 37 zyadatar pehli baar waale applicants hain. Farah das minute mein bata sakti hai.* Jaanchne mein kuch kharcha nahi tha. Zyadatar baar jaanchne ki keemat ek chhoti si baatcheet hoti hai.

## Ab woh kahan hai

Shaam tak whiteboard kisi aise insaan ka kaam lag raha tha jo dono se zyada vyavasthit ho. Chaar shabd, chaar chhote paragraph, aur unke neeche un cheezon ki list jo kisi ko nahi pata thi:

*Kya hum English akshron mein type kiye gaye Hindi naam dhoondh sakte hain? Lakshmi kya kehti hai? Yeh numbers asal mein type kaun karta hai, aur kyun?*

Aakhri sawaal technology ke baare mein tha hi nahi. Uske paas saintees conversations thin aur is baat ka koi andaaza nahi tha ki saintees-wi baar type karne wale ke dimaag mein kya chal raha tha. Uske paas Imran ka reaction tha aur uski apni ginti, aur inmein se koi bhi customer nahi tha.

"Yeh tum yahan se nahi jaan sakti," Imran ne pen ka dhakkan lagate hue kaha. "Jaakar unse poochhna padega."

Anaya ne sir hilaya. Woh pehle bhi logon se sawaal-jawaab kar chuki thi, aur use apni kamzori pata thi: woh sawaal waise poochhti thi jaise log tab poochhte hain jab unhe umeed hoti hai ki jawaab haan hoga. Yeh ek dikkat banne waali thi.

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Har product teen stage se guzarta hai: yeh pata lagana ki problem asli hai ya nahi, cheez ko achhe se banana, aur logon ko use dhoondhne aur istemaal karte rehne ke liye taiyaar karna. Woh aksar sabse kamzor stage par marta hai. Banane se pehle, ek idea ko chaar risks par aazmana chahiye: ki use koi chahta nahi, ki use koi chala nahi sakta, ki use banaya nahi ja sakta, aur ki woh apna kharcha nahi nikaal sakta. Consumers ko bechna companies ko bechne se alag kaam hai, kyunki doosre mein paisa dene wala, chalane wala, aur mana karne wala aksar teen alag log hote hain. Aur aapke apne customers ke baare mein koi assistant jo kuch bhi kahe, woh ek draft hai, finding nahi, jab tak koi asli cheez use jaanch na le.
