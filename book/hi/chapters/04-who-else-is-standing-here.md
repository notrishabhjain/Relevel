Thursday ko Anaya apna saboot lekar Lakshmi Iyer ke paas gayi. Lakshmi Sahaj ki compliance head hain. Unka kaam hai dekhna ki company niyam maan rahi hai. Unhone bees saal ek bank mein kaam kiya tha. Unhone printout, 37 flags aur numbered notes padhe. Woh aise padh rahi thi jaise woh insaan padhta hai jise kaagazon ne pehle dhokha diya ho. Woh dekh rahi thi ki kya chhoot gaya hai.

Unhone dekha ki 9 percent ek hafte aur ek chatbot se aaya hai. Anaya ne kaha ki notes mein yeh pehle se likha hai. "Sirf isi wajah se main abhi tak padh rahi hoon," Lakshmi ne kaha.

Anaya ne bataya ki woh kya banana chahti hai. Tool message mein pehchaan ke numbers aur doosri personal details dhoondhega. Message ke aage jaane se pehle woh unhe chhupa dega. Saaf kiye hue messages phir bhi ek bahar ki company ko jaayenge. Chatbot ke replies likhne wala software (woh program jo replies likhta hai) usi company ka hai.

Lakshmi ne "haan, lekin" kaha. Jo tool Sahaj ke paas kam data rakhe, use woh nahi rokengi. Jo tool zyada data bahar bheje, use woh rokengi. Agar cleaning company ke andar ho, kuch bhi bahar jaane se pehle, toh woh maan sakti hain. Agar woh kisi aur ke computer par ho, toh nahi.

Unhone ek sawaal aur poochha. Anaya ko kaise pata chalega ki tool kaam karta hai? Anaya ko abhi nahi pata tha. Lakshmi ne kaha ki jab pata ho, tab wapas aana.

Yeh chapter aage ka kaam dekhta hai. Anaya check karti hai ki tool pehle se maujood hai ya nahi. Woh naapti hai ki mauka kitna bada hai. Woh doosre options ko parakhti hai. Phir woh baatcheet ke notes ko ek rank ki hui list mein badalti hai.

## Case: ek haan, lekin

Yeh aise dukaandaar jaisa hai jo kahe, "Yahan dukaan kholo, par meri taraf ki sadak par." Is "haan, lekin" ne do kaam kiye.

Pehla, usne pichhle chapter ka riskiest assumption (woh andaaza jo plan ko sabse zyada doba sakta hai) pakka kar diya. Sahaj saaf kiya hua text bahar ki company ko bhej sakta tha, bas ek shart poori ho.

Doosra, usne kisi design ke bante se pehle hi design ka ek niyam tay kar diya. Tool ko Sahaj ki apni deewar ke andar chalna tha. Isse kai aasaan designs hat gaye. Woh har service bhi hat gayi jo cleaning ke liye kaccha message leti hai.

Aisa shuru ka niyam ek tohfa hai. Woh bina kharch ke options hata deta hai, jab tak woh sirf ideas hain aur unpar koi kaam nahi hua.

## Kya yeh kaam kisi ne pehle kiya hai?

Ab woh sawaal aaya jo Anaya ko hafton pehle poochhna chahiye tha. Agar tool pehle se maujood ho toh? Yeh waisa hai jaise nayi dukaan kholne se pehle agli gali ki dukaan dekh lena. Usne ek shaam dhoondhne mein bitai.

Use Rampart naam ka ek open tool mila. Open ka matlab hai ki koi bhi use chala aur padh sakta hai. Woh free tha. Kaabil logon ne use banaya aur test kiya tha. Uska kaam bilkul wahi tha jo Anaya ke dimaag mein tha: text mein personal details dhoondhna. Uske banane walon ne ek imaandaar list chhapi thi. List batati thi ki kin languages par test hua, aur tool kitna achha chala. Usmein English thi aur Europe ki chhe languages. Sab Latin alphabet mein hain, yaani A se Z wale akshar.

Anaya ne Hindi wala hissa do baar padha. Tool ke apne results ke hisaab se, woh Hindi ki personal details mein se 100 mein lagbhag 14 hi dhoondh paata tha. Yeh Devanagari script mein likhi Hindi thi, yaani Hindi ke apne akshar.

Usne number ke aage sawaaliya nishaan likha. Claims ka uska rule dono taraf kaam karta tha. Woh un claims par bhi lagta tha jo use pasand the, aur un par bhi jo nahi the. Woh ise baad mein khud check karegi. Bina check kiye bhi number dikhata tha ki tool ki pahunch kahan khatam hoti hai.

Sahaj ke customers us kinare ke andar nahi rehte the. Woh English mein likhte the. Woh Hindi mein likhte the. Woh us roop mein bhi likhte the jo Sanjay Patil ne bina chaahe dikha diya tha. Yaani English letters mein likhi Hindi, English words ke saath mili hui, aksar ek hi sentence mein. Jo tool ek hi tarah ki likhai ke liye bana ho, use yahan teen tarah ka text milta.

## Mauke ka size naapna

Phir Anaya ne poochha ki yeh tool kaun chahega. Usne jawaab numbers mein diya. Market ka size andaaza karne ke do tareeke hain. Market matlab woh sab log jo kharid sakte hain.

Pehla tareeka kisi industry report se shuru hota hai. Woh aisa sunai deta hai: "Customer-support software 10 billion dollar ka market hai, toh agar hum 1 percent jeetein...". Yeh jaldi ho jaata hai, aur lagbhag bekaar hai. Koi nahi bata sakta ki woh 1 percent kaise jeeta jaayega.

Doosra tareeka un customers se shuru hota hai jinhe aap gin sakte hain, aur upar ki taraf jodta hai. Yeh waisa hai jaise apni gali ki dukaanein gino, phir jodo ki har ek kitna dengi. Ise *bottom-up estimate* kehte hain (zameen se upar banaya hua size). Iski badi khoobi yeh hai ki har andaaza page par saamne rehta hai. Koi bhi us par sawaal utha sakta hai.

Usne sabse bade us group se shuru kiya jiska woh bachaav kar sakti thi. Yaani India ke registered lenders aur finance apps. Ek public list mein lagbhag 9,000 naam the. Yeh ek fact tha. Phir usne andaaza lagaya ki ek chhoti firm aise tool ke liye saal mein lagbhag ₹1.2 lakh de sakti hai. Yeh andaaza tha, aur usne ise andaaza hi mark kiya.

Yahan se teen size baar-baar aate hain, ek doosre ke andar. Yeh rahe unke naam.

::: def Teen size, ek doosre ke andar
Poore shehar ki dukaanein sochiye. *Total addressable market* (TAM) woh sab log hain jinhe problem hai, us daam par jo aap lenge. *Serviceable addressable market* (SAM) TAM ka woh hissa hai jise aap apne paas ke tareekon se sambhal sakte hain. Yeh shehar ke aapke hisse ki dukaanein hain. *Serviceable obtainable market* (SOM) SAM ka woh hissa hai jo aap lagbhag teen saal mein sach mein jeet sakte hain.
:::

Table mein Anaya ke numbers hain. Har row apne upar wali row se chhoti hai.

Table: Privacy tool ke liye Anaya ka bottom-up estimate
| Measure | Matlab | Hisaab |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TAM | Woh sab jinhe problem hai | 9,000 firms × ₹1.2 lakh = lagbhag ₹108 crore saal |
| SAM | Woh hissa jise woh sambhal sakti hai | 2,700 firms jo chat chalati hain aur mahine mein 5,000 ya zyada chats sambhalti hain = lagbhag ₹32 crore |
| SOM | Lagbhag teen saal mein woh jo jeet sakti hai | 2,700 ka 2 percent = 54 customers = lagbhag ₹65 lakh saal |

Aakhri number ne use rok diya. ₹65 lakh saal ek chhoti team ka kharch nikaalta hai. Isse company nahi banti. Lekin us number par woh yakeen kar sakti thi. Pehle number ke baare mein woh yeh nahi keh sakti thi.

Usne yeh estimate kaagaz par nahi, spreadsheet mein banaya. Spreadsheet khaanon ka ek jaal hai jo jodna-ghatana khud karta hai. Har number apne khaane (cell) mein tha. Har total ek formula tha, yaani ek hisaab jo un khaanon ki taraf ishaara karta hai. Farah ne har customer ke daam par sawaal uthaya. Anaya ne ek cell badla, aur uske neeche ka har total badal gaya. Jo estimate khud update ho, woh behas ko bulaata hai. Estimate isi ke liye hota hai.

Table ka maksad aakhri number nahi tha. Har line ek sawaal uthati thi. 9,000 firms ka pata kaise, ya 5,000 chats ka? Aur har sawaal ke paas jaane ki ek jagah thi. Har number pakka ya andaaza, aise mark tha. Jo andaaza the, unhe sabse pehle check kiya jaata.

## Teen tarah ki competition

Zyadatar log rivals ko doosre products ki list samajhte hain. Anaya ne apne rivals ko teen tarah mein baanta. Yeh waisa hai jaise poochhna ki ek parivaar ko garam khaana kaise mil sakta hai. Woh rival restaurant se mangwa sakte hain. Woh tiffin service le sakte hain. Ya woh ghar par hi pakate rahein, jaise hamesha pakate hain.

Table: Privacy tool ke liye teen tarah ki competition
| Kism | Kya hai | Sahaj mein |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct | Wahi kaam wahi tareeke se karta hai | Rampart jaise tools, aur kuch cloud services ke saath aane wale privacy features |
| Indirect | Wahi kaam doosre tareeke se karta hai | Koi insaan jo har export bahar jaane se pehle padhta hai; ek chat vendor ka software jo sirf card numbers chhupata hai |
| Status quo | Log aaj kya karte hain, kuch na karna bhi | Farah ki team jab yaad aaye tab haath se number mitati hai, aur handbook ki ek line jo customers se sensitive details share na karne ko kehti hai |

Export matlab ek system se nikaali gayi data ki copy. Vendor matlab woh company jo aapko ek service bechti hai.

*Status quo* aksar sabse mazboot rival hota hai. Yeh ghar ki purani aadat hai. Woh free hai, jaana-pehchaana hai, aur thoda-bahut kaam bhi karta hai. Naye product ko aisi dopahar ko harana padta hai jismein kuch galat nahi hua. Offices aksar aise dinon ko saboot maan lete hain ki sab theek hai. Isliye jab koi kehta hai ki uske product ka koi rival nahi, toh aam taur par usne dekha hi nahi ki log aaj kya karte hain.

Har asli alternative ke liye Anaya ne ek *competitor teardown* banaya. Sochiye do phone mez par saath-saath rakhe hain. Usne likha ki har tool kiske liye hai, kitne ka hai, kya achha karta hai, aur uske customers kis baat ki shikayat karte hain. Shikayatein dhoondhne ki sabse achhi jagah woh reviews the jo users ne public sites par chhode the. Kaam ki cheez woh shikayat thi jo baar-baar aati thi.

In tools ke baare mein teen shikayatein baar-baar aayi.

- Woh sirf English mein achhe chalte the.
- Woh ordinary product codes aur order numbers ko aise flag karte the jaise woh raaz hon.
- Weekend mein unhe set up karna mushkil tha.

## Apni jagah batana

Teardown se Anaya ko ek sentence ka maal mil gaya. Yeh sentence product ko doosre options ke beech rakhta hai. Ise *positioning statement* kehte hain. Yeh dukaan ke board ki us line jaisa hai jo batati hai ki dukaan kiske liye hai aur alag kyun hai. Ise likhne mein discipline lagti hai. Dhundhla sentence likhna aasaan hai. Saaf sentence mushkil hai.

::: example Guard ka pehla positioning statement
Un companies ke liye jo India mein customer chat chalati hain aur chahti hain ki pehchaan ke numbers zaroorat se zyada door na jaayein, guard ek chhoti privacy layer hai jo English, Hindi aur dono ke mix ko samajhti hai. Ek hi writing system ke text ke liye bane tools ke ulat, yeh isliye bani hai ki log sach mein kaise type karte hain.
:::

Ek test hai. Kya yeh sentence kisi rival par bhi utna hi achha baithta hai? Toh woh abhi position nahi hai. Anaya ne apna sentence zor se padha. Use khushi hui ki woh un tools mein se kisi par bhi fit nahi hota tha jo usne dekhe the.

## Baatcheeton se rank kiye hue ideas tak

"Mujhe problem mil gayi" se seedha "mujhe pata hai kya banana hai" par kood jaana lubhaata hai. Zyadatar bekaar software isi kood se likha jaata hai. Isliye Anaya ne beech mein ek drawing rakhi.

Page ke sabse upar usne woh result likha jo woh chahti thi: chat se kam personal details bahar jaayein. Uske neeche usne branches banayi. Har branch ek zaroorat ya takleef thi jo usne sach mein suni thi. Har ek ke paas usne un baatcheeton ke number likhe jahan se woh aayi.

- Chatbot ka greeting details maangta hai (baatcheet 2 aur 3).
- Agents ke paas koi rule nahi (1 aur 5).
- Photos bina doosri nazar ke le li jaati hain (1).
- Numbers ajeeb tareeke se type hote hain (4).
- Helpers un logon ki taraf se type karte hain jinhe pata nahi ki number kahan jaata hai (5).

Har branch ke neeche usne kuch ideas likhe jo madad kar sakte the.

Is tarah ki drawing ko *opportunity tree* kehte hain. Yeh ideas ke khaandaan-ke-ped jaisa hai. Yeh us result se shuru hota hai jo aap chahte hain. Phir logon ki batayi zaroorton mein branch karta hai. Aur possible fix par khatam hota hai. Phir Anaya ne branches ko chaar sawaalon se rank kiya. Zaroorat kitne logon ko chhuti hai? Kitna dukhati hai? Saboot kitna mazboot hai? Kya woh sabse upar ke result ko hilati hai? Usne dhyaan rakha ki rank saboot ke hisaab se ho, josh ke hisaab se nahi.

Jo tool woh sabse zyada banana chahti thi, woh nahi jeeta. Greeting jeeta. Uske paas do baatcheeton ka saboot tha, ek free fix tha, aur result par seedha asar tha. Anaya ne hashiye mein likha "is hafte greeting dobara likho". Usi dopahar usne Farah ko message kar diya.

Tool doosre number par aaya. Woh ek alag branch ka jawaab tha. Agents ke paas koi madad nahi thi, aur greeting jo chhod de use koi pakadta nahi tha. Tree mein woh "agents ke paas koi rule nahi" ke neeche baitha tha. Ek line "ek chhota tool jo personal details dhoondhe aur chhupaye" tak jaati thi.

::: key Tree kis kaam ka hai
Tree ko "kyun" ka naqsha samjho. Woh dikhata hai ki kab aapka pasandeeda idea sirf doosri sabse achhi branch hai. Woh wajah bhi likhta hai. Har idea ko upar ki taraf ek zaroorat tak, aur neeche ki taraf saboot tak, dhoondha ja sakta hai.
:::

## Saaraansh

"Haan, lekin" design ka ek niyam hai. Sahaj mein usne tay kiya ki tool company ke andar chalega. Isse shuru mein hi kai designs hat gaye.

- Banane se pehle dekhiye ki pehle se kya maujood hai. Rival ke chhape claims khud test kijiye. Dekhiye ki unki pahunch kahan khatam hoti hai.
- Bottom-up estimate un customers ko ginta hai jinke naam aap jaante hain, aur har ek ke daam se guna karta hai. Woh har number ko pakka ya andaaza mark karta hai. TAM, SAM aur SOM use sab se, jinhe problem hai, ghata kar us tak laate hain jise jeeta ja sake.
- Rivals direct, indirect ya status quo hote hain. Status quo aksar sabse mazboot hota hai. Teardown unhe compare karta hai aur baar-baar aane wali shikayatein likhta hai.
- Positioning statement batata hai ki product kiske liye hai aur alag kyun hai. Woh kisi rival par utna hi achha nahi baithna chahiye.
- Opportunity tree result se zaroorat tak aur idea tak jaata hai. Woh branches ko saboot ke hisaab se rank karta hai. Woh dikhata hai ki kab aapka pasandeeda idea sabse achha nahi hai.

Agle chapter mein Anaya ko chunna hai ki kya chhodna hai. Tree ka sab kuch woh nahi kar sakti.
