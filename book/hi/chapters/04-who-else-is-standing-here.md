Lakshmi Iyer ke office ki khidki ek deewar ki taraf khulti thi, aur mez par ek paani ke glass aur ek band folder ke siwa kuch nahi tha. Anaya baithi, aur use, bevajah, aisa laga jaise woh koi customer ho.

"Saboot," Lakshmi ne kaha.

Anaya ne use printout diya, saintees flags, aur interview notes ka woh page jisme lines ke number the. Usne corridor mein tay kar liya tha ki woh in sab ke baare mein apni bhaavnayein nahi batayegi. Lakshmi ne dheere-dheere padha. Woh Sahaj aane se pehle bees saal bank mein rahi thi, aur woh waise padhti thi jaise woh log padhte hain jinhe documents ne jhooth bola ho: yeh dekhte hue ki kya chhod diya gaya hai.

"Nau percent," usne kaha. "Ek hafta. Ek chatbot."

"Haan. Maine notes mein bhi likha hai."

"Maine dekha." Lakshmi ne ek page palta. "Bas isi wajah se main abhi tak padh rahi hoon." Usne folder band kiya aur pehli baar Anaya ko theek se dekha. "Batao tum kya karna chahti ho aur mujhse kya chahiye."

Anaya ne bataya. Ek chhota sa tool banana jo kisi message mein pehchaan ke numbers aur doosri personal details ko pehchaane aur message kahin aur jaane se pehle unhe chhupa de. Aur phir woh hissa jisse use darr lag raha tha: saaf kiye gaye messages phir bhi us bahari company ko jaate rahenge jiska software chatbot ke jawaab likhta hai. Kya yeh theek tha?

Ek lambi khamoshi rahi. AC ne khat se awaaz ki.

"Jo tool humein kam rakhne par majboor kare, use main nahi rokungi," Lakshmi ne kaha. "Jo zyada bahar bheje, use rokungi. Agar safai humari deewaron ke andar ho, kuch bhi bahar jaane se pehle, toh main usse jee sakti hoon. Agar woh kisi aur ke computer par ho, toh nahi. Baaki sab tafseel hai. Aur Anaya." Usne folder par thapki di. "Mujhe jaanna hai ki tumhe kaise pata chalega ki yeh kaam karta hai."

"Mujhe abhi nahi pata."

"Achha jawaab. Jab pata chale tab aana."

Yeh ek shart ke saath haan thi. Anaya corridor se wapas jaate hue do aisi baatein jaanti thi jo ek ghanta pehle use nahi pata thi. Tool ko Sahaj ki deewar ke is taraf chalna hoga, jisse kai aasaan designs kat gaye. Aur uski riskiest assumption bach gayi thi, mushkil se, shartein lagakar. Woh pen dhoondhne gayi.

## Kya kisi ne yeh pehle hi kar diya hai?

Jo sawaal use hafton pehle poochhna chahiye tha, woh ab apne aap aa gaya: *agar yeh pehle se maujood ho toh?*

Usne ek shaam dhoondhne mein lagayi, aur jo mila woh tasalli bhi tha aur thoda sharminda karne wala bhi. Rampart naam ka ek khula tool tha, muft, jiska kaam bilkul wahi tha jo uske dimaag mein tha: text ke ek tukde mein personal details dhoondhna. Usse kaabil logon ne banaya aur jaancha tha. Woh achhe se document kiya gaya tha, aur uske banane walon ne imaandaari se ek list chhapi thi ki use kis par test kiya gaya aur kitna achha chala. List English mein thi, aur chhe European bhashaon mein, jo sab Latin lipi mein likhi jaati hain.

Usne Hindi waala hissa do baar padha. Uske apne chhape hue nateeje, jaise usne padhe, bata rahe the ki Devanagari lipi mein likhi Hindi ke har sau personal details mein se woh sirf lagbhag chaudah dhoondh paata tha. Usne number likha aur uske aage ek sawaalia nishaan laga diya. Daavon ke baare mein uska ek niyam tha, aur woh niyam un daavon ko chhoot nahi deta tha jo use pasand aate the. Woh us figure ko baad mein khud jaanchegi. Lekin afwaah ke taur par bhi woh kaam ka tha, kyunki usne use dikhaya ki us tool ke naqshe ki sarhad kahan hai.

Sahaj ke chatbot mein type karne wale log us sarhad ke andar nahi rehte the. Woh English mein likhte the. Woh Hindi mein likhte the. Aur zyadatar woh likhte the jo Sanjay Patil ne bina jaane dikha diya tha: English akshron mein likhi Hindi, English shabdon ke saath khule taur par mili hui, aksar ek hi vaakya ke andar. Jo tool ek lipi ke text ke liye bana ho, use teen lipiyon ka text milne wala tha.

## Iska size

"Yeh kaun chahega?" Anaya ne ek naye page ke upar likha. Phir, kyunki ise dheele-dhaale tareeke se jawaab dena aasaan hota jo use pasand nahi tha, usne ise numbers mein jawaab diya.

Market ka size naapne ke do tareeke hain. Pehla ek industry report se shuru hota hai: "Customer-support software das billion dollar ka market hai, toh agar hum ek percent jeet lein..." Yeh tez hai aur lagbhag bekaar, kyunki kisi ko andaaza nahi hota ki woh ek percent kaise jeetega. Doosra un customers se shuru hota hai jinhe aap gin sakte hain aur phir upar guna karta hai. Anaya ne doosra raasta liya, jise *bottom-up estimate* kehte hain, aur usne har assumption page par rakhi jahan use dekha aur kaata ja sake.

Usne sabse bade us group se shuru kiya jiska woh bachaav kar sakti thi: India ke registered lenders aur finance apps. Use ek public list mili thi, jo ek tathya tha, aur usme lagbhag nau hazaar naam the. Usne andaaza lagaya ki ek chhoti firm aise tool ke liye saal mein lagbhag ek lakh bees hazaar rupaye de sakti hai, jo ek assumption thi, aur usne use waisa hi nishaan lagaya.

| | Iska matlab | Uska number |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TAM | Woh sab jinhe yeh problem hai | 9,000 firms × ₹1.2 lakh = lagbhag ₹108 crore saal mein |
| SAM | Jise woh sambhal sakti hai | 2,700 jo chat chalate hain aur mahine mein 5,000 ya zyada chats sambhalte hain = lagbhag ₹32 crore |
| SOM | Jo woh lagbhag teen saal mein jeet sakti hai | 2,700 ka 2 percent = 54 customers = lagbhag ₹65 lakh saal mein |

Inmein pehla *total addressable market* hai, yaani TAM. Doosra, *serviceable addressable market* ya SAM, woh hissa hai jise woh apne paas jo tha usse sach-much pahunch sakti thi. Teesra, *serviceable obtainable market* ya SOM, woh hissa hai jo woh kuch saalon mein jeet sakti thi agar cheezein theek-thaak chalein.

Aakhri figure ne use peeche hatkar baithne par majboor kiya. Saal ke ₹65 lakh koi company nahi thi. Woh mushkil se ek team thi. Lekin woh aisa number tha jis par woh yakeen kar sakti thi, jo woh pehle number ke baare mein nahi keh sakti thi. Table ka maqsad kabhi aakhri figure nahi tha. Maqsad yeh tha ki har line par sawaal uthaya ja sakta tha, aur har sawaal ka ek thikana tha: *nau hazaar ka pata kaise? Paanch hazaar chats ka pata kaise?* Har assumption par *known* ya *assumed* ka nishaan tha, aur assumed wale sabse pehle jaanchne wali cheezein thin.

## Jo cheezein raaste mein khadi hain

Zyadatar log competitors ko rival products ki list samajhte hain. Anaya ne ab teen kism dekhe.

*Direct* wale wahi kaam wahi tareeke se karte the: Rampart jaise tools, aur kuch cloud services ke saath aane wale privacy features. *Indirect* wale wahi kaam doosre tareeke se karte the: ek insaan jo har export bahar jaane se pehle padhta tha, ya ek chat vendor ka software jo sirf card numbers chhupata tha aur kuch nahi. Aur teesra kism tha, jo koi product tha hi nahi.

*Status quo* ka matlab hai log aaj jo karte hain, kuch na karna bhi usme shaamil. Sahaj mein woh Farah ki team thi jo kisi ko yaad aane par haath se ek number mita deti thi, aur handbook mein ek line jo customers se kehti thi ki kripya sensitive details share na karein. Woh muft tha. Jaana-pehchana tha. Kisi tarah kaam karta tha. Jo bhi kuch banaye use kisi rival product ko itna nahi, jitna us dopahar ko harana hota hai jab kuch galat nahi hua, jise har sanstha is baat ka saboot maan leti hai ki kuch galat hai hi nahi. Jab koi kehta hai "humara koi competitor nahi", toh aamtaur par matlab hota hai ki unhone dekha nahi ki log abhi kya karte hain.

Har asli vikalp ke liye usne woh kiya jise *competitor teardown* kehte hain: woh kiske liye tha, kitne ka tha, kis mein achha tha, aur uske customers kis baat ki shikayat karte the. Shikayatein dhoondhne ki sabse achhi jagah public sites par asli users ke chhode hue reviews the, aur kaam ki shikayat woh thi jo baar-baar aati thi. In tools ke liye baar-baar aane wali shikayatein yeh teen thin: yeh sirf English mein achha chalta hai; yeh aam product codes aur order numbers ko aise flag karta hai jaise woh raaz hon; aur ise weekend par set up karna mushkil hai.

Teardown se ek vaakya nikalta hai, aur achha vaakya ek anushasan hai. *Positioning statement* batata hai ki product kiske liye hai aur kyun alag hai, ek hi saans mein:

*Un companies ke liye jo India mein customer chat chalati hain aur pehchaan ke numbers ko zaroorat se zyada aage jaane se rokna chahti hain, guard ek chhoti si privacy layer hai jo English, Hindi aur dono ki khichdi samajhti hai. Un tools se alag jo ek lipi ke text ke liye bane hain, ise log asal mein jaise type karte hain uske liye banaya gaya tha.*

Usne ise khaali kamre mein zor se padha aur use raahat mili ki woh kisi competitor ka vivaran nahi tha.

## Interviews se ped tak

Us hafte Anaya ne aakhri kaam woh kiya jo woh ek mahina pehle chhod deti. Usne apne interview notes ko market ke numbers ke bagal mein rakha aur dono ko ek saath draw kiya.

"Mujhe ek problem mili" se seedha "mujhe pata hai kya banana hai" par chhalaang lagana lubhaavna hota hai, aur zyadatar gair-zaroori software isi chhalaang se likha jaata hai. Isliye usne ek ped banaya, sabse upar woh nateeja jo woh chahti thi: *chat se kam personal details bahar jaayein.* Nateeje ke neeche, shaakhaon ki tarah, woh zarooratein aur takleefein jo usne asal mein suni thi, har ek ke interview numbers ke saath. Greeting jo details maangti hai (interviews 2 aur 3). Agents ke liye koi niyam nahi (1 aur 5). Photos bina doosri nazar ke le li jaati hain (1). Numbers ajeeb tareeke se type kiye gaye (4). Doosron ke liye type karne wale madadgaar jinhe pata nahi tha ki baat kahan jaati hai (5). Har shaakha ke neeche, madad ke kuch ideas.

Phir usne shaakhaon ko chaar sawaalon par rank kiya: kitne logon ko chhooti thi, kitna dard deti thi, saboot kitna pakka tha, aur kya woh sabse upar ke nateeje ko hilati thi. Usne dhyaan rakha ki rank saboot ke hisaab se ho, josh ke hisaab se nahi. Jo tool woh banana chahti thi, woh jeeta nahi. Greeting jeeti: do interviews, ek muft ka ilaaj, aur nateeje tak seedhi line. Usne hashiye mein likha *is hafte greeting dobara likho* aur usi dopahar Farah ko message bhej diya.

Tool doosre number par aaya. Woh us shaakha ka jawaab tha jo kehti thi ki agents ke paas koi madad nahi hai aur greeting jo chhod deti hai use kuch pakadta nahi, jo wahi shaakha thi jis par Imran ka chehra chamak utha tha. Woh *agents ke liye koi niyam nahi* ke neeche baitha tha, aur uske neeche se *ek chhota tool jo personal details pehchaane aur chhupa de* tak ek line jaati thi.

Is tarah ki drawing ko *opportunity tree* kehte hain, aur uski shaant keemat yeh hai ki jab aapka pasandeeda hal asal mein doosre number ki shaakha ho, toh yeh aapko batata hai.

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Kuch bhi banane se pehle pata lagao ki wahi jagah aur kaun khada hai. Market ko sabse achhe tareeke se un customers se naapa jaata hai jinhe aap gin sakte hain, har assumption likhkar aur known ya anumaan likhkar. Sabse mazboot competitor aksar wahi hai jo log abhi karte hain, kuch na karna bhi, aur vikalpon ko saath-saath rakh kar dekhna, unke customers ki baar-baar aane wali shikayaton ke saath, dikhata hai ki jagah kahan hai. Ek vaakya jo batata hai ki product kiske liye hai aur kyun alag hai, jaanchta hai ki aapko sach-much pata hai ya nahi. Aur nateeje se zaroorat se idea tak jaane wala ped pasandeeda idea ko imaandaar rakhta hai.
