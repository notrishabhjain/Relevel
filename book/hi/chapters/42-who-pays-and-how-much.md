"Hamein kitne mein padega?" Mr. Menon ne kaha.

Usne yeh seedhiyon par poochha tha, jaate hue, ek haath railing par rakhe. Woh lending partner ka technical lead tha, wahi jisne answer key dekhne ko kaha tha, aur defence ke baad ke teen hafton mein woh demonstration page dekhne do baar aur do saathiyon ko laane ek baar wapas aaya tha. Usne yeh waise poochha jaise log tab poochhte hain jab woh poori dopahar se ek sawaal thaame rahe hon.

Anaya is se dar rahi thi. Usne ek saal yeh seekhne mein bitaya tha ki *kya yeh kaam karta hai?* ka jawaab ek number se kaise dein. *Iska kharcha kya hai?* ek alag kism ka sawaal tha, aur uske paas koi aisa number nahi tha jis par use yakeen ho.

"Mujhe ek hafta dijiye," usne kaha. "Main aapke liye asli wala laungi."

## Teen alag log

Usne jo pehla kaam kiya woh yeh pata lagana tha ki Mr. Menon kaun tha.

Aisi company mein jo doosron ke istemaal ke liye software kharidti hai, teen alag log aamtaur par maayne rakhte hain, aur woh shaayad hi ek hi insaan hote hain. *User* woh hai jo roz product ke saath kaam karta hai. Guard ke liye woh ek developer tha, jo ek chat system mein kit jodta. *Buyer* woh hai jiske paas budget hai aur jo kharch ke liye kisi ko jawab deta hai, yahan engineering ya compliance ka head. Aur *blocker* woh hai jo na keh sakta hai aur use wajah batane ki zaroorat nahi. Lakshmi Sahaj ki thi. Mr. Menon ka ek security lead tha jise, usne khed se kaha, "Deewar" kehte the.

Har ek ko kuch alag parwaah thi. Developer chahta tha ki ise Friday ko jodna aasaan ho. Buyer ko ek number chahiye tha jo woh apne manager ko dikha sake. Blocker jaanna chahta tha ki text kahan jaata hai aur ise kaun padh sakta hai, aur uska jawaab pichhle July mein, Anaya ke saal ki sabse lambi meeting mein nikaala gaya tha.

"Ek keemat tumhare liye kitni maayne rakhegi?" usne poochha, aur ruki. Woh poochhne wali thi ki kya woh iske liye paisa dega. Usne project ke pehle mahine mein padha tha ki yeh galat sawaal kyun hai: log shishtata mein haan kehte hain, aur jawaab kuch ki bhavishyavani nahi karta.

Isliye usne woh poochhe jo use poochhne chahiye the. Aap aaj is se nipatne ke liye kya istemaal karte hain? Isme aapko kitna kharcha aata hai? Us kharch ko kisne manzoor kiya?

Usne use bataya. Woh ek vendor ko mahine mein lagbhag paintees hazaar rupaye deta tha ek tool ke liye jo chat logs mein card numbers ko blank karta tha, aur kuch nahi. Uske paas ek analyst bhi thi, jo apne samay ka lagbhag chauthai hissa Lakshmi ke jaisa ek saptahik sweep karne mein lagati thi. Us fraction par analyst ka vetan lagbhag tees hazaar rupaye mahine aata tha. Vendor ke liye manzoori compliance ke head ne di thi. Yeh sab ek tathya tha. Beeta hua kharch saboot hai. Ek bhavishya ka irada nahi.

## Chhat aur farsh

Anaya ne do figures ek card par likhe, aur unke neeche woh jo woh us dopahar tak nahi samjhi thi. Ek customer jo keemat dega woh uske vikalp se aati hai, aapke kharche se nahi. Vikalp chhat tay karta hai. Aapka apna kharcha farsh tay karta hai. Aisa tool jo ek analyst ke lagbhag chauthai samay ka bada hissa bachata hai woh buyer ke liye tees hazaar se kaafi kam ka hai, kyunki use apnana bhi mehnat ka kharcha hai, aur woh kuch hazaar se kaafi zyada ka hai.

Use beech ka hissa usi card par ginit se mila. Ek vajib keemat das hazaar rupaye ke beech kahin thi, jo uska kharcha poora karti, aur lagbhag bees hazaar jiske upar Mr. Menon poochhna shuru kar dete ki kya purana tareeka sasta tha. Usne pehle andaaze ke roop mein chaubees hazaar chuna, yeh dekhne ke liye ki uske saath kya hota hai.

## Paisa lene ke chaar tareeke

Imran ne, jiski har kism ke bill par raay thi, kaha ki ek AI product ke liye paisa lene ke chaar aam tareeke hain, aur har ek alag jokhim alag party par daalta hai.

*Seat pricing* har user ke liye, prati mahine paisa leta hai. Kharidar ke liye budget banana aasaan hai. Lekin yeh maanta hai ki ek atirikt istemaal seller ko lagbhag kuch nahi padta, jo chaalees saal se software ke liye sach raha hai aur aisi kisi bhi cheez ke liye jhootha hai jo text padhti aur likhti hai. Guard ke paas seats ka koi matlab nahi tha. Aur ek flat keemat par ek bhari customer das halke customers ke margin ko mita sakta tha.

*Usage pricing* har message ke liye charge karta hai. Aay kharche ke saath chalti hai, jo uska akarshan hai. Uski kamzori yeh hai ki kharidar bill ka andaaza nahi laga sakta aur istemaal ko raashan mein baant deta hai, jo ek suraksha tool ke liye ulta hota.

*Outcome pricing* har safal nateeje ke liye charge karta hai. Daam value ke saath chalta hai. Ise kharidar aur seller ko yeh sahmat hone ki zaroorat hai ki safalta kya hai aur use bina behes ke naap sakne ki. Prati satyapit saaf message, Anaya ne socha, kabhi sambhav ho sakta tha. Abhi nahi tha.

*Hybrid pricing* ek platform fee leta hai jisme kuch istemaal shaamil hai, phir uske aage ek dar. Kharidar ko budget ke liye ek number milta hai. Seller apna margin rakhta hai. Keemat samjhana mushkil hai.

## Woh row jo zyadatar price lists mein nahi hoti

Anaya ke paas Imran ke kharche ke figures the. Usne, jaisa use kaha gaya tha, ek mahine ke liye ek customer ko pahunchane ka poora kharcha rakha, sirf model ka bill nahi. Model aur hosting. Records aur traces ke liye storage. Aur ek insaan ka samay unhe onboard karne aur unke sawaalon ke jawaab dene mein, kyunki, jaisa woh seekh rahi thi, support aksar machine se zyada kharcha karta hai.

Usne ise teen baar kiya, kam, aadhar aur ucch istemaal par, aur aakhri row ne use bilkul shaant baitha diya.

| Istemaal | Prati mahine messages | Pahunchane ka kharcha | Flat ₹24,000 par margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Kam | 20,000 | ₹5,900 | 75% |
| Aadhar | 100,000 | ₹10,500 | 56% |
| Ucch | 1,000,000 | ₹58,500 | 144% ka ghata |

Jis margin ka woh varnan kar rahi thi woh *contribution margin* hai: ek customer jo aapke paas chhodta hai un kharchon ke baad jo unke saath badhte hain, jo woh dete hain uske hisse ke roop mein. Aadhar row par woh ek aadarniya chhappan percent tha. Ucch row par koi margin nahi tha. Ek vyast chat system wala customer ek flat keemat par use, jo woh dete, uske doguna se zyada kharcha karwata.

"Hamesha kam, aadhar aur ucch model karo," Imran ne kaha. "Zyadatar margin ki problems sirf aakhri row mein dikhti hain."

Usne keemat ko ek hybrid ke roop mein dobara banaya. Chaubees hazaar rupaye prati mahine mein ek lakh messages shaamil honge, aur har agle ek lakh ke liye nau hazaar. Das lakh messages par, ek customer ek lakh se thoda zyada dega, aur uska margin chaalees-chaar percent hoga. Bill us value ke saath chalta jo customer ko mili, aur uska margin unke badhne par phisalna band ho jaata.

## Woh pahiya jo apne aap ghoomta hai

Finance director ne, jise usne table dikhayi, ek alag sawaal poochha. "Aapke badhne par ise kya behtar banata hai?"

Anaya ko iski ummeed thi. *Flywheel* ek loop hai jisme product ka istemaal use behtar banata hai, jo zyada istemaal laata hai. Woh unn teen kismon se guzri jo AI mein milti hain. Pehla ek data flywheel hai: agents ke sudhaar tool ko behtar banate hain, taaki woh istemaal ke saath behtar ho. Yeh aksar logon ke kehne se kamzor hota hai. Fayde kuch hazaar udaharanon ke baad chadhna band kar dete hain, aur contracts aksar ek customer ke data ko doosre ke saath milane se rokte hain. Doosra ek workflow flywheel hai. Product kisi ke saptahik routine ka hissa ban jaata hai, aur doosre kaam uspar nirbhar hone lagte hain. Guard ke liye, woh maasik sweep tha. Yeh aamtaur par sabse mazboot hai. Teesra ek platform flywheel hai, jisme doosre log aapke product par banate hain. Yeh durlabh hai, aur ise pehle ek bheed chahiye.

Usne woh chetawani joda jo woh ab tak ek darjan documents mein daal chuki thi. Ek flywheel jo customers ke data par nirbhar hai tabhi tak kaam karta hai jab tak customers use apna data saunpne par bharosa karte hain. Saaf kaho ki kya store hota hai, kisse seekha jaata hai, aur use kaun dekh sakta hai. Ek customer ke sudhaaron ko doosre ki madad ke liye istemaal karne se pehle poochho.

## Ek supplier aapke saath kya kar sakta hai

Sheet ki aakhri row ek aise jokhim ke baare mein thi jise usne September mein kathinai se seekha tha. Agar koi product kisi aur company ke computers ya models par chalta hai, toh woh company bina poochhe apne daam aur apni quality badal sakti hai. Ek AI product ke liye, pahunchane ke kharche ka ek bada hissa, aur isliye *gross margin* ka, jo aay ghata product pahunchane ka seedha kharcha hai, kisi aur ka bill hai.

Woh jokhim hata nahi sakti thi, par use keemat laga sakti thi. Uski sifaarish ne kaha ki hosting ka daam doguna hone par kya hoga: aadhar margin chhappan percent se lagbhag saintees tak gir jaata, aur ek test kiye gaye doosre host par switch karne mein lagbhag ek hafta lagta. Usne kaha ki agar koi model retire hua toh woh kya karegi: pinned versions aur ek gate. Aur usne kaha ki agar koi rival wahi feature launch kare toh woh kya karegi: workflow aur rishte ka malik banna.

Usne agle Thursday Mr. Menon ko page bheja, upar pehle paragraph ke saath, jise usne chaar baar dobara likha tha.

*Main chaubees hazaar rupaye prati mahine ke ek hybrid plan ki sifaarish karti hoon, jisme 100,000 messages shaamil hon, aur har agle 100,000 ke liye ₹9,000. Aapka vikalp aapko vendor aur analyst ke samay ke beech lagbhag ₹65,000 mahine kharch karta hai, isliye keemat value se kaafi neeche hai. Hamara margin aadhar istemaal par 56 percent hai aur uske das guna par bhi 40 percent se upar rehta hai. Sabse bada jokhim hosting ka daam hai. Agar woh doguna ho jaye, toh hamara aadhar margin lagbhag 37 percent par aa jaata hai, aur hamare test kiye gaye doosre host par switch karne mein lagbhag ek hafta lagta hai.*

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Ek customer ek product ke liye paisa tab deta hai jab woh ek kaam apne vikalp se behtar ya sasta karta hai, isliye vikalp keemat par chhat tay karta hai aur aapka apna kharcha farsh. Ek company mein, user, buyer aur blocker aamtaur par teen alag log hote hain, aur kisi cheez ki keemat jaanne ka tareeka yeh poochhna hai ki woh aaj kya istemaal karte hain, uska kharcha kya hai aur use kisne manzoor kiya, kyunki beeta hua kharcha saboot hai aur waade nahi. Seat, usage, outcome aur hybrid pricing mein se har ek alag party par alag jokhim daalta hai, aur jo kuch bhi har baar chalne par paise kharch karta hai uske liye ek flat seat keemat khatarnaak hai. Ek customer ko pahunchane ka poora kharcha kam, aadhar aur ucch istemaal par model karo, kyunki margin ki problem aamtaur par aakhri row mein hoti hai. Aur kaho ki agar koi supplier apna daam doguna kar de toh tumhare margin ka kya hoga, aur badalne mein kitna samay lagega.
