Anaya Deshmukh ne pehli baar kisi ajnabi ka Aadhaar number tab dekha jab woh apne desk par baithi samosa kha rahi thi aur dhoondh kisi aur cheez ko rahi thi.

March ke doosre hafte ka ek Tuesday tha, shaam ke aath baj chuke the, aur Baner mein mithai ki dukaan ke upar bana office ghanton pehle khaali ho chuka tha. Din bhar ki jo khushboo bachi thi, garam tel aur chaashni ki, woh har shaam ki tarah farsh ke takhton se upar chadh rahi thi. Anaya ke saamne ek spreadsheet khuli thi aur ek plan tha. Sahaj, jis company mein woh kaam karti thi, ek app chalati thi jahan log bijli ka bill bharte the aur aajkal chhote loan bhi lete the. App ke ek kone mein ek support chatbot baitha tha jo din-raat sawaalon ke jawaab deta tha. Farah Sheikh, jo support team ko lead karti thi, ne uski ek hafte ki baatcheet export karke bheji thi, poori chaar sau conversations, aur Anaya yeh jaanna chahti thi ki itne saare customers late fees ke baare mein wahi teen sawaal baar-baar kyun poochte hain.

Woh conversation number ikyaasi par thi jab late fees mein uski dilchaspi khatam ho gayi.

```
Customer: loan ka status batao, mera aadhaar 4321 5678 9012 hai
Bot:      Aapke loan ka status dekhne ke liye apna registered mobile number bataiye.
Customer: 9876543210
Bot:      Dhanyavaad. Aapka application review mein hai.
```

Number barah ankon ka tha aur chaar-chaar ke saaf groups mein likha tha, bilkul waise jaise card par chhapa hota hai. Aadhaar number woh pehchaan ka number hai jo sarkar yahan ke nivasiyon ko deti hai; lagbhag har baalig Indian jaanta hai ki uska apna kahan rakha hai. Is customer ne yeh number bina maange type kar diya tha. Chatbot ne sirf mobile number poochha tha. Customer ne madad karne ke josh mein apni sabse zaroori cheez saamne rakh di.

Anaya ne wahi kiya jo koi bhi karta. Woh scroll karti gayi, aur use ek aur mila. Phir ek PAN, tax department ka diya hua das akshar ka code, jo ek delayed refund ke baare mein likhe gaye vaakya ke beech mein bade akshar mein type kiya gaya tha. Phir ek poora ghar ka pata. Phir ek bank passbook ki photo, jo chat window ne bina kisi shikayat ke le li thi.

Usne samosa neeche rakh diya.

## Type hone ke baad number ka kya hota hai

Yeh sochna aasaan hota ki woh numbers chat window mein hi pade hain, jahan customer unhe chhod gaya, aur kahin nahi. Aisa hota nahi hai, aur Anaya ko yeh samajhne mein lagbhag das minute lage ki sach se yeh kitna door hai.

Chat box mein type kiya gaya message ek jagah nahi rehta. Woh support system mein save hota hai, taaki agents purani history padh sakein. Woh hafte ke export mein copy ho jaata hai, aur isi raaste woh Anaya ke inbox tak pahuncha tha. Woh us software ko bheja jaata hai jo chatbot ke jawaab likhta hai, aur woh software ek bahari company ka hai. Woh analytics dashboard mein gina jaata hai, aur un logs mein likha jaata hai jo engineers tab padhte hain jab kuch toot jaaye. In sab jagahon ke apne log hain, apne backups hain, aur apne niyam hain ki kaun dekh sakta hai. Ek baar type kiya gaya number paanch gharon mein jaa kar rehne lagta hai.

Privacy ki policies likhne waale log is tarah ki detail ko ek naam dete hain. Woh ise *personally identifiable information* kehte hain, ya chhote mein PII. Shabd thoda bhaari hai, par kaam ka hai, kyunki woh ek test batata hai: kya yeh detail, akeli ya doosri details ke saath milkar, ek asli insaan ki taraf ishaara kar sakti hai? Naam test mein paas ho jaata hai. Mobile number, pata, Aadhaar number, bank account bhi. Kuch details sirf saath mein paas hoti hain. Ek aurat ki umar akeli kuch nahi batati, uska gaon bhi kam batata hai, lekin uski umar, uska gaon, aur yeh baat ki woh wahan ki akeli dentist hai, milkar bilkul ek hi insaan ko bayaan kar dete hain. Anaya ko tab tak nahi pata tha ki yeh aakhri baat aage chalkar kitni zaroori hone waali hai. Usne bas use notice kiya, jaise deewar ki daraar pehle nazar aa jaati hai, jabki yeh pata nahi hota ki wahi deewar poora bojh uthaye hue hai.

## Ginti

Is mod par ek lalach tha ki pehle kuch mehsoos kiya jaaye aur phir kuch kar diya jaaye, aur zyadatar kharab projects isi tarah shuru hote hain. Anaya ne in dono mein se kuch nahi kiya. Usne woh kiya jo ek samajhdaar insaan ek ehsaas ke saath karta hai: use ginti mein badal diya.

Usne spreadsheet mein ek column jodi aur uska title rakha *Kya yeh sirf ek insaan ki cheez hai?* Phir usne chaaron sau conversations dobara padhin, dheere-dheere, har line ke neeche ek ruler rakh kar. Do ghante lage. Samosa thanda ho gaya aur phir bhi kha liya gaya.

Chaar sau mein se saintees conversations mein pehchaan ka number, tax number, bank account, ya poora ghar ka pata tha. Yeh nau percent tha, yaani gyarah mein se ek conversation.

Us figure ke saath usne jo likha, uske baare mein woh bahut saavdhaan thi. Yeh ek hafte ki ginti thi, ek chatbot ki, ek aise insaan dwara ki gayi jo ek khaas cheez dhoondhte hue padh raha tha. Yeh koi finding nahi thi. Yeh aur gehraai se dekhne ki ek wajah thi, aur usne bilkul yahi baat us ke saath wale cell mein type kar di.

## Kya yahi problem hai?

Ek mahine se Anaya iss sawaal ke chakkar kaat rahi thi ki baaki saal woh kis cheez par kaam karegi. Company chahti thi ki April tak har product manager ek bada daav lagaye. Uske paas pehle se do ideas the. Ek tha woh tool jo hafte ke support tickets padhkar mukhya themes bata de, taaki Farah ko har Monday subah haath se yeh kaam na karna pade. Doosra tha ek feature jo salary slips ki photos padhkar loan officers ki jaanch tez kar de.

Ab uske paas teesra idea tha, aur teesra wahi tha jise usne chuna nahi tha. Woh khud us tak pahunch gaya tha. Sirf isi baat se woh us par shak karne lagi.

Isliye usne woh kiya jo use kabhi sikhaya gaya tha aur jo usne kabhi theek se kiya nahi tha: usne teeno ko ek hi jaise chaar sawaalon par naapa. Kya koi aisa user hai jis tak woh isi hafte pahunch sakti hai? Kya koi aisa kaam hai jise woh kisi ko karte hue dekh sakti hai? Kya koi aisa dard hai jise woh gin sakti hai? Aur kya koi sach-much ki wajah hai ki text padhne wala software madad kare, aam software ke bajaye?

| | Hafte ke ticket themes | Salary-slip reader | Chats mein personal details |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Aisa user jis tak isi hafte pahunch sake | Farah | Do loan officers | Farah, Imran, Lakshmi |
| Aisa kaam jo dekh sake | Har Monday | Ho sakta hai, arrange karna dheema | Transcripts uski screen par hain |
| Aisa dard jo gin sake | Hafte mein teen ghante | Har loan par kuch minute | 400 mein 37 |
| AI ki asli wajah | Shayad; tickets par pehle se tags hote hain | Haan, par kaagaz ki photos mushkil hoti hain | Kuch had tak |

Chauthi row mein use imaandaar hona tha. Chaar-chaar ke saaf groups wale barah ankon ke number ke liye ek seedha rule kaafi tha: barah ank dhoondho, chaar ke groups pehchaano, ho gaya. Kisi samajh ki zaroorat nahi. Mushkil kahin aur thi. Log hamesha number waise nahi likhte the jaise card par chhapa hota hai. Woh naam aur pate likhte the, jinka koi pattern nahi hota. Woh Hindi mein likhte the, English mein likhte the, aur un dono ki us khichdi mein likhte the jo raat ke gyarah baje phone ke keyboard par bhar jaati hai. Aadhi problem ek rule se hal ho sakti thi, aur usne yeh likh liya. Baaki aadhi ke liye shayad kuch zyada chalaak cheez chahiye thi, aur usne yeh bhi likh liya.

Product manager ke kaam ka ek hissa yeh bhi hai, usne socha, ki woh zor se keh sake ki is problem ko artificial intelligence ki zaroorat nahi hai. Yeh kehna fashion mein nahi hai, aur yahi pehli cheez thi jo usne likhi.

## Woh page jo yaad rakhta hai

Teesra column jeet gaya. Bahut zyada farak se nahi, aur isliye nahi ki woh sabse romanchak tha. Woh isliye jeeta kyunki sirf usi ke liye woh apna kaam dikha sakti thi.

Usne ek naya document khola, uska naam *Decisions* rakha, aur pehli entry likhi. Usne ek format pakda jo usne kahin padha tha: tareekh, faisla, uske peeche ka saboot, aur woh baat jisse woh apna man badal degi. Is tarah ke page ko *decision log* kehte hain, aur uski poori keemat uski aakhri line mein hai.

```
14 March
Faisla:    Pehle personal-details wali problem par kaam karo. Ticket themes doosre number par.
Saboot:    Ek hafte ki 400 conversations mein se 37 mein ek pehchaan ka number,
           tax number, bank account ya poora pata tha. Meri ginti; Farah
           check kar sakti hai.
Main apna man badal doongi agar:  Lakshmi kahe ki yeh ek jaana-pehchaana, maana hua risk hai, ya
           Imran kahe ki chatbot ko dobara banaye bina ise theek nahi kiya ja sakta.
```

Jisne bhi ek saal tak kisi project ko bhatakte dekha hai, woh jaanta hai ki aakhri line itni zaroori kyun hai. Jis faisle ka koi nikaas ka rasta nahi likha, woh dheere-dheere ek vishwaas ban jaata hai. Jis faisle mein likha ho ki kya hone par woh khatam hoga, use shaanti se dobara dekha ja sakta hai, bina kisi ki naak kate.

## Ek imaandaar score

Us raat usne ek aur kaam kiya, aur bade bejaaz dil se kiya, kyunki yeh aisa kaam tha jo woh saal mein ek baar karti thi aur use pasand nahi tha.

Usne khud ko score diya. Anaya ka kaam, jaisa woh samajhti thi, saat cheezon mein simat jaata tha. Logon se baat karke asli problems dhoondhna. Tay karna ki kaun si hal karni hai. Technology ko itna samajhna ki engineers se behes kar sake. Kisi faisle ko aisi cheez mein badalna jo doosre bana sakein. Jo hota hai use naapna. Yeh tay karna ki kya woh cheez apna kharcha khud utha sakti hai. Aur alag-alag maqsad wale logon ko ek saath chalana. Har ek ko usne shoonya se teen ke beech ek number diya. Shoonya matlab dikhane ke liye kuch nahi. Ek matlab usne koshish ki par saabit nahi kar sakti. Do matlab kaam kaam-chalau tha aur koi aur use check kar sakta tha. Teen matlab asli logon ya asli data par aazmaya ja chuka tha aur uski seemayein likhi hui thi.

Usne khud ko problems dhoondhne mein do diye, kyunki aaj raat uske paas ek ginti thi. Strategy mein ek diya, kyunki uske paas raayein thin par aisi strategy nahi thi jis par kisi saathi ne kabhi amal kiya ho. Aur technology samajhne mein bhi use ek diya, phir use kaata aur dobara likha, jo imaandaar log tab karte hain jab unhe apne number par garv nahi hota. Meeting mein woh keh sakti thi "chatbot AI se chalta hai". Lekin kisi jigyasu barah saal ke bachche ko woh yeh nahi samjha sakti thi ki is vaakya ka matlab kya hai.

Usne apne liye ek niyam banaya. Sirf ek line ka, aur use page ke sabse upar likha jahan woh use dekh sake.

*Kuch bhi tab tak gina nahi jaata jab tak koi aur use jaanch na sake.*

## Raat nau baje ek email

Imran Qureshi, lead engineer, ne chatbot ke peeche ki zyadatar plumbing banayi thi. Sahaj mein woh akela insaan tha jo raat nau baje bhi messages ka jawaab deta tha, zyadatar isliye ki woh waise bhi jaaga hota tha aur din ki roshni mein sawaal poochhe jaana use pasand nahi tha.

Anaya ne use likha. Usne ek vaakya jo "Mujhe lagta hai koi issue ho sakta hai" se shuru hota tha mita diya aur kuch chhota likha.

*Kal ek ghanta mil sakta hai? Chats mein mujhe kuch mila hai, aur main bahut chahti hoon ki main galat nikloon.*

Jawaab uske laptop band karne se pehle hi aa gaya.

*Export le aana. Chai main laaunga.*

## Saath le jaane layak baatein

Type kiya hua number wahin nahi rehta jahan type kiya gaya tha; woh har us system mein jaata hai jo us conversation ko chhoota hai. Aisi details jo ek asli insaan ki taraf ishaara kar sakti hain, unka ek naam hai, PII, aur jab kai details ek saath hoti hain tab woh sabse tez ishaara karti hain. Kisi problem ke baare mein ek ehsaas tab faisla banta hai jab use ginti mein badla jaaye, saboot ke saath likha jaaye, aur use ulatne ka ek rasta diya jaaye. Aur kuch bhi chalaak cheez uthaane se pehle, imaandaari se poochh lena achha hai ki problem ka kitna hissa ek seedha rule hal kar sakta hai.
