AI product kaise banta hai, yeh seekhne ke do tareeke hain. Pehla tareeka hai khud ek banana. Aap thodi code likhte hain. Machine ko ek sawaal bhejte hain. Dekhte hain ki kya toota. Phir naapte hain ki kitna toota. Course app isi tareeke ke liye bana hai. Yeh kitaab uski jagah nahi leti.

Doosra tareeka hai poora subject order se seekhna. Koi aapko ek-ek step samjhata hai. Naye shabd anjaane nahi lagte. Dheere-dheere woh aapke dimaag mein ek tasveer ban jaate hain. Ise ek shehar seekhne jaisa samjhiye. App aapko uski galiyon mein chalata hai. Kitaab aapko naqsha deti hai. Yeh kitaab isi doosre tareeke ke liye hai.

Yeh kitaab us insaan ke liye hai jisne technology projects deliver kiye hain. Ab aap ek AI product ke malik banna chahte hain. Aapko code likhna aana zaroori nahi. Statistics aana bhi zaroori nahi. Rhythm yeh hai. Har hafte, chhabbis hafte tak, aap ek chapter padhte hain. Usmein lagbhag das minute lagte hain. Phir aap title ke neeche likha app chapter karte hain.

Anaya ke aas-paas ek team hai. Aapke paas app hai. Jab Imran kuch chalata hai, app aapko uska button deta hai. Jab Lakshmi poochhti hai ki use kaise pata chalega, app aapse wahi sawaal poochhta hai. Yeh check questions hain. Ant tak aap bata paayenge ki aapne kya banaya. Aap bata paayenge ki aapko kaise pata ki woh kaam karta hai. Aap yeh apne numbers ke saath bata paayenge.

Kitaab wahi cheezein sikhati hai jo app sikhata hai. Order wahi hai, aur terms (is subject ke khaas shabd) bhi wahi. Ismein koi exercise nahi hai. Type karne ke liye koi code bhi nahi hai. Uski jagah ek hi kahani shuru se ant tak chalti hai. Koi idea tab zyada yaad rehta hai jab woh kisi asli decision se juda ho. Yeh waisa hai jaise koi sadak us din se yaad rehti hai jis din aap uspar kho gaye the.

## Case

Sahaj Pune ki ek company hai. Uske app mein log bijli ka bill bharte hain. Haal hi mein woh chhote loan ke liye apply bhi kar sakte hain. App ke andar ek chatbot hai. Chatbot ek madadgaar hai jo type karke jawaab deta hai. Yeh customers ke sawaalon ka jawaab deta hai.

March ke ek din, product manager Anaya Deshmukh ko ek ajeeb baat dikhti hai. Customers chatbot mein apne Aadhaar number type karte rehte hain. Kisi ne unse maanga nahi tha. Yeh waisa hai jaise koi dukaan ke clerk ko apna bank PIN bata de, jabki clerk ne sirf time poochha tha.

Kitaab mein 47 chapters hain. Inmein Anaya aur uske colleagues ek chhota tool banate hain. Uska naam hai Bharat Privacy Guard. Yeh tool personal details pehchanta hai. Yeh English mein kaam karta hai, aur Hindi mein bhi. Yeh dono ke us mix mein bhi kaam karta hai jo zyadatar log type karte hain. Phir woh in details ko zaroorat se aage jaane se rokta hai.

Tool sirf kahani sunane ka ek zariya hai. Asli subject woh hai jo Anaya ko raaste mein karna padta hai. Use tay karna hai ki kya banana theek hai. Use samajhna hai ki ek AI system kin cheezon se bana hota hai. Use check karna hai ki woh kaam karta hai ya nahi.

Use jodna hai ki isme kitna kharch aayega. Use seekhna hai ki woh kaise fail ho sakta hai. Use tay karna hai ki tool ko kya karne ki chhoot hai. Aakhir mein use tool ko asli users ke saamne rakhna hai. Har chapter in mein se ek problem uthata hai. Woh tab aati hai jab project ko uski zaroorat padti hai.

## Kitaab kaise baanti gayi hai

Kitaab ko ek train jaisa samjhiye, jisme saintaalis dibbe hain. Dibbe ek hi line mein numbered hain. Unhe saat parts mein baanta gaya hai. Neeche ki table batati hai ki har part kis baare mein hai.

Har chapter ka layout ek jaisa hai, taaki aapko hamesha pata rahe ki aap kahan hain. Yeh us train jaisa hai jiske saare dibbe ek jaise dikhte hain. Sabse upar ek chhoti list batati hai ki chapter mein kya hai. Shuru ke paragraphs kahani ka ek hissa sunate hain. Phir numbered sections aate hain. Woh kahani ke peeche ke ideas samjhate hain. Definitions, examples aur tables alag dabbon mein rakhe gaye hain. Ant mein ek summary aati hai. Uske baad key terms ki list aati hai.

## Kaise padhein

Chapters ko order se padhiye. Seedhi ka khayal kijiye. Chauthi seedhi ke bina paanchvi tak nahi pahunch sakte. Har chapter wahin se shuru hota hai jahan pichhla ruka tha. Har chapter sirf wahi shabd use karta hai jo pehle ke chapters mein samjhaaye ja chuke hain. Kitaab isi baat ke liye check ki gayi hai. Agar koi shabd apne chapter se pehle aa jaaye, toh use galti maana jaata hai.

Naya term pehli baar highlight hota hai. Yeh us chapter mein hota hai jo use samjhata hai. Uske upar pointer rakhiye, toh matlab khul jaata hai. Har chapter ke ant ki key terms list wahi matlab dobara deti hai. Kitaab ke peeche glossary mein har term A se Z order mein hai. Glossary kitaab ke apne shabdon ki chhoti dictionary hai. Har term ke saath us chapter ka number hai jahan woh pehli baar aaya.

Har chapter ke title ke neeche likha hai ki app ke kaun se chapters usi topic ko cover karte hain. Practice karni ho, toh wahin jaiye.

Shuru karne ke liye aapko AI, programming ya business ke baare mein kuch bhi jaanna zaroori nahi.

## Case ke baare mein ek baat

Sahaj, Anaya, uske colleagues aur customers sab banaye hue hain. Unke numbers bhi banaye hue hain. Tools aur tareeke asli hain. Kahani mein jo galtiyan dikhti hain, woh bhi asli hain. Yeh "sach par aadharit" film jaisa hai. Chehre actors ke hain. Museebatein sachchi hain.

Is kitaab mein kuch bhi legal advice nahi hai. Kahani ka tool company ko kam private information collect karne mein madad karta hai. Woh use aage bhi kam bhejne mein madad karta hai. Kitaab kahin nahi kehti ki yeh tool kisi se kanoon ka paalan karwa deta hai. Aisa koi tool nahi kar sakta. Ise darwaze ke taale jaisa samjhiye. Woh madad karta hai, par aapko imaandaar nahi banata.
