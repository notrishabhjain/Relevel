September mein, us raat ke dedh saal baad jab usne use shuru kiya tha, Anaya Deshmukh ne decision log ke sabse upar scroll kiya aur pehli entry padhi. *14 March. Faisla: pehle personal-details wali problem par kaam karo. Ticket themes doosre number par. Saboot: ek hafte ki 400 conversations mein se 37 mein pehchaan ka number, tax number, bank account ya poora pata tha. Meri ginti; Farah check kar sakti hai.*

Usne aakhri line do baar padhi. *Main apna man badal doongi agar.* Us saal ne use jo kuch sikhaya, usme woh line sabse sasti thi aur usne sabse zyada kaam kiya tha.

Uske peeche, whiteboard par, Imran ne teen rangon mein ek drawing chhodi thi ki ab customer ke keyboard aur jawaab padhne wale ke beech kya-kya baitha tha: guard ke har kaam ke liye ek aakaar, har us jagah ke liye ek teer jahan ek faisla hota tha, aur har us jagah ke bagal mein ek taara jahan galat jawaab sabse zyada mehnga padta. Woh khoobsurat nahi thi. Woh bilkul wahi thi jo usne banaya tha.

Woh ab woh keh sakti thi jo March mein nahi keh paayi thi. Use pata tha ki machine kya hai, saral shabdon mein, aur woh kya nahi kar sakti. Use pata tha ki yeh kaise poochhna hai ki woh kaam karti hai ya nahi, ek number ke saath. Use pata tha ki iska kharcha kitna hai, aur kaun dega. Use kai tareeke pata the jinse woh fail ho sakti hai, aur unme se kuch likhe hue the, aur use pata tha ki use akele kya karne ki ijaazat nahi hai. Use pata tha ki jin logon se woh kabhi nahi mili thi unhone use istemaal kiya tha, aur unme se kuch prabhaavit nahi hue the, jo kaam ka hissa tha.

Use bahut kuch pata nahi bhi tha. Guard ko English, Hindi aur dono ki khichdi mein naapa gaya tha, un messages par jo team ne khud likhe the. Use Tamil ya Bengali ya Telugu mein, ya do shehron ke bahar ke customers ki likhai mein, ya Imran se zyada sabr wale hamlavar ke khilaaf, naapa nahi gaya tha. Use kabhi kisi ne compliant nahi kaha tha jo is shabd ko samajhta ho, aur kisi ke liye bhi uski taraf se aisa kehna galti hogi. Woh ek saavdhaan tool tha imaandaar numbers ke saath. Itna hi woh hone ka daava karta tha, aur aage badhne ke liye yeh kaafi tha.

## Is kitaab ne kya karne ki koshish ki

Is kitaab ne ek chhote project ko ek poori shiksha ke zariye follow kiya, kyunki ek project har idea ko utarne ki jagah deta hai. Isne ek akeli company, ek akele sheher, kalpanik logon aur kalpanik figures ki kahani sunayi. Keemat gadhi hui hain. Messages gadhe hue hain. Naap woh hain jo ek saavdhaan team ko mil sakta hai, woh nahi jo kisi asli team ko mila hai. Jahan ek number maayne rakhta tha, woh yeh dikhane ke liye tha ki number ke baare mein tark kaise kiya jaata hai, aur ise udhaar lene se pehle aapko ise apne cases par jaanchna chahiye.

Isne aapko shabd dene ki bhi koshish ki. Is tarah ke kshetra mein lagbhag har cheez ka ek naam hota hai, aur ise seekhne ki mushkil ka ek bada hissa yeh hai ki naam un cheezon se tez aate hain jinke liye woh khade hain. Har chapter kuch naye shabdon se shuru hua aur unhe use kiya, us chapter mein aur baad ke sabhi mein, usi arth mein. Agar koi shabd aapse door ho gaya hai, toh kitaab ke peeche ka glossary unme se har ek ko rakhta hai, ek chhote saral matlab ke saath aur us chapter ke note ke saath jahan woh pehli baar aaya.

## Yahan se kahan jaayein

Ek kitaab aapko tasveer de sakti hai. Woh aapko aadat nahi de sakti, aur aadat hi zyadatar hunar hai. Jo kuch aapne yahan padha woh tab aapka ban jaata hai jab aap use karte hain: jab aap ek problem ka saaf vivaran likhte hain aur paate hain ki woh jud nahi paata, jab aap chhoti answer key banate hain aur dekhte hain ki aap kitni baar galat the, jab aap jaan-boojh kar apna system todte hain aur dekhte hain ki woh kya karta hai.

Course app isi ke liye bana hai. Kitaab ke har chapter ka app mein ek saathi hai, chapter page ke upar se joda hua, wahi ideas usi kram mein, aur karne ke kaam ke saath: woh tasks aur sawaal jinka aap jawaab dete aur jaanchte hain. Yahan ek chapter padhiye aur phir wahan kariye, ya ulta. Dono ko saath-saath chalne ke liye likha gaya tha.

Agar aapko koi aisi jagah mile jahan kahani aur course asahmat hon, ya jahan koi shabd samjhaye jaane se pehle istemaal hua ho, toh kripya bataiye. Kitaab ko theek isi ke liye, line-dar-line, jaancha gaya tha, aur ek checker ek pathak ka vikalp nahi hai.

Aur agar aap, Anaya ki tarah, us kamre ke woh insaan hain jise kuch samajhne ko kaha gaya hai jise kisi ko pura samajh nahi aata, toh ek notebook lijiye, aur use ek line se shuru kijiye ki aapne kya notice kiya. Baaki sab usi se aata hai.
