September mein, us raat ke dedh saal baad jab usne yeh sab shuru kiya tha, Anaya Deshmukh ne decision log ke sabse upar scroll kiya aur pehli entry padhi. Woh 14 March ki thi. Faisla yeh tha ki pehle personal-details ki samasya par kaam karna hai aur ticket themes par doosre number par. Saboot yeh tha ki ek hafte ki 400 conversations mein se 37 mein pehchaan ka number, tax number, bank account ya poora pata tha, uski apni ginti ke anusaar, jise Farah jaanch sakti thi. Usne aakhri line do baar padhi: "Main apna man badal doongi agar". Us saal ne use jo kuch sikhaya, usme woh line sabse sasti thi aur usne sabse zyada kaam kiya.

## Case kahan khatam hota hai

September tak guard do support teams ki har chat par aur ek lending partner ke trial mein chal raha tha. Anaya ke peeche whiteboard par Imran ne teen rangon mein ek drawing chhodi thi ki ab customer ke keyboard aur jawaab padhne waale insaan ke beech kya-kya hai. Guard ke har kaam ke liye usme ek aakaar tha, har us jagah ke liye ek teer jahan faisla hota tha, aur har us jagah ke bagal mein ek taara jahan galat jawaab sabse mehnga padta. Woh khoobsurat nahi thi. Woh us cheez ka sahi hisaab thi jo banayi gayi thi.

## Project ko abhi bhi kya nahi pata

Jo paathyapustak nishchitta par khatam hoti hai, usne aksar kuch chhod diya hota hai. Guard ko English mein, Hindi mein aur dono ki milavat mein naapa gaya tha, un messages par jo team ne khud likhe the. Use Tamil, Bengali ya Telugu mein, do shehron ke bahar ke customers ki likhai mein, ya Imran se zyada sabr waale hamlavar ke khilaaf nahi naapa gaya tha. Jisne is shabd ko samjha, aise kisi ne use compliant nahi kaha tha, aur kisi ke liye bhi uski taraf se aisa kehna galti hogi. Woh imaandaar numbers waala ek saavdhaan tool tha, aur woh itna hi hone ka daava karta tha.

Yahi saavdhaani kitab par bhi lagti hai. Sahaj, uske log, uske daam aur uske figures kalpanik hain. Tools aur techniques asli hain, aur failures us tarah ke hain jo asli systems mein hote hain. Jahan ek number maayne rakhta hai, woh yeh dikhane ke liye hai ki number ke baare mein tark kaise kiya jaata hai, aur pathak ko use udhaar lene se pehle apne cases par jaanchna chahiye.

## Shabd

Is kshetra mein lagbhag har cheez ka ek naam hota hai, aur ise seekhne ki mushkil ka ek bada hissa yeh hai ki naam un cheezon se tez aate hain jinke liye woh khade hain. Har chapter ne kuch shabd jode aur unhe, usi chapter mein aur baad ke sabhi chapters mein, usi arth mein istemaal kiya. Kitab ke peechhe ka glossary har shabd ko ek chhote matlab ke saath aur us chapter ke saath deta hai jahan woh pehli baar aaya.

## Kitab ko course app ke saath istemaal karna

Ek kitab tasveer de sakti hai, aur aadat nahi de sakti, jo hunar ka bada hissa hai. Yahan ka maal pathak ka apna abhyaas se hota hai: ek samasya ka saaf vivaran likhna aur dekhna ki woh jud nahi rahi, ek chhoti answer key banana aur dekhna ki woh kitni baar galat thi, ek system ko jaan-boojhkar todna aur dekhna ki woh kya karta hai.

Course app isi abhyaas ke liye bana hai. Kitab ke har chapter ka app mein ek saathi hai, jo chapter ke neeche linked hai, wahi vichaar usi kram mein, aur jawaab dene aur jaanchne ke liye kaam aur sawaal ke saath. Pathak yahan ek chapter padh sakta hai aur phir wahan kaam kar sakta hai, ya doosre kram mein. Dono saath istemaal karne ke liye likhe gaye the.

Jahan kitab aur course ek doosre se alag bolein, ya jahan koi shabd samjhaye jaane se pehle istemaal ho gaya ho, wahan galti ki soochna di jani chahiye. Kitab ko theek isi ke liye line-by-line jaancha gaya tha, par ek checker kisi pathak ka vikalp nahi hota. Aur jo koi bhi, Anaya ki tarah, us kamre mein woh insaan ho jisse kaha gaya ho ki kuch aisa samjho jo kisi ko theek se samajh nahi aata, uske liye salaah yeh hai ki ek notebook shuru karein aur usme jo notice kiya uski ek line likhein. Baaki sab uske baad aata hai.
